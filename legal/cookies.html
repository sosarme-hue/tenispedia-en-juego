Cookie policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Do we use cookies?

Ludicopedia uses what it needs to work and, only if you accept, Google Analytics statistics cookies. It does not use advertising or social media cookies.

Your choice: accept, reject or customise

The first time you visit, we show a notice so you can choose. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and the site and games work the same either way. There are three types:

  • Necessary (always on): those that make the site work and remember your preferences and your cookie choice.
  • Statistics (only if you accept them): Google Analytics, from Google Ireland Ltd. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_GE3T2RZVV2, which last 2 years and are used to count visits and see which pages and games are viewed and where visitors come from, in aggregate. Google may process this data outside the EU under the safeguards of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Advertising: not used.

Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage, key aviso-cookies) for 12 months; after that we will ask again. You can change it whenever you like with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the page. If you reject statistics or withdraw your consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and we delete its cookies.

Local storage

To remember your preferences, the site stores some data in your browser’s local storage (localStorage): light or dark theme, whether you have seen each game’s instructions, and your high scores or games. This is technical data, needed for what you yourself ask for; it cannot identify you and is not sent to any server. It is therefore exempt from consent under article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI.

Technical hosting cookies

Our hosting provider and its content delivery network may set a strictly technical cookie, for example to protect the site from attacks or spread the load. These cannot be used to track you either.

How to delete them

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser’s privacy settings. The site will keep working, but will forget your preferences.
