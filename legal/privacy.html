Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

Ludicopedia has no sign-up, no forms and no advertising tools. It only uses Google Analytics if you accept it in the cookie notice. We don’t need to know who you are for you to play.

Data controller

Alejandro Rosàs Navarro, NIF 35056148P, Barcelona (Spain). Contact: partenopides@gmail.com.

What data is processed

  • Technical connection data. Like any website, the server receives your IP address and basic browser data in order to send you the pages. Our hosting provider (Hostinger) keeps them in technical logs for a limited time, for security. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the site working and being secure.
  • Data stored in your own browser. Some preferences (for example, light or dark theme, whether you have already seen a game’s instructions, or your high scores) are stored in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device and you can delete them whenever you like in your browser settings.
  • Online games. If you play an online game with someone else, the moves, the room code, the nickname you choose and a random identifier for your browser are sent to Firebase Realtime Database, a Google service (Google Ireland Ltd.) with servers in the European Union (Belgium), so that they reach your opponent. The Firebase library is downloaded from Google’s servers only when you press “Play online”. We don’t ask for your real name; please don’t use personal data as a nickname.
  • Visit statistics (only if you accept them). If you accept statistics in the cookie notice, Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) receives your IP address and technical browser data to count visits in aggregate. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page. Google may process this data outside the EU under the safeguards of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details in the cookie policy.
  • If you write to us. If you send us an email, we will use your address and message only to reply, and will delete them when they are no longer needed.

Fonts and photos

The fonts and the encyclopedia photos are served from our own server, so visiting the site sends no data to Google Fonts or Wikimedia (except, as explained above, if you play an online game). Only if you click a photo’s link do you go to Wikimedia Commons, which has its own privacy policy.

Sharing data

We don’t sell or pass data to third parties. Only the technical providers needed for the site to work are involved (Hostinger for hosting; Google Firebase only in online games; and Google Analytics only if you accept statistics).

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to partenopides@gmail.com. If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Children

The site is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
