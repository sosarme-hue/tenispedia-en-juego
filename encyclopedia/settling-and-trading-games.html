Settling and trading games

Modern German-style board games, with The Settlers of Catan as the great pioneer.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

In 1995 the German dental technician Klaus Teuber published Die Siedler von Catan (The Settlers of Catan), a game in which players settle an island of hexagonal tiles, gather resources and trade with each other.

Its international success paved the way for so-called “eurogames”: board games with clear rules, one-hour games, little luck, lots of interaction and no one knocked out before the end.

Since then board games have been enjoying a golden age, with thousands of new titles every year and fairs such as Essen in Germany, the largest in the world.

🏛️ Historical context

In 1980s and 1990s Germany a new way of thinking about board games was born: one-hour games, no player elimination, lots of interaction and little luck. They were called eurogames, and the Spiel des Jahres award, created in 1979, helped make them known.

The game of settling an island arrived at just the right time and crossed borders: it was the first modern German game to be a real success in the United States and the rest of the world, and it paved the way for today’s enthusiasm for board games.

📖 How to play

  • The board is made of hexagons of different terrains.
  • Each turn two dice are rolled and the terrains with that number produce resources.
  • Resources are used to build roads, settlements and cities.
  • The first to reach 10 victory points wins.

🎭 Anecdotes

Klaus Teuber was a dental technician who designed games at night. He was inspired by the Vikings who settled Iceland, and tested the game for years with his family before publishing it.

The game was so successful that its original name, The Settlers of Catan, was shortened in 2015 to simply Catan, and today there are expansions for more players, with ships, cities and knights, plus historical and science-fiction versions.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • Klaus Teuber was a dental technician who designed games in his spare time.
  • The board is made of 19 hexagons laid out differently in each game.
  • The robber is activated by rolling a 7, the most likely total with two dice.
  • There is a world championship every two years.
