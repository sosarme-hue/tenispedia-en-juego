Pac-Man and the golden age of arcades

The maze-and-ghosts video game that took arcades all over the world.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

In 1980 the Japanese designer Tōru Iwatani, of the company Namco, created Pac-Man (Puck-Man in Japan). As he told it, he was inspired by a pizza with a slice missing. He wanted a game everyone would like, not just people who enjoyed shooting games.

It was the great symbol of the golden age of amusement arcades, from the late seventies to the mid-eighties, together with Space Invaders (1978) and Donkey Kong (1981).

The four ghosts behave differently: one chases you, another tries to get ahead of you, another moves unpredictably and another switches between chasing and fleeing.

🏛️ Historical context

Pac-Man was born in the Japan of 1980, when arcade video games were dominated by space shooters. Tōru Iwatani wanted to create something different, without violence, that would also attract women and couples to the arcades.

In the United States it became a social phenomenon: there was a cartoon series, a song that reached number nine in the charts, toys, cereals and T-shirts. It was the first video game character to become a star.

📖 How to play

  • You move round a maze eating dots.
  • Four ghosts chase the player.
  • The big power pills let you eat the ghosts for a while.
  • You clear a level by eating all the dots.

🎭 Anecdotes

Puck-Man was changed to Pac-Man in the United States because the makers feared vandals would change the P to another letter on the machines.

In 2010 Google devoted its playable logo to Pac-Man’s 30th anniversary. It was estimated that people spent millions of hours playing on Google’s page that day.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • In 1999 the American Billy Mitchell played the first perfect game of Pac-Man: 3,333,360 points.
  • The name comes from the Japanese onomatopoeia paku-paku, which imitates the sound of a mouth opening and closing while eating.
  • The ghosts are called Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde in English.
  • Level 256 has a bug that fills half the screen with symbols and cannot be completed.
