Role-playing games

Games of telling stories as a group, in which each player plays a character.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

In 1974 Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson published Dungeons & Dragons, which grew out of miniature war games. Each player controlled a single character, and a “game master” narrated the adventure and applied the rules with many-sided dice.

Role-playing games reached Spain in the 1980s and boomed in the 1990s, with Spanish titles such as Aquelarre (1990), set in the medieval Iberian Peninsula.

Role-playing games have had an enormous influence on video games, fantasy literature and series such as Stranger Things.

🏛️ Historical context

Role-playing games were born from the miniature wargames of the 1960s and the boom in fantasy literature, especially Tolkien’s The Lord of the Rings, which became very popular among American university students.

In the 1980s there was a “moral panic” in the United States and parts of Europe: some groups accused role-playing games of encouraging violence or the occult. Later studies found no link at all, and today they are even used in education and therapy.

📖 How to play

  • One player is the narrator or game master.
  • The others create a character with attributes and skills.
  • Uncertain actions are settled by rolling dice.
  • There is no winner: the aim is to live a good story together.

🎭 Anecdotes

Twenty-sided dice, typical of role-playing games, already existed in Roman Egypt: stone icosahedrons with Greek letters have been found. But it was role-playing games that made them a popular object.

Dave Arneson began experimenting with the idea in his Blackmoor campaign, in which each player controlled a single character instead of an army, something revolutionary at the time.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • The famous 20-sided die (d20) is an icosahedron, one of the shapes described by Plato.
  • The game master narrates the story and plays all the non-player characters.
  • A campaign can last years, with the same characters.
  • In Spain they became popular in the 1990s through clubs and gaming conventions.
