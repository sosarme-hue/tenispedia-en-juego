Poker

The great betting card game, won with good hands or with good bluffs.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

Poker was born in New Orleans and on the Mississippi steamboats at the beginning of the 19th century. In 1829 the English actor Joseph Cowell described a betting game with 20 cards and four players. Its ancestors are European bluffing games such as the French poque, the German pochen and the Spanish primero.

During the 19th century the 52-card deck was adopted and the flush, the straight and the draw appeared. The Texas Hold’em variant, with two private cards and five shared ones, was born in Texas at the beginning of the 20th century and became popular in Las Vegas in the 1960s.

The World Series of Poker has been held since 1970, and in the 2000s the game boomed thanks to television and the internet.

🏛️ Historical context

Poker arose in New Orleans and on the Mississippi steamboats around 1820–1830, a frontier society that mixed merchants, professional gamblers and adventurers. It had European betting and bluffing forerunners, such as the French poque and the Spanish primero.

The American Civil War spread it across the whole country, because soldiers played in the camps. In the 20th century cinema made it a myth of the Wild West, and in the 21st century television and the internet turned it into a worldwide phenomenon.

📖 How to play

  • The best 5-card hand wins.
  • From highest to lowest: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair.
  • Players bet over several rounds.
  • If everyone else folds, you win without showing your cards.

🎭 Anecdotes

The “dead man’s hand” is two black aces and two black eights. According to legend, it is what the gunfighter Wild Bill Hickok was holding when he was shot in the back during a game in Deadwood in 1876.

In 2003 Chris Moneymaker, who had qualified for 39 dollars in an online tournament, won the world championship and 2.5 million dollars. His surname seemed made up for the occasion.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • The Spanish game of primero, mentioned by Cervantes, is one of poker’s ancestors.
  • There are 2,598,960 possible five-card hands.
  • The royal flush is the highest and rarest hand.
  • Bluffing is an essential part of the game.
