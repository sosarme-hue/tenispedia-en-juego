Yo-yo

A disc that goes up and down a string: a toy from ancient Greece that became a worldwide craze in the 20th century.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

The yo-yo is one of the oldest known toys. In ancient Greece yo-yos were made of wood, metal and painted terracotta; there is a Greek vase from the 5th century BC showing a boy playing with one.

In the Philippines the yo-yo was a traditional toy, and the word yo-yo probably comes from Ilocano, a Philippine language. In 18th-century Europe it became fashionable among aristocrats, who called it bandalore or incroyable.

In 1928 the Filipino Pedro Flores opened a yo-yo factory in California and organised contests. The businessman Donald Duncan bought his company and launched a big advertising campaign that made the yo-yo a mass-market toy all over the world.

🏛️ Historical context

In Revolutionary France the yo-yo, called émigrette, was very popular among nobles fleeing the country: it was said they used it to calm their nerves.

The yo-yo went through great crazes in the 20th century: in the 1930s, the 1960s, the 1980s and the 1990s. Each craze filled school playgrounds with children doing tricks.

📖 How to play

  • You wind the string round the axle and tie the end to a finger.
  • You throw the yo-yo downwards and bring it back with a gentle tug.
  • With practice you can do tricks such as “walk the dog” or “around the world”.
  • There are championships judged on the difficulty and creativity of the tricks.

🎭 Anecdotes

It has often been said that the yo-yo was a hunting weapon in the Philippines, but there is no evidence at all: it is a widespread myth.

In 1965 a US court ruled that “yo-yo” was a generic word and not a trademark, and the Duncan company lost exclusive use of the name.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • In 1985 a yo-yo travelled into space on the shuttle Discovery to study how it behaves without gravity.
  • Modern yo-yos have ball bearings so they spin for longer.
  • There have been world yo-yo championships since the 1990s.
