Slot machines

Machines with reels of symbols that spin when you pull a lever or press a button, paying out if the symbols line up.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

The first modern slot machine is credited to Charles Fey, a San Francisco mechanic who around 1895–1899 built the Liberty Bell: three reels with horseshoes, diamonds, spades, hearts and a Liberty Bell. Three bells paid the top prize.

At the beginning of the 20th century, to get round the laws against gambling, some machines gave flavoured chewing gum as prizes. That is where the famous fruit symbols (cherry, lemon, orange, plum) come from, and the word BAR comes from the logo of a chewing gum brand.

Over the years they went from mechanical to electronic and then computerised. In Spain they became very popular in bars from the 1980s.

🏛️ Historical context

At the end of the 19th century the United States had a craze for coin-operated machines: they sold chewing gum, told fortunes, showed pictures. The first poker machines, by the Sittman and Pitt company (1891), had five drums with cards, and the bar paid the prize in drinks.

The machines were banned in California in 1909 and in other states, and moved to Nevada, where gambling was legalised in 1931. In Spain, the legalisation of gambling in 1977 opened the door to machines in bars and arcades.

📖 How to play

  • You bet an amount per spin.
  • The reels spin and stop at random.
  • If certain combinations appear on a payline, you are paid according to the pay table.
  • The machine is programmed to pay out less than it takes in: in the long run it always wins.

🎭 Anecdotes

Charles Fey’s Liberty Bell is now a museum piece much sought after by collectors. Fey did not patent his invention, and other manufacturers copied it without paying him.

The BAR symbols on many machines come from the Bell-Fruit Gum brand, whose logo was a bar.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • The side lever of the first machines earned them the nickname “one-armed bandit”.
  • Machines that share a jackpot that grows with each play are called progressives.
  • Modern machines use a random number generator.
  • In Spain bar machines are regulated by each autonomous region.
