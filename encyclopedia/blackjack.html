Blackjack (twenty-one)

A casino game in which you must get as close to 21 as possible without going over, playing against the dealer.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

The clearest ancestor of blackjack is the game of veintiuna, which Miguel de Cervantes describes in his exemplary novel Rinconete y Cortadillo, published in 1613. In France vingt-et-un was played in the 18th century.

In the United States, at the beginning of the 20th century, some casinos paid a special bonus if the hand was the ace of spades with a black jack, and that is where the name comes from.

In 1962 the mathematician Edward O. Thorp published Beat the Dealer, showing that counting cards could give you an edge over the casino. Since then casinos have used several decks shuffled together.

🏛️ Historical context

Twenty-one descends from European gambling card games of the 17th and 18th centuries, such as the French vingt-et-un and the Spanish veintiuna, already mentioned by Cervantes in Rinconete y Cortadillo. It reached America with French settlers.

In 20th-century American casinos it became the most played card game, partly because, unlike roulette, the player’s skill affects their chances of winning.

📖 How to play

  • Cards are worth their number; court cards 10 and the ace 1 or 11.
  • You ask for a card (hit) or stand.
  • If you go over 21 you lose.
  • The dealer draws up to 17, and whoever gets closest to 21 wins.

🎭 Anecdotes

The mathematician Edward Thorp proved with an IBM computer that counting cards could beat the casino. He tested his method in Nevada with an investor’s money and won. Casinos reacted by using more decks and banning card counters.

In the 1990s a group of MIT students won millions of dollars in casinos by counting cards as a team. Their story inspired the film 21 (2008).

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • Cervantes was already describing the game of twenty-one at the beginning of the 17th century.
  • The name blackjack comes from an old casino bonus: it paid more if the hand included the ace of spades and a black jack.
  • In most casinos the dealer must stand on 17.
  • With basic strategy the house edge drops below 1%.
