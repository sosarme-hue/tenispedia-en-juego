The handkerchief (el pañuelo)

Two teams, a handkerchief in the middle and a number: whoever grabs it has to get back to their side without being caught.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

El pañuelo is a traditional playground and summer camp game that mixes speed, reflexes and cunning. It is played all over Spain and has variants in many countries.

The players are divided into two teams with the same number of members, and each one is given a number. One person stands in the middle holding a handkerchief out in their hand and calls out a number.

In English a very similar game is known as Steal the Bacon, and in Italy as Ruba bandiera (“steal the flag”).

🏛️ Historical context

It is a classic of break time, summer camps and PE lessons, because all it needs is a handkerchief and an open space.

To make it harder, the person in the middle can call two numbers at once, or a sum whose answer is the number that has to run.

📖 How to play

  • The two teams line up facing each other, the same distance from the person holding the handkerchief.
  • The person in the middle calls a number, and the two players with that number run for the handkerchief.
  • Whoever grabs the handkerchief has to get back to their line before their opponent tags them.
  • If they reach their line, they win a point or knock out their opponent. If they are tagged, the point goes to the other team.
  • You cannot cross the middle line without having grabbed the handkerchief.

🎭 Anecdotes

PE teachers often use el pañuelo to revise times tables: they say “three times four” and number twelve has to run.

At summer camps, a game of el pañuelo can last a whole afternoon, with teams of twenty and shouting that can be heard from far away.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • Often the two players stand still next to the handkerchief, feinting, because whoever grabs it first becomes the prey.
  • Feinting to fool your opponent is part of the game.
  • If both players touch the handkerchief at the same time, the round is replayed.
  • You can play with any object instead of a handkerchief.
