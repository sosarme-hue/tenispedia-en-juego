Super Mario Bros.

Nintendo’s platform game that rescued the video game industry and created a worldwide character.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

Super Mario Bros. was created in 1985 by Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka for Nintendo’s Famicom console, called the NES in the West. The character Mario had appeared earlier in Donkey Kong (1981), when he was called Jumpman.

In 1983 the video game industry in the United States had suffered a great crash: too many bad games had been released and shops did not want to sell any more consoles. Super Mario Bros. and the NES restored the public’s confidence.

It was one of the first side-scrolling games with a large world to explore, with secrets, pipes and hidden blocks. More than 40 million copies were sold, and Mario became the most famous video game character in the world.

🏛️ Historical context

Nintendo was a company with almost a hundred years of history, born in 1889 to make hanafuda cards. In the 1980s it bet on home consoles, and the Famicom reached millions of Japanese homes.

The game was born just after the 1983 video game crash in the United States, and its success made Japan the world centre of the video game industry for two decades.

📖 How to play

  • Mario moves right through levels full of obstacles.
  • You jump on enemies to defeat them.
  • Mushrooms make him grow and flowers let him throw fireballs.
  • At the end of each world you have to defeat Bowser to rescue the princess.

🎭 Anecdotes

Mario was named after Mario Segale, the landlord of Nintendo’s warehouse in the United States, who had come to demand overdue rent. The staff thought he looked like the character.

The designers reused the same drawing for the clouds and the bushes in the game, changing only the colour, to save memory.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • Mario has a moustache and a cap because, with so few pixels, it was easier to draw a moustache than a mouth, and a cap than hair.
  • The game’s music, composed by Kōji Kondō, is one of the best-known melodies in the world.
  • The record for finishing the game as fast as possible is under 5 minutes.
  • Mario was a carpenter in his first games, and later a plumber.
