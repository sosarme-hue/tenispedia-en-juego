Donkey Kong

Nintendo’s arcade machine in which a carpenter jumps barrels to rescue a girl from a gorilla. It was Mario’s debut.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

Donkey Kong came out in arcades in 1981. It was designed by the young Shigeru Miyamoto, who would later create Super Mario Bros. and The Legend of Zelda. It was one of his first jobs at Nintendo.

Nintendo had many unsold machines in the United States of an earlier game, Radar Scope, which had flopped. It commissioned a new game to make use of those cabinets, and Miyamoto invented a story: a gorilla escapes and kidnaps a girl, and a carpenter has to climb girders dodging barrels to save her.

The hero was called Jumpman. Shortly afterwards his name was changed to Mario, and in 1985 he became the hero of Super Mario Bros. Donkey Kong was one of the first platform games, with jumping and several different screens.

🏛️ Historical context

In the early 1980s arcade machines were enjoying their golden age. In arcades and bars all over the world, players competed to put their name on the high-score table.

Donkey Kong was Nintendo’s first big hit in the United States, and it gave the company the money and fame it needed to later launch its first console there.

📖 How to play

  • You control Mario, who has to reach the top of the screen where Donkey Kong is.
  • You climb ladders and jump the barrels the gorilla throws.
  • The hammer lets you smash barrels for a few seconds.
  • There are four different screens: girders, conveyor belts, lifts and rivets.
  • If a barrel or a flame touches you, you lose a life.

🎭 Anecdotes

Universal Studios sued Nintendo, claiming that Donkey Kong copied King Kong. Nintendo won, and it is said that the character Kirby is named after the lawyer who defended it, John Kirby, as a thank-you.

Mario wore a cap and a moustache because with so few pixels it was hard to draw hair and a mouth, and the coloured dungarees made his arms easy to see as he moved.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • Miyamoto wanted the name to mean “stubborn gorilla”, and chose “donkey” because he associated it with stubbornness.
  • The girl to be rescued was called Lady and was later renamed Pauline.
  • Beating the Donkey Kong high score became a famous competition, with documentaries devoted to it.
  • It was one of the first video games to tell a story with scenes between levels.
