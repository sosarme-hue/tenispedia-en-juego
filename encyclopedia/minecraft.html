Minecraft

The building and exploring game with blocks that has become the best-selling video game in history.

📜 History🏛️ Historical context⏳ Timeline📖 How to play🎭 Anecdotes💡 Fun facts

📜 History

Minecraft was created by the Swedish programmer Markus Persson, known as Notch, who published the first version in May 2009. At first it was a very simple personal project: a world made of cubes in which you could break and place blocks.

The game gradually grew with the help of players’ feedback. Notch founded the company Mojang, and the full version was launched in November 2011. By then it already had millions of players.

In 2014 Microsoft bought Mojang for $2.5 billion. Minecraft has sold more than 300 million copies and is the best-selling video game in history. It is also used in many schools to teach maths, history or programming.

🏛️ Historical context

Minecraft arrived at a time when the internet allowed an independent programmer to publish a game without a big company behind them. Players’ videos on YouTube made it known all over the world.

For many children and young people it was a way of expressing their creativity: whole cities, historic monuments and even working computers have been recreated inside the game.

📖 How to play

  • You start in a huge randomly generated world, made of blocks of earth, stone, wood, water…
  • In survival mode you have to gather materials, make tools and build a shelter before night, when the monsters appear.
  • In creative mode you have unlimited blocks and can build whatever you like without danger.
  • Materials can be combined to craft objects, from torches to circuits.
  • You can play alone or with other people on servers.

🎭 Anecdotes

Some players have rebuilt whole places in Minecraft, block by block, such as fantasy cities or real monuments, and shared them with millions of people.

Reporters Without Borders built a library inside Minecraft with articles censored in some countries, taking advantage of the fact that the game is not blocked there.

💡 Fun facts: did you know…?

  • The creeper, the famous green monster that explodes, was born from a mistake: Notch wanted to make a pig, but swapped the height and length of the model and ended up with a tall, strange creature.
  • Each Minecraft block represents one cubic metre.
  • A Minecraft world is so big that exploring all of it would take an enormous amount of time.
  • The game can be modified with mods made by players.
