Stories of Greek heroes retold by Percy Jackson

Percy Jackson's Greek Heroes

Published by Rick Riordan on August 18, 2015, this book gathers stories about various heroes of Greek mythology. It is the sequel to Percy Jackson's Greek Gods and is narrated by the protagonist of the series.

Percy Jackson's Greek Heroes is a mythology book by Rick Riordan, released on August 18, 2015. It is the sequel to Percy Jackson's Greek Gods and belongs to the same franchise as the Camp Half-Blood Chronicles, a contemporary world in which teenage demigods face monsters and the gods of Greco-Roman mythology.

What sets this volume apart is its narrator. Rather than presenting the tales from a neutral voice, Riordan tells them through Percy Jackson, the series' protagonist, using the wry and conversational tone found throughout his novels. Percy acts as a guide through the stories of Greek heroes, which brings very old material closer to young readers without losing the thread of the narrative.

It is worth remembering that Greek heroic myths have no single author and no definitive version. They were passed down for centuries through epic poetry, tragedy, and painted pottery, and different ancient writers told the same figure or episode in different ways. Riordan takes that inheritance as his starting point and reshapes it for a modern audience, so the book works as a doorway into the tradition rather than as a reference text.

The success of the main series helps explain the place of this book. Percy Jackson & the Olympians has sold more than 69 million copies in over 35 countries, and it has introduced many readers to Greek mythology for the first time. Greek Heroes extends that approach into the territory of heroes, figures who in the tradition often face trials, confront monsters, and maintain complicated relationships with the gods.

It should be noted that this is an accessible retelling, aimed mainly at younger readers. Those who want to know the ancient versions will need to turn to the original sources, but as a first encounter with the world of Greek heroes, the book is very approachable.

Trivia

A companion volume. Greek Heroes is the direct sequel to Percy Jackson's Greek Gods, which came out a year earlier, on August 19, 2014.
A shared universe. The franchise shares its fictional universe with The Kane Chronicles, which centers on Egyptian mythology, and Magnus Chase and the Gods of Asgard, which centers on Norse mythology.
Publishing success. The main series, Percy Jackson & the Olympians, has sold more than 69 million copies in more than 35 countries.
