Percy Jackson & the Olympians (film series)

Percy Jackson & the Olympians is a two-film series based on Rick Riordan's novels, released in 2010 and 2013. Its hero, a teenager who learns he is the son of the sea god Poseidon, faces adventures alongside other demigods at Camp Half-Blood. The films reimagine Greek myth for a modern audience and sparked debate about their faithfulness to the books.

Percy Jackson & the Olympians is a film series based on Rick Riordan's novel sequence. It consists of two films, released in 2010 and 2013, whose hero discovers that he is the son of Poseidon, the Greek god of the sea. Like other figures of Greek tradition, Percy is a demigod, the child of a god and a mortal woman, and he trains at Camp Half-Blood alongside other young people in the same situation.

The first film, The Lightning Thief, opened on February 12, 2010, and was directed by Chris Columbus. Percy is accused of stealing Zeus's lightning bolt, and to clear his name and rescue his mother, who has been taken by Hades, he sets out on a quest that carries him across America and down into the underworld. Reviews were mixed, but the film earned almost 227 million dollars worldwide on a budget of 95 million.

The second, Sea of Monsters, reached cinemas on August 7, 2013, under the direction of Thor Freudenthal. Percy meets his half-brother Tyson, a Cyclops, and joins the search for the Golden Fleece, the legendary object from the myth of Jason and the Argonauts. The goal is to save a magical tree that protects the camp, and the journey leads the heroes to the Sea of Monsters, which the film links to the Bermuda Triangle.

The films took considerable liberties with the books. The second one in particular merged the plots of two separate novels, a choice critics noted. Riordan has said in interviews that he never watched the films so as not to shape his view of the characters, and in 2016 he asked teachers not to show them during class time. In 2018 he published an account of how little influence he had over the productions.

The series grossed nearly 430 million dollars worldwide. A third film was planned, but it was never made. After Disney acquired 21st Century Fox in 2019, the rights to the novels passed to Disney, which premiered a live-action television series on Disney+ in December 2023. This time Riordan took part in its development alongside his wife, Becky, a notable change from the film series.

Date: 2010-

Trivia

An author who never watched the films. Rick Riordan has said in several interviews that he has never seen the films, so they would not influence how he imagines his characters.
A third part that never arrived. A third film was planned and the second one laid groundwork for it, but it was never produced.
From Greek myth to the Bermuda Triangle. In the second film, the Sea of Monsters is presented as the Bermuda Triangle, a place that does not appear this way in Greek myth.
