The Sun and the Star

The Sun and the Star is a 2023 children's fantasy novel written by Rick Riordan and Mark Oshiro. It opens The Nico di Angelo Adventures and draws on Greek and Roman myth. The story follows the sons of Hades and Apollo on a quest to rescue an old friend who turns out to be a Titan known as Bob.

The Sun and the Star takes place about two months after the defeat of Nero and Python, the villains of The Tower of Nero, the book that closes The Trials of Apollo. It is the first volume of a series led by Nico di Angelo, son of Hades, and Will Solace, son of Apollo. The two are demigods and a couple, and the novel treats their relationship as a natural part of the story, a choice the authors took care over.

The starting point is a prophecy delivered by the Oracle of Delphi, Rachel Elizabeth Dare. The boys must descend into Tartarus, the deepest pit beneath the Greek underworld, to rescue Bob, who is really Iapetus, one of the Titans of myth. In the tradition, Iapetus is the father of Atlas. In the novel, his Titan past catches up with him, because Nyx, the primordial goddess of night, holds him captive and tries to force him back into the identity he has rejected.

The journey is full of encounters with figures from classical myth. Nico and Will pass through the Door of Orpheus, where they are attacked by Epiales, the demon of nightmares. They then deal with the troglodytes, visit the farm of Menoetes, and steal pomegranates from Persephone's garden. Persephone herself meets Will and advises him to accept his inner darkness. Later, the nymph Gorgyra lends them her canoe to cross the River Acheron, one of the rivers of the land of the dead.

In the Mansion of Night, Nyx tries to persuade Nico that he belongs in the darkness of Tartarus. She has shaped cacodemons out of his fears. Nico answers by facing his shadows and setting the creatures free. Will draws on the plague powers of Apollo. Afterwards, the group returns to Camp Half-Blood, and in a dream Hades admits to his son that he has changed the way he treats him.

The novel was published in the United States by Disney-Hyperion and in the United Kingdom by Puffin Books. It reached number one on the New York Times children's bestseller list and has been translated into sixteen languages. Its sequel, The Court of the Dead, was released in 2025.

Author: Rick Riordan

Trivia

Co-written. Rick Riordan wrote the book together with Mark Oshiro, an author of children's fiction centred on LGBT+ characters. Both received equal credit as authors.
A Titan named Bob. The character the heroes rescue, known as Bob, is really Iapetus, a Titan of Greek myth. Bob resists returning to who he once was.
Meant to stand alone. The novel was first intended as a standalone book, but its success led to a sequel, The Court of the Dead, published in 2025.
