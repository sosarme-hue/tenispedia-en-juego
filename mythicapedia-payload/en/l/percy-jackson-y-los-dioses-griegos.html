Greek myths retold by a modern demigod

Percy Jackson's Greek Gods

Percy Jackson's Greek Gods is a collection of short stories in which Percy Jackson tells the myths of ancient Greece with humor and sarcasm. Rick Riordan published it in 2014 as a companion to his series, moving from the origins of the world to the twelve major gods and goddesses of Olympus.

Percy Jackson's Greek Gods is a collection of short stories that Rick Riordan wrote as a companion to the Percy Jackson and the Olympians series. Its defining feature is the narrator: the myths are told by Percy himself, the young demigod readers already know from the books, and he does so with an irreverent wit that turns ancient figures into familiar, and often rather ridiculous, characters.

Riordan first hinted at the project in April 2013, when he announced on Twitter that he was writing a new book retelling Greek mythology from Percy's point of view. The book was released in the United States in August 2014. In the United Kingdom and much of the Commonwealth it appeared under the title Percy Jackson and the Greek Gods, and it is often presented as the sixth volume of the series. The illustrator, John Rocco, previewed one of his artworks showing Hades carrying off Persephone, a scene from one of the best-known stories in Greek myth.

The book is organized into nineteen chapters. It opens with the creation of the world, Gaia and the Titans, drawing on the tradition found in ancient sources such as Hesiod's Theogony, and then introduces the twelve major gods of Olympus. Along the way, the collection mixes classic myths with individual tales about gods that readers first met in the Camp Half-Blood novels. The chapter titles set the tone at once, with lines such as Zeus Kills Everyone and Artemis Unleashes the Death Pig.

What makes the book interesting is the way Riordan uses a modern teenager's viewpoint to reinterpret old stories without losing their basic shape. Percy judges the gods, complains about their behavior, and comments on their choices, which brings out their contradictions in a humorous way. For readers who already know the myths, it offers a fresh take; for those who arrive from the series, it is a way back to the stories behind the adventures. Riordan later announced a sequel, Percy Jackson's Greek Heroes, focused on the heroes of Greek myth.

Trivia

A different title. In the United Kingdom and much of the Commonwealth the book was published as Percy Jackson and the Greek Gods, a slightly different title from the American edition.
A sequel announced. During the Blood of Olympus tour, Riordan announced a sequel, Percy Jackson's Greek Heroes, which came out in 2015.
John Rocco's illustrations. The book includes illustrations throughout, and Rocco was the artist who drew them.
