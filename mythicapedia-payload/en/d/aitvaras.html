nature spirit of Lithuanian folklore

Aitvaras

The aitvaras is a nature spirit in Lithuanian mythology, closely related to the Latvian pūķis. It usually appears as a white or black rooster with a fiery, meteor-like tail, and its arrival in a house brings both good fortune and trouble.

The myth

According to tradition, the aitvaras hatches from an egg laid by a rooster aged between nine and fifteen. When it dies, it does not vanish entirely but turns into a spark. Although it is sometimes described as a bird with the look of a dragon, its most common form is that of a rooster with a burning tail.

Once it settles into a house, it rarely wants to leave. It brings its hosts stolen gold and grain, but it often gets them into trouble too, so living with one is a mix of blessing and nuisance.

Trivia

Many names. It is also known as Atvaras, Damavykas, Pūkis or Spirukas. It is identical to the Latvian pūķis.
After death. When an aitvaras dies, it turns into a spark instead of vanishing without a trace.
