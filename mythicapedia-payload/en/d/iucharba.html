one of the Sons of Tuireann, Irish hero

Iucharba

Iucharba is one of the three Sons of Tuireann, heroes of Gaelic mythology, along with his brothers Iuchar and Brian. Their story, the tragedy of the Sons of Tuireann, is one of the great narratives of the Celtic tradition.

The myth

According to the tale Oidheadh Chlainne Tuireann, the brothers kill Cian, the enemy of their father and father of Lugh, one of the Tuatha Dé Danann. Cian shapeshifts into a pig, but they hunt him as dogs, kill him and dismember his body to hide the crime.

Lugh sets them a quest across the known world to gather magical weapons. They gather them, but return wounded, and Lugh refuses to heal them. In some versions he gives them eight impossible tasks; they fail the last one, and he kills them.

Legacy

Their tale has been compared to the Greek Argonautica, for its quest for magical objects in distant lands.

Ancient sources: The tale Oidheadh Chlainne Tuireann (The Tragedy of the Sons of Tuireann), preserved in medieval Irish manuscripts.

Trivia

Another name. In many manuscripts Brian appears as Uar or Uair, a name that modern editors have corrected to Brian.
