wife of Danaus in Greek mythology

Elephantis

Elephantis is, in Greek mythology, one of the wives of Danaus, king of Libya, and the mother of the Danaids Hypermnestra and Gorgophone. Her descendants founded a new branch of the Argive dynasty.

The myth

According to the tradition, Danaus had many wives, and Elephantis bore him Hypermnestra and Gorgophone. Gorgophone married Proteus and murdered him on their wedding night. Hypermnestra, by contrast, spared the life of her husband Lynceus. Both couples started a new line of the Argive dynasty, from which descended Acrisius, Danae, Perseus and Heracles, among others.

The accounts do not fully agree. According to Hippostratus, Danaus had all his offspring with Europa, daughter of the river-god Nilus, and in some versions he married Melia, daughter of his uncle Agenor, king of Tyre.
