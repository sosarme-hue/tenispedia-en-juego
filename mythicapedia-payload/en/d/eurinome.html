Oceanid and ancient goddess of Greek mythology

Eurynome

Eurynome is an Oceanid of Greek mythology, daughter of Oceanus and Tethys, and one of the oldest goddesses of their pantheon. She is remembered as the mother of the Charites by Zeus, as an early queen of Olympus and as the protector of the young Hephaestus, which makes her a key figure in the most archaic stories.
