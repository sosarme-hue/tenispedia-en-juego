mythical king of Phrygia

Teleutas

Teleutas is a mythical king of Phrygia who appears in Greek tradition as the father of Tecmessa. He matters because of an episode of the Trojan War told by Sophocles.

The myth

In Sophocles' tragedy Ajax, the hero Ajax, son of the king of Salamis, took Tecmessa captive during a raid in the Trojan War. Tecmessa was the daughter of Teleutas, ruler of Phrygia. To attack his territory, Ajax had to destroy the kingdom or city of the king and his wife, leaving the young woman without a homeland.

In the play, Tecmessa laments that if Ajax died she would have no country to return to. The story thus shows a captive woman who depends entirely on her master and husband. No other ancient source in the material mentions this king.

Ancient sources: Sophocles, Ajax, where he is named as the father of Tecmessa.
