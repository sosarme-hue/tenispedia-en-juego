valkyrie of Norse mythology

Hlökk

Hlökk is a valkyrie of Norse mythology, whose Old Norse name means noise or battle. She belongs to the group of warrior maidens whom Norse texts link with the battlefield and the fate of fallen warriors.

The myth

Her name appears in the poem Grímnismál, included in the Poetic Edda, which lists thirteen valkyries. She is one of the few things recorded about her, since texts mention her mostly within a list rather than as the protagonist of a story of her own.

In Norse imagination, valkyries are beings of supernatural nature who serve Odin and accompany warriors who have fallen in battle. Hlökk also appears in both name lists (Nafnaþulur) of the Prose Edda, which suggests that her place in the tradition was consistent across different authors.

Ancient sources: Grímnismál in the Poetic Edda and the Nafnaþulur lists in the Prose Edda.
