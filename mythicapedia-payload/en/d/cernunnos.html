Celtic god of fertility and lord of animals

Cernunnos

Cernunnos is a god of Celtic religion, shown as a man with stag antlers, often seated with crossed legs and accompanied by serpents and wild animals. His name appears only once, on the Pillar of the Boatmen in Paris, and today it is used to group a set of images linked to fertility and the natural world.
