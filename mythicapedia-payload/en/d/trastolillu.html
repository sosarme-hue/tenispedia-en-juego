domestic sprite of Cantabrian folklore

Trastolillu

The trastolillu is a household sprite from Cantabrian tradition, a protector of the home who is also very mischievous. It resembles the Asturian and Galician trasgo and shares traits with the Germanic kobolds.

The myth

He is described as a little genie as black as soot, with long hair, bright green eyes, twisted fangs and small horns. He wears a red cloak made of tree bark sewn together with ivy, covers his head with a white cap and often carries a small wooden staff. His most common pranks include spilling flour, drinking fresh milk, hiding clogs in impossible places, rattling window latches in the wind and burning stews, all amid shrieks of laughter and mock wailing if he is caught.

According to tradition, one way to get rid of him is to give him impossible tasks, such as fetching water in a sieve or carrying ash in a wet sack. Unable to complete them, he leaves in shame, although he often comes back.

Legacy

His mischievous figure has inspired modern handmade creations, such as the Asturian Magikitos, which reinterpret these folklore beings alongside the trasgo.

Trivia

Impossible tasks. To get rid of him, people give him jobs he cannot finish, such as collecting water in a perforated basket. Having failed, he leaves in shame.
