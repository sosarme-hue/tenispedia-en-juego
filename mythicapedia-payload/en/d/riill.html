sword of Norse mythology

Riðill

Riðill is a sword from Norse mythology, one of the many named weapons that appear in the traditions of the Scandinavian and Germanic peoples. The available sources barely describe it, so it is known mainly by its name.

The myth

Ancient texts that preserve the Norse tradition mention many named weapons, and some have detailed stories, such as Thor's hammer Mjölnir or Odin's spear Gungnir. Others appear only as a name or in a brief reference. Riðill belongs to this second group: the available material mentions the sword without explaining its origin, who owned it or what deeds it performed.

These weapons often have their own stories, in which they pass from hand to hand, are inherited or decide battles, but in the case of Riðill there is not enough information to rebuild a narrative. Anyone wishing to learn more will need to consult the original sources.
