form of Vishnu in Krishnaite Hinduism

Garbhodakasayi Vishnu

Garbhodakasayi Vishnu is one of the principal forms of Vishnu in the Krishnaite tradition of Hinduism, the form that lies in the ocean found at the base of every material universe. His figure shows how the preserver god manifests in the cosmology of this current.

The myth

According to Krishnaite accounts, Garbhodakasayi Vishnu lies on the serpent Shesha, which floats on an ocean at the bottom of each of the countless universes. From his navel, also called Padmanabha, a giant lotus grows.

From the lotus is born Brahma, who with his mind creates the planets of the material universe through Prakriti. At the feet of the reclining god is Lakshmi, sized to match him.

This form is placed among two others: Karanodakasayi Vishnu, who floats on the causal ocean in Vaikuntha, and Ksirodakasayi Vishnu, who rests on the ocean of milk of this planet. From the latter expands Paramatma, who accompanies every soul from within the heart.
