Grootslang

The Grootslang is a legendary creature of South African folklore, said to dwell in a cave in Richtersveld. Its name means 'big snake' in Afrikaans and Dutch, and it embodies fear of a monstrous guardian of diamonds.

The myth

According to the tale, the Grootslang predates the world. The gods, still inexperienced at creation, gave it so much strength and cunning that it became dangerous, so they split it into separate creatures; from these came the first elephants and snakes. One escaped, and all the others descend from it.

It lives in a cave called the Wonder Hole, said to be filled with diamonds, and it lures elephants into it. It craves gems, and according to the story, anyone who offers enough of them may buy their freedom.

Legacy

It appears in the series The Secret Saturdays, the comic Lumberjanes and the video game Kingdom Rush: Origins. Writer Lawrence G. Green suggested the legend may have arisen from sightings of real pythons.

Trivia

Diamonds in the eyes. Some versions say the monster has diamonds set in its eye sockets, a detail that Green considers a narrative exaggeration.
A modern image. In 21st-century popular culture the Grootslang is often shown with an elephant's trunk, tusks and four legs, features that come from author Carol Rose's description.
