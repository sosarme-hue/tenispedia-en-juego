sea goddess of Hindu-Buddhist and Southeast Asian mythology

Manimekhala

Manimekhala is a sea goddess of Hindu-Buddhist mythology, regarded as a guardian of the Indian Ocean and the South China Sea in Southeast Asian folklore. She matters for her role as protector of sailors and for the way her figure travelled and was adapted across many cultures of the region.

The myth

In Buddhist tradition, Cātummahārājika placed her in charge of protecting virtuous beings from shipwreck. In the Mahajanaka Jataka, the goddess rescues Prince Mahajanaka from a shipwreck and carries him to safety.

In the classical literature of Cambodia and Thailand, her story is entwined with that of Ramasura, usually identified with Parashurama, and with Arjuna. According to the legend, lightning comes from the flash of Manimekhala's crystal ball as she flees across the sky, and thunder from the sound of Ramasura's axe as he pursues her.

In the Tamil Buddhist epic Manimekalai, she puts the heroine to sleep and carries her to the island of Maṇipallavam, where she later becomes a Buddhist nun. In the cycle of the god Devol, when his ship founders near Sri Lanka, Manimekhalai, on Śakra's orders, conjures a stone boat that saves him.

Worship and religion

In Cambodia, the classical dance Robam Moni Mekhala belongs to the buong suong, one of the most sacred suites of Khmer dance, and is performed in ceremonies to invoke rain. In Thailand, the Mekkhala-Ramasun dance opened performances of lakhon nai or khon as a prelude.

Legacy

Her name has reached popular culture: Burmese pop singer May Kha Lar took it as her stage name, Thailand has used it for typhoon names, and since 1980 a Thai television award has been called the Mekkhala Award.

Ancient sources: Manimekalai, a Tamil Buddhist epic, and the Pali Mahajanaka Jataka, which present her as a protector of seafarers.

Trivia

A typhoon with her name. Thailand proposed the name Mekkhala for the typhoon naming list, and it has been used in 2002, 2008, 2015, 2020 and 2026.
A television award. The Mekkhala Award is given to the Thai television industry and has been presented since 1980.
