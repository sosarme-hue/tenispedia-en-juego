legendary king of Sicyon

Leucippus

Leucippus is a legendary king of Sicyon, an ancient Greek city, to whom tradition assigns a reign of 53 years. His lineage places him among the earliest rulers of the city, with roots in an autochthonous ancestor.

The myth

According to tradition, Leucippus was the son and heir of King Thurimachus, and descended from Aegyrus and Thelxion, in a line that went back to Aegialeus, regarded as autochthonous.

Pausanias tells that Leucippus had only one child, a daughter named Calchinia, who bore Poseidon a son, Peratus. The boy was reared by his grandfather and inherited the kingdom, so power passed to the next generation.

In some accounts, Leucippus was succeeded by Messapus as the ninth king of Sicyon.

Ancient sources: Pausanias, in his Description of Greece, is the source that records his lineage, his only child and the succession to the throne.
