Egyptian god of the north wind

Qebui

Qebui is a god of ancient Egypt associated with the north wind. He belonged to a group of deities who governed the winds of the four directions, and he was depicted with ram features.

The myth

The sources about Qebui are few, and his stories have not survived in detail. What we know comes mostly from his imagery: he was shown as a man with four ram heads, or as a winged ram with four heads.

Within Egyptian tradition he formed a set with Henkhisesui (east wind), Ḥutchai (west wind) and Shehbui (south wind), each with his own form and place in the order of the winds. This compass-based arrangement was common in Egyptian culture and gave every natural force a place.
