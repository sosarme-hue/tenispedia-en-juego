Maya god of death and earthquakes

Cizin

Cizin, also known as Kisen or Kisin, is the Maya god of death and earthquakes and the most important death deity in Maya culture. Scholars call him God A, and to the Yucatán Maya he was also known as Hun-Came and Vucub-Came.

The myth

His name probably means "stench," from a Yucatec Maya root referring to flatulence or a foul smell. It may also have been a title, "the Stinking One," since he was believed to smell of decay. He went by many names, including Ah Puch, Yum Cimil and Hunhau, and today the Maya still call him Yum Cim or Yum Cimil, "lord of death."

According to a Lacandon myth, when someone dies Cizin burns the soul on its mouth and anus. If the soul complains, he douses it in cold water, which makes it cry out even more, and he burns it again until it dissolves into nothing. The soul then goes to the god Sucunyum, who cleans it by spitting into his hand and lets it go wherever it wishes.

Legacy

He appears as Ah Pook in William S. Burroughs's book Ah Pook Is Here (1979). He also appears as Ah Puch in a 2006 episode of Ben 10, as a playable character in Smite (2014), and as the main antagonist of J. C. Cervantes's novel The Storm Runner (2018). He also features in the video game 9 Clues: The Secret of Serpent Creek (2013) and in Maya and the Three (2021), voiced by Rita Moreno.
