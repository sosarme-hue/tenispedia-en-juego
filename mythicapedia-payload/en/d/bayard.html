magical horse of medieval chivalric romance

Bayard

Bayard is a magical bay horse of supernatural origin, a star of the French chansons de geste of the Middle Ages. His legend still lives on in the folklore of the Ardennes and Belgium, where local rocks are linked to him and processions still honour him.

The myth

In The Four Sons of Aymon, a twelfth-century chanson de geste, Bayard belongs to Renaud de Montauban. His origin is marvellous: some versions make him the offspring of a dragon and a serpent and say that the magician Maugis rescued him from a volcanic island. The horse is so strong that he can carry all four brothers at once, and he understands human speech.

When Charlemagne demands that Renaud hand the animal over, the king punishes him by throwing him into the river with a millstone tied around his neck. Bayard smashes the stone with his hooves, escapes, and lives on forever in the forests of the Ardennes, where tradition says his neigh can be heard at every midsummer solstice. In later versions Maugis wins him before he reaches Renaud, and the river may be the Rhine or the Meuse.

Worship and religion

He had no formal religious cult, but he has a central place in popular festivals in Flanders and Wallonia. In processions such as the Ducasse d'Ath and the Ommegang of Dendermonde, Bayard appears among the northern giants, and these celebrations continue today.

Legacy

His name became a common word for any reddish-brown bay horse. Chaucer used it for a blind, foolish horse in his works, and Bayard Rock near Dinant recalls the legend of his hooves. There are statues of him in Ghent, Namur and Grembergen, and in Riudoms, in Catalonia, the Cavall dels Nebot is carried through the streets every year.

Ancient sources: The chanson de geste The Four Sons of Aymon, from the twelfth century, is his foundational text; later Pulci, Boiardo, Ariosto and Tasso took him up.

Trivia

A rock with a hoofprint. According to local tradition, Bayard Rock near Dinant was split by the horse's hooves.
A horse that changes size. Bayard has the supernatural ability to adjust his size to fit his riders.
