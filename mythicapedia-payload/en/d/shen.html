Chinese concept of deity and spirit

Shen

Shen (神) is the Chinese term for deities and spirits, ranging from dead ancestors to the forces that shape the world. It is also a key concept in Chinese philosophy, religion and traditional medicine.

The myth

According to tradition, shen are gods or spirits deeply involved in human affairs, and from them come rivers, mountains, thunder and stars. In the sanbao theory, Heaven is linked to shen and the heart, and Earth to jing and the kidneys. Shennong, the Divine Farmer, a culture hero credited with inventing agriculture, carries the term in his name.

Worship and religion

The character includes a left-hand element related to the altar and ritual, and originally it referred to a stone table used for sacrifices. Veneration continues in Taoism, Buddhism and Chinese folk religion, where balance between yin and yang is sought.

Legacy

The term appears in many Chinese compounds and in traditional medicine. In Japanese it relates to shin, and in Christian translation of 'God' into Chinese it became a matter of debate.

Ancient sources: Classical Chinese texts use the term in its various senses, and the Erya, one of the earliest Chinese dictionaries, records some of its meanings.

Trivia

Lightning in the character. The earliest form of the character includes a component depicting lightning, a sign for ancient people that lightning was a manifestation of the divine.
