nymph in Greek mythology, mother of Chrysippus

Axioche

Axioche is, in Greek mythology, a woman or nymph known mainly as the mother of Chrysippus, a son she had with King Pelops. She matters because her son was born outside Pelops's marriage to Hippodamia.

The myth

Little is recorded about Axioche's origins, since her parentage is not known from the sources. What is recorded is her link to Pelops, a king who was already married to Hippodamia when Chrysippus was born. For this reason the boy is described as illegitimate, a detail that shapes his place in the royal household and keeps Axioche on the fringes of Pelops's story.

In other accounts the same figure is called Danais, which shows that the stories do not always agree on her identity. This variety of names is common among minor figures of Greek myth, who were passed down in fragments and under different names depending on local tradition or the author recording them.
