Thracian warrior of the Trojan War

Peirous

Peirous (Πείροος), also spelled Peiroos, was a Thracian war leader from the city of Aenus and an ally of King Priam during the Trojan War. He matters for his role as a warrior on the Trojan side and for his place in the family tree of the heroes who fought at Troy.

The myth

According to tradition, Peirous was the son of Imbrasos and the father of Rhygmus, who fought at Troy alongside his father. He led the Thracians of Aenus in defence of Troy, a sign of the wide network of allies that Priam gathered for his cause.

The story goes that Peirous was killed by Thoas, leader of the Aetolians. His death is recorded as one of the many losses among allied warriors that marked the conflict.

Legacy

His name lives on in the asteroid 2893 Peiroos, a Jovian body named after this figure of Greek mythology.

Trivia

An asteroid named after him. The asteroid 2893 Peiroos, a Jovian asteroid, is named after this Thracian war leader.
Father and son at Troy. His son Rhygmus fought at Troy alongside him, according to the accounts that name both warriors.
