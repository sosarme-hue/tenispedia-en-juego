Gallo-Roman goddess of the river Yonne

Icaunis

Icaunis is a river goddess of Gaul, the divinity of the Yonne river in present-day Burgundy. She belongs to Gallo-Roman religion and is known almost only from a single inscription.

The myth

No stories about Icaunis have survived, so nothing is known of her origin, her family or her deeds. What we can say is that she was identified with the Yonne and that her power was linked to its waters.

The inscription, from the Gallo-Roman period, presents her as the goddess of that river and is the basis of nearly everything known about her. Any detailed story about Icaunis would therefore be a modern reconstruction.

Worship and religion

Her worship is documented only through the inscription found at Auxerre, the town beside the Yonne. No evidence survives about temples, festivals or offerings.

Ancient sources: A single Gallo-Roman inscription found at Auxerre, in present-day Burgundy.
