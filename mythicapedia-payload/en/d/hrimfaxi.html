night horse in Norse mythology

Hrímfaxi

Hrímfaxi, whose name means "frost mane", is the horse of Nótt, the night, in Norse mythology. He is paired with Skinfaxi, the horse of day, and together they account for the cycle that repeats between night and day.

The myth

According to Norse tradition, each of the two horses has a role in the sky. Skinfaxi pulls the chariot of Dagr, the day, and his mane lights up the sky and the earth below. Hrímfaxi serves Nótt, the night, and his name refers to his icy appearance.

The stories also tell of a detail about his journey: the froth that falls from his bit toward the earth becomes dew. In this way, the night horse is directly linked to the droplets that appear on the grass at dawn.
