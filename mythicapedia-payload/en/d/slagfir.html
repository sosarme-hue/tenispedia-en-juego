Viking prince of Norse mythology

Slagfiðr

Slagfiðr is a Viking prince of Norse mythology, son of Finn of Frisia and brother of Völundr and Agilaz. He matters chiefly because he appears as one of three brothers in the poem Völundarkviða, preserved in the Poetic Edda.

The myth

According to the tradition preserved in the Poetic Edda, Slagfiðr belongs to a trio of brothers with Völundr and Agilaz, who take centre stage in the Völundarkviða. The poem presents him as the husband, for seven years, of the valkyrie Hlaðguðr Svanhvít, one of the female figures linked to battlefields in Norse tradition.

His name, in Old Norse, is usually read as "beating-Finn", though the sources do not clarify its exact meaning. The surviving references are brief, so many details of his life and deeds remain in the shadows.

Ancient sources: The Poetic Edda, in the poem Völundarkviða.
