Japanese yōkai of smoke and darkness

Enenra

The enenra is a Japanese yōkai, or monster, made of smoke and darkness. It is usually regarded as a demon or a divine being of darkness and smoke, and it first appears in Japanese tradition in the Konjaku Hyakki Shūi.

The myth

According to tradition, enenra mostly dwell in bonfires and, when they emerge, they take human shape or another form. It is said that only those with a pure heart can see them, which makes them almost invisible to most people.

The tale distinguishes two kinds. The more common are enenra born as such, while the rarer kind are people who died and were transformed into enenra. Both forms share the same nature of smoke and darkness. So an enenra may be a being of darkness from the start or the remnant of a human life that has ended.

Ancient sources: It first appears in the Konjaku Hyakki Shūi, c. 1781.
