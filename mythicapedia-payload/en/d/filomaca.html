Minyan princess and queen of Iolcus in Greek mythology

Philomache

Philomache is a Minyan princess of Greek mythology, daughter of King Amphion of Orchomenus, whom some traditions make queen of Iolcus. Her figure is little known and appears in very few ancient sources.

The myth

According to the account, Philomache was sister to Chloris, wife of Neleus, and she was married to Pelias, king of Iolcus. With him she had five children: Acastus, Pisidice, Pelopia, Hippothoe and Alcestis. The tradition places her among the royal families linked to Orchomenus and Iolcus in the mythical world of the Minyans.

Other sources, however, change the story: they say the wife of Pelias and mother of these children was a daughter of Bias named Anaxibia or Alphesiboea. Philomache's role therefore depends on which version one follows, and in the ancient texts her name stays in the background.

Ancient sources: Apollodorus is the only ancient author who mentions her, in his mythographic work.
