Metiadusa

Metiadusa is a figure of Greek mythology from the Athenian royal family. She is best known as the wife of King Cecrops II and the mother of Pandion.

The myth

According to tradition, Metiadusa was the daughter of Prince Eupalamus, and her mother may have been Alcippe, although accounts do not fully agree on this point. She was also the sister of Daedalus and Perdix, two names that give her family a prominent place in Athenian stories.

She married King Cecrops II of Athens and became the mother of Pandion. Her role in the stories is modest: no deeds or episodes are attributed to her alone, and her importance lies mainly in her place in the royal genealogy, linking one generation of the House of Athens to the next.
