minor Roman god of sowing

Insitor

Insitor is a minor god of Roman religion, associated with the sowing of crops. He belongs to the group of agricultural deities who assisted Ceres, goddess of harvests and grain.

The myth

The surviving texts hardly record any stories about Insitor, so his mythical personality remains very vague. His importance lies in the specific role he played: accompanying the work of sowing, one of the key moments of the Roman agricultural cycle, under the protection of Ceres.

As an assistant to the goddess, Insitor does not appear as the protagonist of heroic tales or of conflicts among the gods, but as a presence tied to the everyday work of the fields.

Worship and religion

His name was invoked during the Cerealia, the festival dedicated to Ceres, alongside the eleven other helper gods of the goddess. This group shows how Roman religion divided agricultural duties among many minor deities, each with a specific part in the cycle of crops.
