hero of Greco-Roman mythology linked to Iberia and Sardinia

Norax

Norax is a hero of Greco-Roman mythology, son of Hermes and Erytheia, linked by ancient sources to the Iberian Peninsula and to the founding of the city of Nora in Sardinia. His story matters because it explains, through tradition, the legendary settlement of the island.

The myth

According to tradition, Norax was the son of the god Hermes and Erytheia, daughter of Geryon. Pausanias relates that he led a group of Iberians to Sardinia, where he founded the city of Nora, which was regarded as the first city on the island.

Solinus adds that Norax came from Tartessos in southern Iberia. His expedition belongs to several mythical traditions about the colonization of Sardinia, alongside figures such as Sardus, Aristaeus and Iolaus. These stories explain the arrival of peoples on the island from the viewpoint of ancient accounts.

Ancient sources: Pausanias and Solinus, Greek and Roman writers who record his tradition.
