prince of Lesbos, son of Poseidon

Myton

Myton is a prince of the island of Lesbos, son of the god Poseidon, to whom Greek tradition credits the founding of the city of Mytilene, which would become the island's chief city. His name is tied to that of the city.

The myth

According to the myth, Poseidon fathered Myton with Mytilene, daughter of King Macareus of Lesbos. Another tradition, recorded in later sources, makes Mytilene the daughter of Pelops, king of Pisa in Elis, so the genealogy is not uniform. Mother and son are linked to the founding of the city, which took its name from her or from him.

Stephanus of Byzantium mentions Myton, but notes that others gave the city's name to a different figure called Mytile. The origin of the place name thus remained open among the ancient accounts.

Ancient sources: Stephanus of Byzantium mentions him in his geographical work, which also records the alternative explanation of the city's name.

Trivia

A disputed name. Stephanus of Byzantium records that some attributed the name Mytilene to a figure called Mytile, not to Myton.
