wise seer of Irish mythology

Fintan mac Bóchra

Fintan mac Bóchra, 'the Wise', is a seer from Irish mythology who, according to tradition, arrived in Ireland before the Flood with Cessair, Noah's granddaughter. His long life and shape-shifting make him the keeper of all the island's knowledge and history.

The myth

According to the story, Fintan sailed to Ireland with Cessair in an expedition of three men and fifty women; each man had sixteen wives, and he also had a son, Illann. When the Flood came, his family drowned, but he survived in the form of a salmon and spent a year under the water in a cave called Fintan's Grave.

He then turned into an eagle and a hawk before regaining human form. He lived 5500 years after the Deluge as an advisor to kings; he counselled the Fir Bolg king Eochaid mac Eirc when the Tuatha Dé Danann invaded, and fought in the first Battle of Magh Tuiredh. In the time of Fionn mac Cumhail he met a magical hawk born on the same day as him, and at the end of their lives the two shared their stories and chose to leave the mortal world in the 5th century, after Ireland had become Christian.

Trivia

Confusion with the Salmon of Knowledge. Because he can turn into a salmon and bears the title 'the Wise', he is often confused with the Salmon of Knowledge, a similarly named animal figure linked to Fionn mac Cumhail.
A life of five millennia. According to tradition, Fintan lived 5500 years after the Flood, outlasting successive invasions of Ireland while advising its kings.
