Hindu goddess, companion of Radha and Krishna

Lalita

Lalita is a Hindu goddess and one of the most prominent companions of the divine couple Radha and Krishna. In many Vaishnava traditions she is regarded as the chief of the Ashtasakhi, the eight eternal companions of the two.

The myth

According to Vaishnava tradition, Lalita was born in Unchagaon, near Barsana, in the Braj region during Dvapara Yuga. She was the daughter of Visoka and Saradi, and is described as being twenty-seven days older than Radha. Her duty was to arrange the meetings and divine pastimes of the couple, such as the Raslila, the Radha Krishna Vivah lila and the Lathmar Holi.

When Krishna left Vrindavan, according to the Brahma Vaivarta Purana and the Garga Samhita, Lalita and the other Ashtasakhi followed Radha to the Kadli forest. After a hundred years of separation, Krishna returned, summoned a vast celestial chariot and carried Radha, the gopis and the people of Braj to Goloka, his heavenly abode.

Worship and religion

Lalita is worshipped in several Vaishnava traditions, including Gaudiya Vaishnavism, Radha Vallabh Sampradaya and Haridasi Sampradaya. Her main temple stands in Unchagaon, her birthplace, where she is worshipped beside Krishna as his consort, and she is also honoured in temples in Vrindavan, Karauli and Barsana. Her birthday is marked in Braj with the festival of Lalita Saptami, one day before Radhashtami.

Legacy

In Ramanand Sagar's 1993 television series Shri Krishna she was played by Papiya Sengupta, and in the 2018 series RadhaKrishn she was portrayed by Priya Thanki.

Ancient sources: She is mentioned in the Garga Samhita, the Padma Purana, the Narad Purana and the Brahma Vaivarta Purana.
