mother of King Arcesius of Ithaca

Euryodeia

Euryodeia (also Euryodia) is a figure from Greek mythology, the mother of King Arcesius of Ithaca. She is of interest because she shows how Greek stories explained the origin of a royal line through unions with gods or other figures.

The myth

In the main version, Euryodeia was loved by Zeus, and from that union Arcesius was born, later becoming king of Ithaca.

Other accounts change the hero's origin entirely. In these, Arcesius is the son of Cephalus and Procris, a couple whose story appears in several Greek traditions. A third version presents him as born from a she-bear who had been transformed into a woman, a detail that links his lineage to the wild world.
