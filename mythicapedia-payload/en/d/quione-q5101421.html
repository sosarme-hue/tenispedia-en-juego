Greek nymph associated with snow

Chione

Chione is a naiad in Greek mythology, daughter of the river Nile and the Oceanid Callirrhoe, whom the myth links to snow. Her story, preserved in only one late source, explains the origin of the snow that falls over the desert.

The myth

According to the myth, Callirrhoe, Chione's mother, was raped by a local peasant. At the order of Zeus, Hermes transformed the young woman into a snow cloud and placed her among the clouds.

From then on, the clouds cast snow over the desert, and the myth portrays snow as an enemy of the peasants' work.

Legacy

The Greek word for snow, chiōn, was thought to have come from her name, though this is a hypothesis rather than an established fact.
