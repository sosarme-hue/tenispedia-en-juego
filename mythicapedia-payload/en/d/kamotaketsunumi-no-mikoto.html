Japanese god, founder of the Kamo clan

Kamotaketsunumi no Mikoto

Kamotaketsunumi no Mikoto is a god in Japanese mythology and the founder of the Kamo clan of Yamashiro Province. He is also identified with Yatagarasu, the three-legged crow that guided Emperor Jimmu.

The myth

According to the tradition, Kamotaketsunumi descended from heaven on the orders of Takamimusubi and Amaterasu, reached the peak of Tsune in Hyuga, and arrived at Mount Katsuragi in Yamato. There he took the form of Yatagarasu, guided Emperor Jimmu and helped him win his campaign. He later settled at Kamo in Yamashiro, where the Katsuno and Kamo rivers meet, as the Yamashiro Fudoki recounts.

His genealogy varies between authors. The Shinsen Shojiroku makes him a grandson of Kamimusubi, while other accounts name Futodama as his father. He had two children, Tamayorihiko and Tamayorihime. It is said that Tamayorihime conceived the deity of Kamigamo Shrine after placing a red lacquered arrow beside her bed.

Worship and religion

He is the deity of Shimogamo Shrine, linked to his clan. The sources give no details about specific festivals or rites.

Ancient sources: He appears in the Kojiki and the Nihon Shoki, where he helps Emperor Jimmu during the eastern expedition.

Trivia

A golden kite. The Nihon Shoki also says that a golden kite, kinshi, helped Jimmu in his battle against Naganomushihiko. This bird is associated with the god Amanohiwashi.
Several gods, one figure. Hirata Atsutane and other scholars considered Amanohiwashi the same figure as Ame-no-hikawashi-no-mikoto and Kamo-kenkakumikami-no-mikoto.
