winged creature of Serbian folklore

Cikavac

The cikavac is a creature from Serbian folklore, a winged bird with a long beak and a pelican-like sack. It was obtained through a ritual and was said to steal from the neighbours.

The myth

According to Serbian tradition, a cikavac was obtained by taking an egg from a black hen. A woman had to carry it under her armpit for 40 days, during which she could not confess, cut her nails, wash her face or pray. The account describes in detail the prohibitions she had to observe throughout that time.

Once the period was over, the creature worked for its owner: it sucked honey from other people's beehives and milk from their cattle, and brought it back to her. It also granted any wish of its owner and allowed her to understand the language of animals. Tradition presents it as a shortcut to wealth and favours without visible effort.

Trivia

A bird with a pouch. It is described as a winged bird with a long beak and a pelican-like pouch.
A demanding ritual. For 40 days the woman carrying the egg could not confess, cut her nails, wash her face or pray.
