wife of Aeson and mother of Jason in Greek mythology

Polymede

Polymede is a figure of Greek mythology, the wife of King Aeson and mother of Jason, the hero of the Golden Fleece. Her name also appears in other stories, showing how varied the traditions about female characters in Greek myth could be.

The myth

According to tradition, Polymede was the daughter of Autolycus and the partner of Aeson, king of Iolcus. From their marriage came Jason, whose destiny was shaped by his uncle Pelias, who had seized the throne from Aeson. To keep the child safe, the family sent him away from the court.

In some versions, Polymede also appears as the mother of Nestor, born of her union with Neleus, according to a later commentator. This genealogy differs from the usual account, so Polymede is presented with more than one identity within the Greek tradition.
