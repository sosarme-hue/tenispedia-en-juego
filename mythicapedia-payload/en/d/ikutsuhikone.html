Japanese god, fourth child born from Amaterasu's jewels

Ikutsuhikone

Ikutsuhikone is a god of Japanese mythology, one of the kami who emerged from the creation contest between Amaterasu and Susanoo. He is best known for his birth story, which combines a rivalry between two divinities with the origin of several gods.

The myth

According to the story, Amaterasu and Susanoo competed to create deities. Susanoo took Amaterasu's jewels, chewed them in his mouth and spat them out, and from them came five male gods. Ikutsuhikone was the fourth born, after Ame-no-oshihomimi, Ame-no-hohi and Amatsuhikone; the fifth was Kumanokusubi.

In many versions, then, Ikutsuhikone is a son of Amaterasu, born from her jewels. In another tradition he comes directly from Susanoo's jewels, and in that case he is Susanoo's son. His parentage therefore varies by telling, although the record lists him as a son of Susanoo and Amaterasu. His siblings include Ōkuninushi and Amenooshihomimi.
