Great Marquis of Hell in Goetic demonology

Forneus

Forneus is a demon of Western demonology, a Great Marquis of Hell described as a teacher of rhetoric and languages. He appears in the catalogues of demons in the Goetic tradition, where he is credited with power over reputation and human relationships.

The myth

According to demonology, Forneus is a Great Marquis of Hell with twenty-nine legions of demons under his rule. His specialty is teaching: he instructs in rhetoric and languages, and he grants those who summon him a good name and the affection of both friends and foes.

He is depicted as a great sea monster, though he can take many different forms and mainly prefers his human shape. Some accounts describe him as a mixed nature, partly of the Order of Thrones and partly of the Order of Angels. His name is thought to derive from the Latin fornus or furnus, meaning oven.
