magic ring of King Solomon

Seal of Solomon

The Seal of Solomon is a legendary signet ring attributed to King Solomon, which tradition credits with power over spirits and animals. Born in Jewish mysticism, it spread through Islamic mysticism and Western occultism, and it is the predecessor of the Star of David.

The myth

According to the tales, God himself engraved the ring and handed it to Solomon directly from heaven. It was made of brass and iron: each metal was used to seal commands addressed to good and evil spirits, respectively. With it, the king commanded demons and, in some versions, could speak with animals.

A famous episode tells how the demon Asmodeus, or Sakhr in other versions, seized the ring and ruled in Solomon's place for forty days. In another variant, inspired by the ring of Polycrates in Herodotus, the demon threw it into the sea, a fish swallowed it, a charitable fisherman caught the fish and unknowingly fed it to the deposed king, who regained his throne. The Talmud, in the tractate Gittin, tells a similar story: Solomon sent Benaiah to catch the demon Ashmedai to obtain his help in building the temple, but the demon tricked the king into giving him the ring and swallowed it.

Legacy

The seal gave rise to the Star of David, which became a symbol of Judaism and has appeared on the flag of Israel since 1948. The star on the flag of Morocco, adopted in 1915, also originally represented this seal. In 1874 an Order of Solomon's Seal was founded in Ethiopia, and the playwright Lippmann Moses Büschenthal wrote the tragedy Der Siegelring Salomonis.

Ancient sources: Josephus, the Talmud (tractate Gittin), the Testament of Solomon and the Jewish magical text Sefer HaRazim all mention it.

Trivia

A shield against demons. According to the Testament of Solomon, the two interlaced triangles confused the demons, so they could not harm the king.
An emblem in Granada. The Sacromonte Abbey in Granada decorated it profusely in the 17th century as a symbol of wisdom.
Barbarossa's flag. The flag of Hayreddin Barbarossa showed the Seal of Solomon between the blades of a zulfiqar.
