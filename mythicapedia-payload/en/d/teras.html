hero who founded Thera in Spartan tradition

Theras

Theras is a hero of Theban descent linked to early Spartan history who, according to tradition, founded the colony on the island that came to bear his name. His story connects the Spartan royal house with the line of Cadmus.

The myth

According to Herodotus and Pausanias, Theras was the son of Autesion, grandson of Tisamenus and great-great-grandson of Polynices, who descended from Cadmus, founder of the Cadmea of Thebes. When Aristodemus, king of Sparta, died, Theras, brother of his widow Argeia, took over as guardian and regent of the twins Procles and Eurysthenes, who would later rule Sparta.

When the nephews came of age and claimed power, Theras chose to leave. He gathered Dorians and a group of Minyans and sailed in three triremes-like penteconters toward the island of Calliste, inhabited by descendants of the Phoenicians, intending to form a shared community. Herodotus says his son Eolicus did not join him. Pindar records a different tradition in which some Aegidae followed Theras. Pausanias adds that the hero wanted the descendants of Membliaros to yield the throne to him, and that the Minyans had been expelled from Lemnos by the Pelasgians. The colony flourished, and the island and city came to be called Thera.

Worship and religion

Pausanias reports that annual sacrifices in honor of Theras were still held on the island in his time. In Sparta, on the second street leading from the dromos, stood a sanctuary of Athena that the hero was said to have founded at the time of the colonization, and beside the Pecile were heroa of Cadmus and his descendants Theras, Eolicus and Aegeus.

Legacy

The island of Thera, today known as Santorini, takes its name from this hero.

Ancient sources: Herodotus and Pausanias describe his lineage and the colonization of the island; Pindar offers an alternative version.

Trivia

A name that survives. The island still bears the ancient name Thera, although it is better known today as Santorini.
A sanctuary in Sparta. Pausanias places in Sparta a sanctuary of Athena that Theras founded in connection with the colonization.
