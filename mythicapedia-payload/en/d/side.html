wife of Orion in Greek myth

Side

Side is a little-known figure in Greek mythology, the first wife of the hunter Orion. Her name means «pomegranate», and her story is brief, surviving mainly in a account in the Bibliotheca.

The myth

According to the tradition, Side was beautiful and foolishly tried to rival Hera in beauty. As punishment, the goddess cast her into the Underworld. Her story appears in the Bibliotheca, attributed to pseudo-Apollodorus, and in a brief passage by Ovid, who writes that Orion wandered pale through the woods for her.

No parentage or family is recorded for her. Some have suggested she may have been the mother of his daughters Menippe and Metioche, although the text does not state it.

Legacy

Side's name is linked to the pomegranate, and some scholars read her union with Orion as an image of pomegranates ripening in autumn. An ancient Greek colony on the southern coast of Asia Minor, in Pamphylia, still bears her name, and its coins showed pomegranates.

Ancient sources: The Bibliotheca of pseudo-Apollodorus and Ovid's Art of Love.

Trivia

A name that means fruit. Side means «pomegranate» in ancient Greek. Some scholars think her story is tied to the ripening cycle of this fruit.
A parallel with Persephone. The scholar Karl Kerényi compared her story with that of Persephone, who also descends to the Underworld and is linked to the pomegranate.
