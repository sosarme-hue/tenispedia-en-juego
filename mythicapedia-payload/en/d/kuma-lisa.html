trickster fox of Eastern European folklore

Kuma Lisa

Kuma Lisa is a sly fox from Slavic folklore, found especially in Macedonian, Bulgarian, Russian and Ukrainian traditions. She embodies the trickster archetype, and her figure appears both in folk tales and in literary works by named authors.

The myth

In many folk tales, Kuma Lisa meets Kumcho Vulcho, a wolf who is usually the victim of her schemes. The stories show her repeatedly outwitting the stronger animal through cleverness rather than violence.

In some sources her name is linked to that of a sly prince called Patrikas, to whom tradition gives a reputation for cunning. Each version adds its own episodes, but the pattern repeats: the wolf falls into the trap and the fox comes out ahead.
