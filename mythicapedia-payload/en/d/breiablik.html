home of Baldr in Norse mythology

Breiðablik

Breiðablik is the home of the god Baldr in Norse mythology, where he lives with his wife Nanna. It is one of the most admired places in Asgard for its brightness and purity.

The myth

According to Norse tradition, Breiðablik was the seventh of the twelve main dwellings in Asgard, the home of the gods. Its most striking feature was a golden roof hanging from pillars of solid silver.

The hall was described as a long, empty yet pure expanse. The sources say that nothing evil, false or dark could pass through its gate, so the place embodied the light and beauty associated with Baldr.

Legacy

The name has been linked to Baldr's attributes of light and beauty. Scholars have compared its description with that of Heorot, the hall in the Old English poem Beowulf, since both are free of wickedness, and it has been proposed that both served as sanctuaries. In popular culture, it appears as a sacred weapon in the game Fire Emblem Heroes, and the name is also used by an Icelandic sports club from Kópavogur.

Ancient sources: The Eddic poem Grímnismál (stanza 12) and Snorri Sturluson's Gylfaginning in the Prose Edda.

Trivia

Meaning of the name. The name has been translated as something like 'broad gleam' or 'the far-shining one', alluding to the brightness of the place.
An Icelandic club. Breiðablik is also the name of a multi-sports club from Kópavogur, Iceland, which has won three men's football league titles.
