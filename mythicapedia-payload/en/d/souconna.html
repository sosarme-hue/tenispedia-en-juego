Celtic goddess of the river Saône

Souconna

Souconna is a Celtic goddess, the divinity of the river Saône as it flows past Chalon-sur-Saône. She matters because she was invoked in inscriptions, which lets us know about her local worship. Like other river deities of Gaul, she shows how Celtic peoples saw rivers as sacred beings.

The myth

Little myth survives about Souconna: no stories of her origin, family or deeds have come down to us. Her identity is understood mainly through her link to the river, whose divine personality she embodied and from which she takes her name.

Worship and religion

She was venerated as a local deity of the Saône, and invocations were addressed to her through inscriptions in the Chalon-sur-Saône area. These inscriptions are the main source for her cult. No details survive about temples, festivals or specific rites, and her worship is not known to continue today.

Ancient sources: She is known only from epigraphic inscriptions; no ancient literary source mentions her.
