Red Boy

Red Boy is a demon child from the 16th-century Chinese novel Journey to the West. Son of the Bull Demon King and Princess Iron Fan, he is a fierce boy who tries to devour the monk Tang Sanzang and who commands an inextinguishable fire called True Samādhi Fire.

The myth

In the novel, Red Boy, also called the Boy Sage Great King or Bull Boy Sage, is the son of the Bull Demon King and Princess Iron Fan, a rakshasa. Independent from childhood, he rules lesser demons and local deities in a land far from his parents.

When Tang Sanzang reaches his mountain, Red Boy kidnaps him to eat his flesh and extend his life. After several clashes with Sun Wukong, who nearly burns to death in his flames, the goddess Guanyin appears to subdue him. She binds his head, wrists and legs with golden bands that tighten when she recites her mantras, and he ends up submitting to her discipline.

Ancient sources: He appears in the 16th-century Chinese novel Journey to the West.
