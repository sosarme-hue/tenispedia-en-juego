hero of Greek mythology, first inhabitant of the Reitoi

Crocon

Crocon is a figure of Greek mythology linked to the region of the Reitoi and to the royal house of Eleusis through his marriage. He matters mainly because ancient authors give different versions of his family line.

The myth

According to Pausanias, Crocon was the first inhabitant of the Reitoi area, a place that in his day was still called the Palaces of Crocon. Athenian tradition linked him to Saesara, daughter of Celeus, although not everyone accepted this: only the people of the Demos of Skambonidai upheld it.

Apollodorus makes him the father of Meganira, who in turn had Apheidas and Elatus with Arcas. Yet the same author records other versions: according to Eumelus, the mother of these sons was instead the nymph Chrysopeleia.

Ancient sources: Pausanias mentions him as the first inhabitant of the Reitoi, and Apollodorus as the grandfather of Apheidas and Elatus through Meganira.

Trivia

A place named after him. Pausanias reports that in his time the Reitoi area was still called the Palaces of Crocon, although he could not find the hero's tomb.
A disputed marriage. Only the people of the Demos of Skambonidai maintained that Crocon had married Saesara, daughter of Celeus.
