Roman goddess of treachery and fraud

Fraus

Fraus is the personification of treachery, fraud and cunning in Roman mythology. She is linked to the night and to Mercury, the god of thieves, and she is the Roman counterpart of the Greek Apate.

The myth

Accounts of her parentage differ. Some name Night as her mother and Orcus as her father, while others make Erebus her father. She is said to serve Mercury, and at times Fraus is even given as another name for him or described as his helper.

One description pictures her with a woman's face, the body of a snake and the sting of a scorpion on her tail, a monstrous image that links her to the creatures of myth.

Legacy

Her name gave Spanish the word fraude, derived from the Latin term fraus.

Trivia

An inherited word. Her name lies behind the Spanish word fraude, which comes from Latin.
Mixed image. Her description combines a human face, a snake's body and a scorpion's tail.
