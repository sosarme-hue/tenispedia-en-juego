Glasya-Labolas

Glasya-Labolas is a demon of Western demonology, described as a great president of Hell. His fame comes from the grimoires, which credit him with powers over knowledge, love and violence.

The myth

According to the Ars Goetia, included in the Lesser Key of Solomon, Glasya-Labolas is a great president of Hell who commands thirty-six legions of demons. He is described with the form of a dog and the wings of a griffin. Tradition says he teaches the liberal arts, makes men invisible and foretells the future, yet he also incites bloodshed and is the author of all murders. He can likewise kindle love between friends and enemies.

In the Grand Grimoire he appears as Classyalabolas, a sort of sergeant who sometimes serves as a mount for Naberus. Other sources consider him an earl of Hell, and his name is spelled in many ways, such as Caasimolar or Glacialabolas.

Legacy

He appears in Collin de Plancy's Infernal Dictionary, which describes him as a great president of the infernal realms, and in several occult grimoires that recorded his figure.

Ancient sources: The Ars Goetia, included in the Lesser Key of Solomon, and the Grand Grimoire, both grimoires of the Western magical tradition.

Trivia

Many names. His name appears in many spellings, such as Caasimolar, Glacialabolas or Glasya Labolas, depending on the translations of the grimoires.
A demon's mount. The Grand Grimoire presents him as a kind of sergeant who sometimes serves as a mount for Naberus.
