the sea and earth beasts of the Book of Revelation

The Beast of Revelation

The Sea Beast and the Earth Beast are two creatures from the Book of Revelation, the last book of the Christian Bible, embodying a power hostile to God. Often linked to the Antichrist and the false prophet, they have fed centuries of end-times imagination.

The myth

According to Revelation, the Sea Beast rises out of the sea with seven heads and ten horns crowned with diadems, and names of blasphemy written on its heads. It resembles a leopard, with bear's feet and a lion's mouth, and the dragon gives it his power, his throne and great authority. One of its heads seems fatally wounded, but the wound heals, and the amazed people follow and worship it. It speaks arrogant words against God, makes war on the saints, and is given authority for forty-two months.

Then comes the Earth Beast, with two horns like a lamb and a voice like a dragon. It promotes the authority of the first beast, performs great signs, even making fire fall from heaven, and persuades the inhabitants of the earth to make an image of the Sea Beast. That image can speak and demands the death of anyone who refuses to worship it. The Earth Beast also requires everyone to receive a mark on the right hand or the forehead, needed to buy and sell.

In the end, Christ defeats both beasts, which are thrown into the lake of fire, while the dragon is imprisoned in the bottomless pit for a thousand years.

Legacy

From Revelation, the phrase the mark of the beast has passed most widely into common speech, naming the sign required for economic life. The two beasts have also remained a constant reference in interpretations of the end of time.

Ancient sources: The Book of Revelation of John of Patmos (chapters 11, 13, 17 and 19), drawing on the four beasts of the Book of Daniel (chapter 7).

Trivia

Seven heads, seven mountains. According to the text, the beast's seven heads stand for seven mountains and also for seven kings.
Three and a half years. It has been proposed that the forty-two months equal a time, times and half a time, that is, three and a half years.
