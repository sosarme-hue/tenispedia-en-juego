The Eight Immortals

The Eight Immortals (八仙, Bāxiān) are a group of legendary immortals in Chinese mythology, revered in Taoism and deeply present in popular culture. Each one stands for a trade or a social condition in Chinese life.

The myth

According to tradition, they were once mortal people who probably lived during the Tang or Song dynasties. They practised alchemy and other techniques to prolong life, until they reached true immortality through the Tao. They were first described in the Yuan dynasty.

Lü Dongbin is usually considered their leader. Zhongli Quan carries a fan and is linked to turning metal into gold and silver; Li Tieguai, with his iron crutch and gourd, heals the sick; and He Xiangu, the only woman in the most common version, holds a lotus flower.

In the anonymous Ming work The Eight Immortals Cross the Sea, they travel to the birthday feast of the goddess Xi Wangmu. At the shore, Lü Dongbin proposes crossing the sea not by clouds but through their own powers, which gave rise to the proverb 'each reveals his divine power'.

Worship and religion

They are worshipped in Taoism and in Chinese folk religion. In Xi'an, a Song-dynasty temple, the Eight Immortals Palace, houses their statues, and Taipei has the Muzha temple. Their veneration remains alive, and in some folk branches they are associated with the closeness of the deceased.

Legacy

Their image recurs in painting, sculpture and Song-era celadon vases, and later in Ming and Qing art. They inspired the Eight Piece Brocade exercises and drunken boxing styles, and appear in Jackie Chan's film Drunken Master and the video game Fear Effect 2.

Ancient sources: The Journey to the East of the Eight Immortals by Wu Yuantai (Ming dynasty), and the anonymous Ming work The Eight Immortals Cross the Sea.

Trivia

A hydrangea named after them. In Chinese, the hydrangea is called the 'flower of the Eight Immortals' (八仙花, Bāxiān huā).
The hidden Eight Immortals. Each immortal's power can be transferred to an object called An Baxian, 'the Covert Eight Immortals', able to give life or destroy evil.
