creator god of Haudenosaunee mythology

Taryenyawagon

Taryenyawagon, whose name is usually translated as "Holder of the Heavens", is the creator deity in Haudenosaunee mythology, the tradition of the Iroquois Confederacy. He is often regarded as synonymous with Hahgwehdiyu, so the two names frequently refer to the same divine being.

The myth

In Haudenosaunee mythology, Taryenyawagon appears as the creator. His name, "Holder of the Heavens", points to his role in relation to the sky and places his figure at the origin of the world the stories describe.

As with many figures from oral tradition, his identity is not fixed in a single form: different accounts and communities may name him differently, and his equivalence with Hahgwehdiyu is not uniform. The surviving sources about him are brief, so his figure is best read as a starting point rather than a full portrait.
