home of the god Ull in Norse mythology

Ydalir

Ydalir, meaning «yew dales», is the dwelling of the god Ull in Asgard according to the poem Grímnismál. It matters mainly because its name links Ull to the bow, a weapon tied to yew wood.

The myth

According to stanza 5 of Grímnismál, collected in the Poetic Edda, Ullr has a dwelling built in Ydalir. Odin, disguised as Grímnir, mentions it while speaking to the young Agnar. The same stanza recalls that the gods once gave Alfheim to Freyr as a tooth-gift, so the text places Ull's home among the realms of the gods of Asgard.

Ydalir appears in no other known story. It is known only from this single mention, so the myth surrounding it is very thin: nothing is told of what Ull did there or what his home looked like.

Legacy

The most widespread explanation links the name to the bow: yew wood was used for bows in the North, which is why Ull has been read as an archer god. Today the name survives mainly in popular culture. Ichaival, a legendary bow in the 1996 Super Famicom game Fire Emblem: Genealogy of the Holy War, probably derives from Ydalir, and the term reappeared in the 2012 anime Symphogear. The Icelandic heavy metal band Skálmöld released the album Ýdalir in 2023, inspired by Grímnismál.

Ancient sources: Attested solely in stanza 5 of Grímnismál, in the Poetic Edda.

Trivia

A bow with a borrowed name. The legendary bow Ichaival in the video game Fire Emblem: Genealogy of the Holy War probably takes its name from Ydalir. Ichii is the Japanese word for yew.
An error that spread. In 2013 «Ichaival» was added to an English Wikipedia list as a norse bow of Odin, when it was actually a Japanese phrase. The mistake later appeared in the video game Smite (2014).
