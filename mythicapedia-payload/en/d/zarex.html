hero of Greek mythology, son of Carystus

Zarex

Zarex (also Zarax) is a hero of Greek mythology, son of Carystus, credited with learning the music of Apollo and founding a town in Laconia. He appears mainly in local traditions and in the family line of Anius.

The myth

According to tradition, Zarex married Rhoeo and became the father of Anius and four other children. Ancient accounts differ on his ancestry: some make him a direct son of Carystus (Carycus), while others make him a son of Petraeus, who was in turn a son of Carystus.

In some versions, Zarex adopted his wife's son, Anius, who was raised by his divine father, Apollo. Zarex was also said to have learned Apollo's music and to have founded the town of Zarex in Laconia. A mountain on Euboea was thought to be named after him.

Worship and religion

He had a heroon at Eleusis, next to that of Hippothoon, which shows he was honoured as a hero there. The sources do not say whether this cult lasted.

Ancient sources: Pausanias wrote that there may have been another Zarex, an Athenian hero, but he said nothing more about him.

Trivia

A mountain with his name. A mountain on Euboea was thought to have been named after Zarex.
Neighbour of Hippothoon. His heroon at Eleusis stood next to that of Hippothoon, another local hero.
