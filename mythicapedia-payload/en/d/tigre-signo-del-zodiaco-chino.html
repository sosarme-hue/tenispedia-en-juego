Tiger (Chinese zodiac)

The Tiger is the third of the twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac, which follow a twelve-year cycle in the Chinese calendar. In Chinese astrology it stands for power, boldness and unpredictability, and it is linked to the Earthly Branch 寅.

The myth

The Tiger is an animal of the Chinese zodiac rather than a figure of its own myth. Astrological readings describe people born in its year as active, magnetic, competitive and brave, able to lead groups and to devote themselves fully when they love. They are also said to have an explosive temper, quick changes of mood and a tender heart toward family and friends.

Within the system, the Tiger is compatible with the Dog and the Horse, while the Monkey and the Snake are considered its rivals. These affinities belong to the astrological reading of the twelve-year cycle and its combination with the five elements.
