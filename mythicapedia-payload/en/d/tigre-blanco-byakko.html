guardian creature of the west in Chinese mythology

White Tiger (Byakko)

The White Tiger, or Báihǔ, is one of the Four Symbols of Chinese constellations, linked to the west, autumn, metal and the evening. It joins the image of an animal that heralds cold weather with a symbol of military power.

The myth

According to Chinese tradition, a tiger's fur turns white at five hundred years of age, and the White Tiger appears only when the emperor rules with perfect virtue or when there is peace in the world. Its roar is identified with the cold autumn winds, the season when hungry tigers, driven by the chill, come down to the inhabited plains, just as livestock is brought from mountain pastures into the stables.

In the Ming dynasty novel Journey to the West, attributed to Wu Cheng'en, the White Tiger appears as a demonic spirit. It takes the form of a woman in white who tries to deceive the monk Tang Sanzang, but the Monkey Walker sees through her, forces her to reveal her true nature and defeats her by making her vomit a swarm of old monkeys from her belly.

Worship and religion

During the Han dynasty there was an ancient custom of worshipping the White Tiger god on the 14th day of the first lunar month in Huiji, Zhejiang, at the Baihu temple, one of the four Weiyang temples. According to textual research, its cult already appears among the ancient Qiang Rong peoples. Today the Yi, Bai, Buyi and Tujia nationalities of China still regard it as their ancestor.

Legacy

In Chinese art the White Tiger often appears alongside the dragon, and in antiquity its image was associated with military banners and symbols. Its name also belongs to one of the four emblems of the Chinese constellations.

Ancient sources: It appears in the Ming dynasty novel Journey to the West, attributed to Wu Cheng'en.

Trivia

White coat. According to tradition, a tiger turns white at five hundred years old. White tigers do exist, but their colour comes from a genetic mutation.
Tigers in the novel. In Journey to the West, the White Tiger disguises itself as a woman in white to deceive Tang Sanzang.
