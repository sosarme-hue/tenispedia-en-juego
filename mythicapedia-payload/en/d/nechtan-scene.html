character of the Irish Ulster Cycle

Nechtan Scéne

Nechtan Scéne is a figure of Irish Celtic tradition who appears in the Ulster Cycle, the body of medieval Irish heroic legends. The sources available describe his role only briefly, so he is best understood within that wider narrative world.

The myth

The Ulster Cycle sets its tales in what is now eastern Ulster and northern Leinster, at the court of King Conchobar mac Nessa in Emain Macha. Its stories revolve around the hero Cú Chulainn and the conflict with the Connachta and Queen Medb, whose best-known tale is the epic Táin Bó Cúailnge, or Cattle Raid of Cooley. Mostly told in prose, the tales mix violence, humour and supernatural elements, portraying a pagan, pastoral society of warrior aristocrats who fought over cattle and single combats at fords.

As for Nechtan Scéne himself, the available sources do not detail his lineage, deeds or fate, so his story cannot be reconstructed with confidence. What is clear is that he belongs to a world where characters are bound by alliances, fosterage and religious taboos known as geasa.
