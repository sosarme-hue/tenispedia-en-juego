Roman god of fertility and newborns

Picumnus

Picumnus is a god of Roman mythology, brother of the god Pilumnus, and is generally regarded as an agricultural deity linked to fertility. He is also associated with newborn babies and marriage, making him a figure of domestic life and the cycles of the land.

The myth

Surviving information about Picumnus is scarce. Tradition presents him as the brother of Pilumnus, another god, and both brothers may have been agricultural deities.

The spheres attributed to them are broad: the fertility of the fields, the care of small children and the bonds of marriage. This mix of roles was common in Roman religion, where many deities covered both the work of the land and family life.

The texts gathered here do not preserve detailed stories about his deeds, his parents or his end, so any fuller picture of him is a reconstruction by modern scholars.
