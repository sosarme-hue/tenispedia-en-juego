ghost whale yōkai of Japanese folklore

Bake-kujira

The bake-kujira, or "ghost whale", is a yōkai from Japanese folklore linked to the coast of the Shimane Peninsula in western Japan. It is usually seen as the vengeful spirit of a whale, bearing a grudge against the people who hunted its kind.

The myth

In the popular legend, a group of fishermen set out on a rainy evening when a large white shape drifted toward the coast from offshore. Taking it for an easy catch, they hurled their harpoons, but none of them left a mark. As they drew near, they saw that the figure was nothing but the white skeleton of a whale, with no skin or flesh.

Strange fish and odd-looking birds gathered around the creature. When the tide went out, they all retreated far to sea without harming anyone. From then on, the fishermen said the apparition was the ghost of a dead whale seeking revenge, and it was never seen again.

Legacy

The bake-kujira appears in the manga and anime Gegege no Kitarō, where it helps Kitarō and his friends as a holy and virtuous being, and in the manga Thousand Yōkai Tales. The manga artist Shigeru Mizuki, a master of modern yōkai imagery, later recalled that he fell ill with a high fever while drawing a kamishibai about this creature. It is also a playable character in the video game Smite.

Trivia

A feverish artist. Shigeru Mizuki said he fell ill with a high fever while working on a kamishibai about the bake-kujira, and later wondered whether the creature had possessed him.
