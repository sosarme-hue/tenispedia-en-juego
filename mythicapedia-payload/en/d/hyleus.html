Greek hunter killed in the Calydonian boar hunt

Hyleus

Hyleus is a hunter from Greek mythology who took part in the Calydonian boar hunt. His story is brief: he was killed by the animal along with Ancaeus, while the rest of the hunters managed to bring it down.

The myth

In the tale, Hyleus is one of the heroes gathered to hunt the enormous Calydonian boar, a beast that was ravaging the region. As the hunters surrounded the animal and eventually slew it, luck did not favour Hyleus, who lost his life to the boar. Ancaeus met the same fate, so both men are counted among the casualties of the expedition.

The episode is told very briefly, and Hyleus's fate mainly serves to underline the danger of the enterprise: even a large company of heroes could pay a heavy price for facing a creature like this. His story stands as a reminder of how risky that hunt was.
