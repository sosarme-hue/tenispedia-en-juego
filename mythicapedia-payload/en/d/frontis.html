hero of Greek mythology, son of Phrixus

Phrontis

Phrontis is one of the sons of Phrixus and Chalciope, grandsons of King Aeetes of Colchis. His story belongs to the cycle of the Argonauts and the theft of the Golden Fleece.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Phrontis and his brothers Cytissorus, Argus and Melas were raised in Colchis. After their father died, they set out to avenge the ill treatment Phrixus had suffered at the hands of King Athamas of Orchomenus. Their journey left them stranded on the Island of Ares, in the Black Sea, also called Dia, until Jason and the Argonauts rescued them.

When Jason learned that they were grandsons of Aeetes, he persuaded them to return with him to Colchis and help him win the Golden Fleece. He also questioned them about the layout of the land and its defences. Once the Fleece was taken, they sailed back to Greece with the crew of the Argo.

Trivia

Number of brothers. Some accounts add a fifth brother, Presbon, so the number of Phrixus's sons varies from version to version.
