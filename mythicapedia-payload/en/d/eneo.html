king of Calydon, father of Meleager

Oeneus

Oeneus is a legendary king of Calydon in Aetolia and the father of Meleager and Deianeira. Greek tradition remembers him as the first to receive a grapevine from Dionysos, which links him to wine.

The myth

Son of Porthaon and Euryte, Oeneus first married Althaea, with whom he had Meleager and Deianeira, later the wife of Heracles. According to the story, Dionysos rewarded his hospitality with the first vine, and tradition claims the Greek word for wine, oinos, came from his name, though this is a later folk explanation rather than a true etymology.

When Oeneus forgot to honour Artemis during the harvest sacrifices, the goddess sent a huge boar that ravaged Calydon. Meleager gathered the greatest heroes of Greece to hunt it, and after the beast fell, a quarrel over its hide led him to kill his maternal uncles. Althaea then brought about her son's death and took her own life.

Oeneus later married Periboea, with whom he had Tydeus. In old age he was deposed by the sons of his brother Agrius, and his grandson Diomedes restored him to the throne. In another version, the surviving sons of Agrius ambushed him near the altar of Telephus in Arcadia, and after his death he was buried in Argolis, where a town was named Oenoe in his honour.

Legacy

His name survives mainly in the Argive town of Oenoe, which tradition links to him. Because of his connection with wine, he is often associated with the history of the vine in ancient Greece.

Ancient sources: Homer, in the Iliad (Book VI), recalls that Oeneus once hosted the hero Bellerophon in his household.

Trivia

A king who lends his name to wine. Tradition links him to the Greek word for wine, oinos, although that is not its real origin. Other accounts say the grape or its juice was named after his servants instead.
A hunt for heroes. The hunt for the Calydonian boar gathered heroes from across Greece, and many of them lost their lives during the mission.
Two characters named Oeneus. Another Oeneus, son of Apollo, was the founder of the city of Eno, today Enez, and father of Cyzicus.
