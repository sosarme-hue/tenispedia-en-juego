supreme god of the Akan people

Nyame

Nyame is the supreme god of the Akan people of Ghana, creator of the universe, life and death. His name means roughly 'the one who knows and sees everything.'

The myth

According to Akan tradition, Odomankoma, the creator, shaped the universe and also made life and death. Yet Owuo, the death he had created, ended up killing him.

The story continues: life woke him, and he was reborn as Nyankopon, his successor. After a struggle with Owuo, Nyankopon forced Death to drink its own poison, though Death still causes mortals to die.

Worship and religion

He is revered as the supreme being and creator. The Akan associate him with the vulture and the spider, valued for their wisdom.

Legacy

The Adinkra symbol Gye Nyame ('I fear none except God') takes its name from him, and Nyame is the Twi word for God.

Trivia

Adinkra symbol. The Gye Nyame symbol remains very present in today's Akan culture, although its meaning changed under the influence of Christianity.
Drum of death. According to tradition, Owuo speaks through a drum, which has a drummer named Odomankoma Kyerema.
