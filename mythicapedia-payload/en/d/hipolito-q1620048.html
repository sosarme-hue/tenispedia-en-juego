Hippolytos

Hippolytos is one of the giants of Greek mythology, a son of Uranus and Gaia. He matters mainly because of the Gigantomachy, the war between the giants and the gods, in which he was killed by Hermes.

The myth

According to tradition, Hippolytos was one of the sons of Uranus and Gaia, that is, one of the giants born from the union of the first divine couple. His name appears in the list of giants given by Apollodorus in his Library, where the name is recorded without going into lengthy stories about him.

His main episode is the Gigantomachy, the battle in which the giants fought the gods for power over the world. During the fight, Hermes wore the helmet of Hades, which protected him, and he killed Hippolytos.

Ancient sources: Apollodorus's Library lists him among the sons of Uranus and Gaia.
