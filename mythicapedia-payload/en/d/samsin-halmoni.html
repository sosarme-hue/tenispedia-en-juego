Korean goddess of childbirth and fate

Samsin Halmoni

Samsin Halmoni, the 'Grandmother Samsin', is the goddess of childbirth and fate in Korean mythology. Her name is linked to the number three, and she is venerated as a wise ancestral crone who protects mothers and newborns.

The myth

In oral tradition, Samsin Halmoni was the daughter of the virgin sky goddess Tanggum Aeggi, who became the first mudang shaman. After descending to Earth, she gave birth to the Samsin in a cave.

When Buddhism arrived, the story changed: Tanggum Aeggi bore three sons, who became Buddhist heavenly gods. Samsin Halmoni then created the first humans and became the mother and ancestor of all people.

Worship and religion

Every village and household had its own Samsin. Offerings of rice, soy sauce and wine were made at births and birthdays, and her sacred corner in the warm part of the anbang still hosts prayers today. During childbirth, the room was sealed with ropes to contain her power.

Legacy

She appears in Korean popular culture: Lee El played her in the TV drama Goblin (2016-2017), and Kim Soo-Mi in a cameo in Ohlala Couple.

Trivia

A mountain named for her. Samsinbong, known as the Peak of Three Spirits, takes its name from her.
A lucky rope. After birth, a rope was hung outside the house to mark the happy event of 'opening what has been tied' and to ward off evil spirits.
