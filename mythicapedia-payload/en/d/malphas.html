Great President of Hell in demonology

Malphas

Malphas is a demon from early modern demonology, described as a Great President of Hell and second in command under Satan. He appears in Renaissance and nineteenth-century grimoires, so his origin is late and unconnected to ancient mythologies.

The myth

According to Johann Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, published in 1577, and the Lesser Key of Solomon, Malphas commands forty legions of demons. These texts credit him with mastery over building: he raises houses, towers and strongholds, and throws down the buildings of his enemies. He can also expose or destroy an adversary's thoughts and desires, supply good familiars, and quickly gather artificers from across the world.

He appears as a raven, though on request he can take the form of a man with a hoarse voice. The accounts warn that he gladly accepts any sacrifice offered to him, but then deceives the conjurer.

Legacy

He appears as a summonable demon in the video game Bayonetta (2009), and also in Devil May Cry 5 (2019). In the series The Owl House he is the Master Librarian of Hexside school, and in the Korean film The Priests (2015) he is revealed during an exorcism.

Ancient sources: Johann Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum (1577) and the Lesser Key of Solomon, both demonological texts written long after the Middle Ages.

Trivia

A raven in the grimoires. In demonological texts his usual form is that of a raven, though he can take human form if the conjurer asks for it.
A demon in horror film. In the film The Priests he appears as an ancient demon during the exorcism of a young girl.
