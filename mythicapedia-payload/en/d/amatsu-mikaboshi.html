god of stars in Japanese mythology

Amatsu-Mikaboshi

Amatsu-Mikaboshi is a star god of Japanese mythology, mentioned mainly in the Nihon Shoki. He matters because he is almost the only star kami who appears as an opponent of the amatsukami.

The myth

The Nihon Shoki tells him in two versions. In the first, during the conquest of the Central Land of Reed-Plains, he is a rebellious kunitsukami whom Takemikazuchi and Futsunushi subdue at the command of the heavenly deities.

In the second, he is an amatsukami whom Takemikazuchi and Futsunushi slay before descending to earth. The Kojiki does not mention him.

Worship and religion

At a shrine in Hitachi, in Ibaraki Prefecture, he is venerated as a jinushigami alongside Takehazuchi-no-Mikoto, and a spirit-housing stone within the grounds is believed to hold his ara-mitama. Star shrines dedicated to him can also be found across Japan.

Legacy

The kokugaku scholar Hirata Atsutane identified him with the planet Venus. In popular culture he appears as the Chaos King in Marvel comics, and he gives his name to a sorcery clan in the manga Kagurabachi.

Ancient sources: The Nihon Shoki gives him two brief passages; the Kojiki does not name him.

Trivia

Venus. Hirata Atsutane linked this god to the planet Venus, based on his reading of one part of the name.
A comic book villain. Marvel Comics made him the Chaos King, a fictional character unrelated to Japanese worship.
