daughter of Helios and mother of Alcyone

Aegiale

Aegiale is a figure from Greek mythology, daughter of Helios and Clymene, best known as the mother of Alcyone. Her role is minor, but she shows how ancient writers linked divine family trees to those of human and semi-divine heroes.

The myth

According to tradition, Aegiale was a daughter of Helios, the sun god, and Clymene, and a sister of Phaethon. Her story is known mainly from the version preserved by the mythographer Hyginus: from her union with Aeolus, lord of the winds, Alcyone was born.

Other accounts give that motherhood to Enarete instead, so the tradition is not unanimous on this point. As with many minor figures in Greek myth, her name mostly serves to connect a divine household with a character who has far more recorded episodes.

Ancient sources: The mythographer Hyginus names her as the mother of Alcyone by Aeolus, while other ancient accounts attribute that motherhood to Enarete.
