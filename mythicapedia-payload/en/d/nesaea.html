Nereid of Greek mythology

Nesaea

Nesaea is one of the Nereids, the fifty sea-nymph daughters of the Old Man of the Sea, Nereus, and the Oceanid Doris, in Greek mythology. Her name, from the Greek Nêsaiê, means "the dweller on islands", and she is known as the "white" Nereid of the islands.

The myth

According to the tradition, Nesaea joined the other Nereids who gathered around Thetis in her grief when Achilles lost Patroclus. In some accounts, Nesaea travelled in the train of Cyrene together with her sisters Thalia, Cymodoce and Spio.

Later, these four and their sisters Thetis, Melite and Panopea helped the hero Aeneas and his crew during a storm at sea.

Legacy

Her name is used to describe a genus of plants in the family Lythraceae.

Trivia

A name with meaning. The name Nesaea comes from Greek and means "the dweller on islands", a reference to her role as the Nereid of the islands.
Helping Aeneas. Together with her sisters, Nesaea came to the aid of the hero Aeneas and his companions when a storm threatened their voyage.
