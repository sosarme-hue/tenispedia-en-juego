wife of Menoetius and mother of Patroclus

Philomela

In Greek myth, Philomela is the wife of Menoetius and the mother of Patroclus, who fought in the Trojan War. She matters mainly because she appears in the family line of one of that war's best-known participants.

The myth

According to tradition, Philomela was married to Menoetius, one of the Argonauts who sailed with Jason. That membership places her household within the story of the expedition for the Golden Fleece, a voyage Greek tradition presents as a quest led by Jason. Their son Patroclus later took part in the Trojan War.

The sources do not agree on who his mother was. The Bibliotheca lists three other wives of Menoetius who could also have been Patroclus's mother: Periopis, Polymele and Sthenele. Philomela therefore appears as one candidate among several in the genealogy of this hero.

Ancient sources: The Bibliotheca, which lists other wives of Menoetius as possible mothers of Patroclus.

Trivia

Several possible mothers. The Bibliotheca proposes three other wives of Menoetius as possible mothers of Patroclus, showing that ancient genealogies were not uniform.
