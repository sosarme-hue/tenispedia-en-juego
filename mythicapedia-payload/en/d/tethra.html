Irish god of darkness and death

Tethra

Tethra is, in Irish mythology, the king of the Fomorians and a figure linked to primordial darkness and the realm of the dead. His story shows how the power of the ancient dark beings gives way to the new peoples who come to rule Ireland.

The myth

According to Irish tradition, Tethra ruled the Fomorians, a people of dark beings who fought the Tuatha Dé Danann, the people who went on to rule the island until the arrival of the sons of Míl. He is described as having an unattractive face, as if he had been shaped with a red-hot iron, and burning eyes.

He was king of the Fomorians before Balor and lost the battles of Mag Tuired. After dying in the second, the tale says he ruled Mag Mell, the land of the dead, and he left Ireland with his army of demons. After the battle, his sword, Orna, was taken by Ogma, and according to the story it then recounted everything it had done.

Legacy

His name has reached popular culture: it is the name of an armour set for the Highlander character in the video game For Honor, and also that of an Italian melancholic doom death metal band, active since 2008. The band Ogives Big Band released a song called 'Tethra' in 2019, with a music video.

Trivia

Possible origin of the name. It has been proposed that Tethra derives from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning 'quacking sound'. In Old Irish the word means both sea and the scald-crow Badb.
The sword Orna. After the battle, his sword Orna passed to Ogma, and according to the story the sword went on to recount everything it had done.
