Messenian nymph, nurse of Zeus

Ithome

Ithome is a Messenian nymph of Greek mythology and one of the nurses of the infant Zeus. Her story ties her to Messenia and to the landscape that still bears her name.

The myth

According to the myth, the newborn Zeus was in danger from his father, so the Curetes carried him off to keep him safe. It was then that Ithome and Neda took him in, bathed him and raised him through his infancy.

The two nymphs appear together in the story as a pair of caretakers, and what is told of Ithome goes no further than her role as a nurse.

Legacy

Ithome gave her name to Mount Ithome, and Neda is linked to the river of the same name, so both landmarks keep the memory of the two nurses alive.

Trivia

Mountain and river. According to the story, Mount Ithome and the Neda river take their names from these two nymphs.
