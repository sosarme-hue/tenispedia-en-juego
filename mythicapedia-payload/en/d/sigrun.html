Norse valkyrie

Sigrún

Sigrún is a valkyrie in Norse mythology whose name means "victory rune" in Old Norse. She is the central figure, alongside the hero Helgi Hundingsbane, of one of the best-known tales of love and revenge in the Poetic Edda.

The myth

According to the story, Sigrún was leading a band of nine valkyries when she met Helgi, and the two fell in love. Her father Högni had promised her to Höðbroddr, son of King Granmarr, so Helgi invaded Granmarr's kingdom and killed anyone who opposed their union. He spared only Sigrún's brother Dagr, on condition that he swear loyalty to Helgi.

Bound by honour to avenge his brothers, Dagr killed Helgi at a place called Fjoturlund, after receiving a spear from Odin. Sigrún cursed him to live in the woods feeding on carrion. Helgi returned from Valhalla one last night to be with her, and Sigrún died of sorrow and was reborn as a valkyrie. In her next life she was Kára, and Helgi was Helgi Haddingjaskati.

Legacy

Sigrún appears as the Valkyrie Queen in the video game God of War (2018) and in God of War Ragnarök (2022), where she is an ally of Kratos and one of the series' most demanding fights. She also appears in the Dresden Files novel series, where she is known as Gard, and in the 2024 verse play Sigrun & the Mist.

Ancient sources: Her story is told in the poems Helgakviða Hundingsbana I and II, in the Poetic Edda.

Trivia

A name with a rune. Her name in Old Norse means "victory rune", a natural link to her role as a valkyrie.
A reborn life. The original editor of the poem notes that Sigrún was Sváfa reborn, placing her in a chain of successive lives.
