valkyrie of Norse mythology

Svipul

Svipul is a valkyrie in Norse mythology whose old name means "changeable." She appears in several lists of valkyries, placing her among the female beings who, in these traditions, decided the fate of warriors in battle.

The myth

Our sources mention her mainly in lists of valkyries. Her name appears in the poem Darraðarljóð and in the longer of the Nafnaþulur lists, included in the book Skáldskaparmál of the Poetic Edda.

In addition, in Skáldskaparmál the name Svipul serves as a synonym for "battle," linking this valkyrie to the battlefield. According to the scholar Rudolf Simek, her name probably refers to the changeable nature of fate, which the valkyries direct, especially in Darraðarljóð.

No extended story about her survives. She is known mainly through her name and through the collective role of the valkyries, which makes her a quiet presence within Norse mythology.

Ancient sources: Darraðarljóð and the valkyrie lists of the Poetic Edda (Skáldskaparmál), where she also appears as a synonym for battle.

Trivia

A synonym for battle. In Skáldskaparmál, her name is used as a synonym for battle, an unusual use for a personal name.
