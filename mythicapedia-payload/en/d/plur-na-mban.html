heroine of Irish mythology

Plúr na mBan

Plúr na mBan, whose Irish name means "the flower of women", is a figure of Irish mythology. She is the daughter of Oisín and Niamh, and her story is tied to the family of one of the great poet-heroes of the Fenian cycle.

The myth

In Irish tradition, Plúr na mBan is the daughter of Oisín, a warrior and poet of the Fenian cycle, and of Niamh, the woman from the otherworldly land of Tír na nÓg with whom he departed, according to the legend. Her name, pronounced roughly "ploor-nu-mon", presents her as a figure held in high regard within the family story.

Though the texts that mention her are brief, her family ties place her at the heart of the Fenian tradition. As with many figures of this oral tradition, her story was passed on mainly by word of mouth.
