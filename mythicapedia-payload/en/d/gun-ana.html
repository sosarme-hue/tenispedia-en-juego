solar goddess of the Turkic peoples

Gun Ana

Gun Ana is the common solar deity of the Turkic peoples, treated as a goddess in Kazakh and Kyrgyz mythology. Her name means 'Sun Mother', and she is one of the most powerful deities in her tradition, linked to life, fertility, warmth and health.

The myth

According to tradition, Gun Ana lives on the seventh floor of the sky. She is the goddess of life, fertility, warmth and health, and the patron of the unfortunate, especially orphans.

In the story of creation, Tengri made the Earth from rays of sunlight, and Gun Ana took part in this act. The rays are seen as ties linking the Sun with the spirits of plants, animals and humans. She is depicted with bright yellow skin.

Worship and religion

Worshippers turn toward the sunrise when they pray.

Legacy

In Turkish and Kyrgyz, 'gün' is also the everyday word for 'sun', derived from the Proto-Turkic 'kün'.

Trivia

Sun Mother. Her name means 'Sun Mother' in Turkic languages, and in some sources she also appears as Yaşık Ana.
A word for the sun. The noun 'gün' means the sun in Turkish and Kyrgyz, and goes back to the Proto-Turkic name 'kün'.
