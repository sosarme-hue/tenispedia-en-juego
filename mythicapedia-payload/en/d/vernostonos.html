Celtic god associated with alder trees

Vernostonos

Vernostonos was a god of ancient Celtic religion worshipped in Roman Britain, and his name seems to be linked to alder trees. He is known mainly from altar stones, and on the one at Ebchester he appears alongside Cocidius, the war god.

The myth

No literary stories about Vernostonos survive, so we know very little about his origins, family or deeds. What we know comes from inscriptions dating to the Roman period.

One proposed etymology derives his name from Proto-Celtic *Werno-stonos, read as 'the groaning of alder trunks'. This is a scholarly hypothesis rather than a detail transmitted by tradition.

Worship and religion

Stone altars were dedicated to him in Britain, such as the one at Ebchester in County Durham (RIB 1102). In that dedication he appears together with Cocidius, which suggests he may have been linked to that god or was an epithet of him. There is no evidence that his cult survives today.

Ancient sources: Latin inscription on the Ebchester altar (RIB 1102), from the Roman period.
