Trojan hero in the Iliad

Iamenus

Iamenus is a Trojan hero who appears in Homer's Iliad, fighting on the Trojan side. His presence in the poem is brief and tied to a single episode of the war.

The myth

According to the Homeric account, Iamenus fought alongside Asius during the Trojan attack on the Greek camp. Both were part of the force trying to break through the Achaean defences, during one of the fiercest phases of the fighting.

Leonteus, a warrior on the Greek side, killed them in that battle. The text gives no further details about their lives or families, so their role is limited to this scene of the Trojan War, told as one more moment in the clash between the two armies. The tradition remembers them as two among many warriors whose deaths show the intensity of the fighting.

Ancient sources: Homer's Iliad.
