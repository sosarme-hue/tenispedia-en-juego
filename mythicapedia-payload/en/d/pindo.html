hero of Greek mythology, son of Macedon

Pindus

Pindus is a hero of Greek mythology, son of Macedon according to tradition. His story, known from only a few versions, is a tale of friendship, betrayal and revenge involving a snake.

The myth

According to the tradition, Pindus had an unusual friendship with a snake. His three brothers killed him and, moved by loyalty to their companion, the snake took revenge by killing all three.

The story is brief and simple. The available sources give no details about the motive for the crime or the place where it happened, and the known version describes him only in relation to his friendship with the snake and his brothers, with no further episodes from his life. For that reason the tale is best read as a story of loyalty, punishment and the bonds between brothers and an animal.
