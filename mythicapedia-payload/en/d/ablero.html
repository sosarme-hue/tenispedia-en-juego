Trojan soldier of Greek mythology

Ablerus

Ablerus is a Trojan soldier in Greek mythology who is killed by Antilochus, son of Nestor, with his lance during the Trojan War. He appears only briefly in the Homeric epic, so his role amounts to that single moment in the fighting.

The myth

According to the Iliad, Ablerus was fighting on the Trojan side when Antilochus, the son of Nestor, struck him with his spear and killed him. The poem mentions him in only a few lines, without describing his lineage, his character or the circumstances leading up to the fight. His death is one of the many casualties Homer lists across the course of the war, one of the many minor episodes that fill the story of the conflict.

Outside that passage, no other ancient references to this figure are known, so his place in the Greek tradition is limited to that single moment of combat.

Ancient sources: Homer's Iliad mentions him briefly as a victim of Antilochus during the Trojan War.
