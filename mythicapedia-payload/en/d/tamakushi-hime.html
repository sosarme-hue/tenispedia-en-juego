Japanese goddess of Kamigamo Shrine

Tamakushi-hime

Tamakushi-hime is a goddess of Japanese mythology, mother of Himetataraisuzu-hime, whom tradition regards as Japan's first empress. She is also the deity of Kamigamo Shrine.

The myth

According to tradition, Tamakushi-hime, also called Mishimanomizokui-hime or Seyadatarahime, is the daughter of Kamo Taketsunumi. She is presented as the wife of several deities: Kotoshironushi, Yakusanoikazuchi and Ōmononushi.

These unions produced several children, among them Himetataraisuzu-hime, mother of the first empress, and Kamo no Okimi, a distant ancestor of the Miwa clan. Other children named include Isuzu-yori Hime-no-mikoto and Kamo Wakeikazuchi.

In this way, her lineage links the imperial family with the Miwa and Kamo clans.

Trivia

Several names. She is known by several names, such as Mishimanomizokui-hime, Seyadatarahime, Katsutamayori-biyorihime or Kimikahihime.
A line of clans. Her descendants are linked to the Miwa and Kamo clans, two groups tied to the shrines associated with her cult.
