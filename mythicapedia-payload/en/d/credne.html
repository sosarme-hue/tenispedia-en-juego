goldsmith of the Tuatha Dé Danann

Credne

Credne is the goldsmith of the Tuatha Dé Danann, the divine people of Irish mythology. He worked gold, bronze and brass, and belonged to the Trí Dée Dána, the three gods of craft alongside his brothers Goibniu and Luchtaine.

The myth

According to Irish tradition, Credne and his brothers forged the weapons the Tuatha Dé Danann used in their battles against the Fomorians. Credne is also credited with fashioning King Nuada's silver hand, together with Dian Cecht.

A poem attached to the Lebor Gabála Érenn describes his death: Credne, the pleasant craftsman, drowned in the lake-sea, the sinister pool, while bringing noble gold treasures to Ireland from Spain.

Ancient sources: The Lebor Gabála Érenn preserves the poem about his death.
