Oceanid of Greek mythology

Iache

Iache is one of the Oceanids of Greek mythology, water nymphs. She is one of the three thousand daughters of the Titans Oceanus and Tethys, and her name is remembered mainly for her role as a companion of Persephone when Hades abducted her, an central episode in the myths of Demeter.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Iache was one of the three thousand Oceanids, water nymphs born to the Titans Oceanus and his sister and spouse Tethys. Like her sisters, she belonged to a large family whose members were spread across the waters of the world.

Her name is linked to the abduction of Persephone. When Hades carried off the daughter of Demeter, Iache was among the companions who were with the young goddess, and she was present at the moment of the disappearance. The story gives her no deeds of her own beyond that episode.
