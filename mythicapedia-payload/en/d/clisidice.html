Eleusinian princess, daughter of Celeus

Cleisidice

Cleisidice is, in Greek mythology, a princess of Eleusis, daughter of King Celeus and Metaneira. Her story is tied to the arrival of Demeter at the royal house while she searched for her daughter Persephone.

The myth

According to the Homeric Hymn to Demeter, Cleisidice was one of Celeus's daughters, along with her sisters Demo, Callidice and Callithoe. The four were at the virgin well Callichoros in Eleusis when they saw an old woman resting there. It was Demeter, wandering in disguise in search of Persephone, who had been abducted by Hades.

The girls invited her to come with them to their father's house, where they welcomed her as a guest. Her name is traditionally linked to the Greek root kleis (key, bolt) and to dike (justice), though this is an interpretive association rather than an explicit account.

Ancient sources: She appears in the Homeric Hymn to Demeter, which recounts the search for Persephone and the goddess's stay in Eleusis.
