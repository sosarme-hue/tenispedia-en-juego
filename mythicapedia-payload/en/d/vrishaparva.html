king of the Danavas in Hindu mythology

Vrishaparva

Vrishaparva is the king of the Danavas, demons of Hindu mythology and enemies of the gods. He matters chiefly as the father of Sharmishtha, whose story becomes entangled with that of King Yayati and the rivalry with Devayani.

The myth

In Hindu tradition, Vrishaparva rules the Danavas, a people of asura lineage who fight against the devas. Accounts present him as a protector of his people and an ally of the sage Shukra, the teacher of the asuras.

His daughter Sharmishtha appears in the Mahabharata and the Puranas as an asura princess. She clashed with Devayani, Shukra's daughter, and the quarrel between them had consequences for both families. Sharmishtha later married King Yayati, an episode that angered Devayani and led her to ask her father for punishment.

Ancient sources: The Mahabharata mentions Vrishaparva in its episodes on Yayati and the asuras, and the Puranas preserve details of his lineage.
