Amanozako

Amanozako is a monstrous goddess of Japanese folklore, known for her furious and rebellious temper. She is regarded as the ancestor of several yōkai with an unruly nature, such as the tengu and the amanojaku.

The myth

According to tradition, she was born when Susanoo, god of storms, let his furious spirit (his ara-mitama) build up inside him until he vomited her out. She has no mother and, as the story goes, she bore her only child, Amanosaku, alone.

She has a beastly head, a long nose and long ears, and fangs strong enough to tear through steel blades. She can fly a thousand li and eats with tremendous voracity. She is capricious: if she does not get what she wants, her rage cannot be stopped. She is said to enjoy possessing human hearts to manipulate their emotions.

Her son Amanosaku drove the eight million gods of heaven mad, and for this he was made ruler of all malicious and troublesome kami.

Legacy

Toriyama Sekien drew her in the third volume of his Konjaku Gazu Zoku Hyakki. In the video game Shin Megami Tensei V (2021) she appears as a small, cute, fairy-like creature who accompanies the protagonist.

Ancient sources: The Wakan Sansai Zue, a Japanese encyclopedia from the Edo period, describes her in detail.

Trivia

Classic illustration. Toriyama Sekien drew her in the third volume of his Konjaku Gazu Zoku Hyakki.
Fairy in video games. In Shin Megami Tensei V (2021) she looks small and cute, very different from her monstrous appearance in older sources.
A mother without help. According to the story, she gave birth to Amanosaku without anyone's help.
