wife of Polybus, queen of Corinth in Greek myth

Periboea

Periboea is, in some versions of Greek myth, the wife of Polybus, king of Corinth, and the adoptive mother of Oedipus. She matters mainly as the woman who raises the child who will later fulfil the prophecy about his fate.

The myth

In one version of the myth, Polybus and Periboea had no children. When the shepherds of the court found an abandoned boy in the forest and brought him to the royal household, the couple took him in and raised him as their own.

The tradition does not agree on the name of Polybus's wife. According to Hyginus she was called Medusa, and according to Sophocles, Merope. Modern scholars do not know whether the three versions refer to the same woman.

Ancient sources: Hyginus and Sophocles give different names to Polybus's wife, and both mention her as the adoptive mother of Oedipus.
