valkyrie of Norse mythology

Mist

Mist is a valkyrie in Norse mythology, one of the warrior maidens who, according to the sources, served Odin. Her name appears in valkyrie lists, but almost nothing else is known about her.

The myth

Mist appears only in lists of valkyries. She is named in the poem Grímnismál in the Poetic Edda and in both valkyrie lists of the Nafnaþulur, catalogues of names of these beings. None of these sources gives her deeds, family or specific episodes, so her story amounts to her presence in the catalogue.

Her name is the most informative detail. According to Rudolf Simek, it is probably related to Old Norse mistr, meaning cloud or mist. For this scholar, the name recalls the way valkyries ride through the air and over water, as described in the poems Helgakviða Hjörvarðssonar and Helgakviða Hundingsbana II. This is a modern interpretation, which suggests a link to the skies and to haze, but the ancient texts do not explain it.

Ancient sources: She appears in the poem Grímnismál in the Poetic Edda and in both valkyrie lists of the Nafnaþulur.

Trivia
