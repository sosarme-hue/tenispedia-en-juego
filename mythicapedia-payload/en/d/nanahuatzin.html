Aztec god of humility

Nanahuatzin

Nanahuatzin is the Aztec god of humility, whose name means «full of sores». According to Mexica tradition, he willingly threw himself into a fire to become the Sun.

The myth

In the myth of the Fifth Sun, recorded by Bernardino de Sahagún, the gods gathered at Teotihuacán to decide who would light a new world. The proud Tecciztecatl volunteered first, but nobody wanted to join him until the gods noticed Nanahuatzin, a quiet god covered in sores. He accepted with humility.

For four days both gods did penance. Tecciztecatl offered quetzal feathers, coral and precious stones; Nanahuatzin offered green reeds, balls of straw and maguey spines soaked in his own blood. At midnight, beside a great bonfire, Tecciztecatl tried to leap four times and drew back each time. Nanahuatzin closed his eyes and jumped into the flames; seeing his courage, Tecciztecatl threw himself in after him.

From the east rose the Sun, Tonatiuh, followed by the Moon, almost as bright. To dim it, one of the gods threw a rabbit into Tecciztecatl's face, and the mark remains. The Sun would not move until Ehecatl, the wind, blew hard after the other gods had sacrificed themselves.

Worship and religion

The sources describe penance and blood sacrifice as a model of conduct: according to the myth, humans were left in debt to the gods and had to offer their own blood and that of others. In the story, this principle gave rise to wars aimed at capturing victims for the Sun. The sources do not describe a specific cult of Nanahuatzin.

Legacy

Tonatiuh, the name by which the Sun is known in this tradition, and the rabbit mark on the Moon are motifs the myth still preserves today. In the Codex Borgia he appears as a man emerging from fire, and he is considered probably an aspect of the god Xolotl.

Ancient sources: The Fifth Sun accounts recorded by Bernardino de Sahagún, and the depiction in the Codex Borgia.

Trivia

The Moon's mark. According to the myth, a rabbit thrown at Tecciztecatl's face left a dark mark that, by tradition, can still be seen on the Moon.
A name that describes him. His name means «full of sores», and in the tradition he is covered in tumours, which is why the gods chose him.
