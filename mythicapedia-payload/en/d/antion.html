hero of Greek mythology, great-grandson of Apollo

Antion

Antion is a figure from Greek mythology, the eldest son of Periphas and Astyaguia and the father of the demigod Ixion. He matters mainly for his lineage, since tradition made him a great-grandson of the god Apollo.

The myth

According to legend, Antion was the eldest son of Periphas and Astyaguia, daughter of Hypseus, a couple who were distantly related to each other. His ancestry tied him to Apollo: his paternal grandfather was Lapithus, a son of Apollo and Stilbe, remembered as the patriot of the Lapith people.

Antion married Perimele, and they became the parents of the legendary demigod Ixion. Accounts differ about Ixion's father. Some say it was Ares, while others name Phlegyas or Leonteus instead.
