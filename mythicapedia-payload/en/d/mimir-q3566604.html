wise being of Norse mythology

Mímir

Mímir is a being in Norse mythology famed for his wisdom. After he was beheaded, Odin kept his head, which went on counseling him and revealing secrets.

The myth

According to the myth, Mímir owned the well that bears his name, located beneath one of the roots of Yggdrasil, and drank from its waters with the Gjallarhorn to gain immense knowledge.

During the war between the Æsir and the Vanir, according to Snorri Sturluson, he was sent to the Vanir as a hostage. There he advised Hœnir, who without him could only answer 'let others decide'. The Vanir beheaded him and sent his head to Asgard. Odin embalmed it with herbs and charmed it so that it would speak.

The Völuspá places him at Ragnarök, when Odin rides to the well to seek counsel.

Legacy

Mímir appears in the video games God of War (2018) and its sequel God of War Ragnarök (2022), where he is voiced by Scottish actor Alastair Duncan.

Ancient sources: The Poetic Edda (Völuspá, Sigrdrífumál, Fjölsvinnsmál), Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda and Heimskringla.

Trivia

A name tied to memory. His name probably derives from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning to think or remember, the same base as Sanskrit smárati.
A possible uncle of Odin. Some scholars believe Mímir may be the brother of Bestla, Odin's mother, which would make him Odin's maternal uncle.
