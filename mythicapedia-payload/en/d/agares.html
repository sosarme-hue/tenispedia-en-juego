great duke of hell in demonological grimoires

Agares

Agares is a great duke of hell who appears in European demonological grimoires as the commander of legions of demons. He matters for his role in the manuals of ceremonial magic and for the image of the old demon those texts helped popularise.

The myth

According to the grimoires, Agares was originally a member of the order of Virtues, before the fall of the rebel angels. After the rebellion he ruled in hell with the rank of great duke and commands thirty-one legions of demons, although the number varies between texts: the Livre des Esperitz gives him thirty-six.

His most repeated image is that of an old man riding a crocodile and carrying a hawk on his fist, though the Book of the Office of Spirits describes him on a cockatrice and without the bird. His powers include teaching languages, making fugitives return, causing earthquakes and granting noble titles, and he is said to take pleasure in spreading immoral expressions.

In the infernal hierarchy he appears subordinate to Lucifuge Rofocale in the Grand Grimoire, and according to Thomas Rudd he is opposed by the angel Jelial. Some manuscripts of ceremonial magic also invoke him to guard treasure or to trap him in a crystal.

Legacy

In popular culture he appears in the film The Crucifixion (2017), directed by Xavier Gens, where he possesses a young nun, and in the anime and manga series Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun, under the same name as a sleepy young man. He also appears as a card in the Yu-Gi-Oh! trading card game under the name Dugares the Timeless.

Ancient sources: He appears in demonological grimoires such as the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, the Lesser Key of Solomon and the Livre des Esperitz.

Trivia

Disputed legions. The grimoires do not agree on how many legions he commands. Some give him thirty-one, while the Livre des Esperitz gives thirty-six.
Changing mount. His best-known mount is a crocodile, but in the Book of the Office of Spirits he appears on a cockatrice and without the hawk.
