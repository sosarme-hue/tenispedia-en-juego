Aztec god of the East wind

Tlalocayotl

Tlalocayotl is the Aztec god of the East wind, one of four wind deities who personify the winds of the cardinal directions. He belongs to the Aztec (Mexica) tradition and is best understood within the cosmic order to which he belonged.

The myth

According to the sources, the four winds personified the cardinal directions: Tlalocayotl of the East, together with Mictlanpachecatl, Huitztlampaehecatl and Cihuatecayotl. Although each had his own direction, all were under the command of Ehecatl, god of wind and an aspect of Quetzalcoatl.

These winds were also under the rule of the regents of each quarter: Xipetotec in the East, Tezcatlipoca in the North, Quetzalcoatl in the West and Huitzilopochtli in the South. One English-language account describes him as a brother of the other three winds, yet the tradition gives no detailed deeds or episodes of his own.
