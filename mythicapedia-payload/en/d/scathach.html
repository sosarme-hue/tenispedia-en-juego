Scottish warrior woman and martial arts teacher of the Ulster Cycle

Scáthach

Scáthach is a legendary warrior woman and martial arts teacher in the Ulster Cycle of Irish mythology, set in Scotland, especially on the Isle of Skye. She is famous for training Cú Chulainn, the great hero of Ulster.

The myth

In Tochmarc Emire (The Wooing of Emer), Cú Chulainn must prove his worth before he can marry Emer, daughter of Forgall Monach. He is sent to Alpae, a term that seems to refer to Scotland, where he first trains with Domnall. He then travels to Dún Scáith, Scáthach's fortress, where he learns combat skills and receives the deadly spear Gáe Bulg.

There he begins an affair with Scáthach's daughter Uathach. After he accidentally breaks her fingers, her lover Cochar Croibhe arrives, and Cú Chulainn defeats him in a duel. He then takes over Cochar's duties and becomes Uathach's partner. Scáthach eventually promises her daughter to him without demanding a bride price.

Later, her rival and sister Aífe threatens her territory. Cú Chulainn defeats her and spares her life on condition that she bear him a son: Connla. Years later, the hero kills Connla without realizing he is his own son.

Legacy

Scáthach appears as a playable character in the video game Fate/Grand Order and in Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne, where she can evolve into the goddess Skadi. She also appears as a character in Michael Scott's book series The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel.

Ancient sources: Tochmarc Emire, an Irish tale preserved in a short Old Irish version and a longer Middle Irish version.

Trivia

Fortress of Shadows. Her residence, Dún Scáith, means "Fortress of Shadows". This is where her nickname "the Shadow" comes from.
A teacher with two warrior daughters. Scáthach and her rival Aífe are both daughters of Árd-Greimne of Lethra, so the texts present them as sisters.
