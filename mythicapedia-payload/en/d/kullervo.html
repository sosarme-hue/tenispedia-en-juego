tragic hero of Finnish mythology

Kullervo

Kullervo is a tragic hero of Finnish mythology, the protagonist of one of the darkest stories in the Kalevala. His tale, also found in runic songs from Finland, Karelia and Estonia, makes him a key figure in Finnish folklore.

The myth

According to tradition, Kullervo is the son of Untamala, a woman of the family of Kalervo, whose uncle Untamo spared her life after slaughtering the tribe. Untamo tried to kill the infant three times, but his magical powers saved him. As a youth, the uncle sold him into slavery to Ilmarinen, whose wife enjoyed tormenting him.

One day she gave him bread baked with stones inside, and the old knife he kept from his family broke. Enraged, Kullervo cursed the cows she milked, which turned into bears and wolves that killed her. He fled and discovered that his family was alive, except for a sister who had vanished. On his way back from collecting a tribute, he seduced a beggar without recognising her; when he realised she was his sister, she took her own life.

Kullervo sought revenge on Untamo with a magic sword given to him by Ukko. He killed his uncle and the whole clan, but on returning home he found his relatives dead. In despair, he asked the sword whether it was ready to take his life, and it replied that it was eager to drink his blood. Kullervo died upon it. At the end of the poem, the old sage Väinämöinen warns parents against treating their children too harshly.

Legacy

Jean Sibelius chose Kullervo as the subject of his first major work, the symphonic poem Kullervo (1892), for soprano, baritone, choir and orchestra. The character also inspired J. R. R. Tolkien's Túrin in his invented mythology.

Ancient sources: Oral runic songs from Finnish, Karelian and Ingrian traditions, written down and gathered into Elias Lönnrot's Kalevala.

Trivia

Many versions. In some runic songs the protagonist is not Kullervo but Soini or Lemminkäinen, and in Estonia he appears as Kuller, son of Kalev.
A snake born from a spear. In a runic song from Kainuu, Kullervo's war spear snaps in two and a cowshed snake is born from it.
