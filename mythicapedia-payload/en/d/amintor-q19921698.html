son of Aegyptus in Greek myth

Amyntor

Amyntor is one of the fifty sons of Aegyptus in Greek mythology. His story is tied to that of the Danaids, the daughters of Danaus, and to a killing that took place on the wedding night.

The myth

According to the myth, Aegyptus had fifty sons, and his brother Danaus had the so-called Danaids, his daughters. Amyntor, known in Greek as Amýntōr, married Damone, one of them. His fate was bound to that of his brothers in a story of family violence that the texts relate only briefly.

Like almost all his brothers, Amyntor was killed by his wife on their wedding night. The only exception was Lynceus, who survived that massacre and whose tale is the one most often remembered.
