night creature of Asturian folklore

Pesadiellu

The pesadiellu is a creature of Asturian folklore that, according to tradition, appears while a person is asleep. Its name is associated with sleep paralysis and the crushing sensation that often accompanies it.

The myth

According to Asturian stories, no one has actually seen the pesadiellu, yet many people say they have suffered its effects. Whoever falls under its influence feels an extreme pressure on the body, as if something were pressing down on them, and tradition recommends reciting prayers to be freed from it.

Other tales describe how it arrives at night, while the person is sleeping. It takes the form of a tremendous weight settling over the whole body, which can even cause suffocation. In some versions it takes the shape of a hairy, flying hand.
