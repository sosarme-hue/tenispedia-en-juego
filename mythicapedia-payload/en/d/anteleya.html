Anthelea

Anthelea is one of the fifty Danaids of Greek mythology, daughter of King Danaus and the naiad Polyxo. Her name is tied to one of the darkest episodes in the traditions surrounding these sisters.

The myth

According to the story, Danaus was king of Libya and later of Argos, and from his union with the naiad Polyxo came fifty daughters, Anthelea among them. The Danaids formed a group bound by the fate that awaited them alongside their cousins, the sons of Aegyptus.

On her father's orders, Anthelea married her cousin Cisseus, son of King Aegyptus and the naiad Caliadne, and then killed him. With this act she became one of the protagonists of the slaying of the cousins, which tradition remembers as a terrible obedience to the father.
