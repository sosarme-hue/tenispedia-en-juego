king of Cos in Greek myth

Merops

Merops is a mythical king of the island of Cos in Greek mythology, best known for the family tragedy he suffered and for the impiety of his descendants.

The myth

According to the story, Merops was the son of Triopas and married the nymph Etemea, also named Echemeia in some accounts. She died pierced by the arrows of Artemis for having stopped honouring the goddess. Grief-stricken, Merops tried to take his own life, but Hera, moved by his suffering, placed him among the stars in the form of an eagle.

He was the father of Eumelus and, through him, grandfather of Agron, Bisa and Meropis, all remembered for their impiety. Among his daughters were Clytia, wife of Eurypylus of Cos, and Titanas, who is said to have been turned into a deer because of her beauty.
