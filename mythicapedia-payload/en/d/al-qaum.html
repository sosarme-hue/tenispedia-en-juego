Nabataean god of war and night

Al-Qaum

Al-Qaum was the Nabataean god of war, the night and the caravans, known as the "Protector of the Clan". Archaeologists consider him a major deity of the Nabataean pantheon, and his name appears in large numbers of inscriptions.

The myth

According to Nabataean tradition, Al-Qaum watched over the sleep of people. He protected their souls as they slept, in the form of stars that accompanied them on their nightly journey through the heavens. The same stars guided caravans crossing the desert. As a god of war, he also defended his clan.

Over time he was linked to the Greek god Ares or the Roman god Mars, in order to fit in with Western civilization.

Legacy

His name lives on in popular culture. The Saudi black metal band Al-Namrood draws on this pantheon for its music. Al-Qaum is also the name of one of the spaceships in the Exodan fleet in Becky Chambers' 2018 novel Record of a Spaceborn Few.

Trivia
