Hindu goddess of western Rajasthan

Rani Bhatiyani

Rani Bhatiyani is a Hindu goddess venerated mainly in western Rajasthan, India, and in the Sindh region of Pakistan. She is especially honoured by the Merasi Manganiyar bard community.

The myth

According to tradition, Swarup, a Rajput princess of the small kingdom of Jogidas in Jaisalmer district, was known as Bhatiyani after her father's Bhati clan. She married Kalyan Singh, a Rathore prince of Jasol.

Accounts of her death vary. In one, Kalyan Singh's jealous first wife, Devri, poisons Bhatiyani's son, Lal Singh. In another, a false rumour that her husband had died in battle leads her to commit sati on the pyre of her brother-in-law. Afterwards the family suffers misfortune, and a shrine in Jasol placates her spirit, which is said to become benevolent.

Worship and religion

Her major temples are in Jasol and Jogidas, where she is called Bhuasa. Women of the Dholi community sing Ghoomar songs in her honour, and bards invoke her as Majisa, or Mother.

Trivia

Vandalised temple. In 2020, vandals attacked a Hindu temple dedicated to Rani Bhatiyani in Tharparkar district, Pakistan. They damaged her idol and set fire to holy books.
First vision. It is said that the goddess gave her first vision to a member of the Dholi community, whose women sing her Ghoomar songs.
