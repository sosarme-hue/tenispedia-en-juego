legendary sixth king of Sicyon

Aegyrus

Aegyrus was, in Greek myth, the sixth king of Sicyon, a city in the Peloponnese. His reign matters because it links the succession of the city's early kings to the legendary rivalry between Sicyon and Argos.

The myth

Aegyrus was the son of Thelxion, and he inherited the throne from him. According to the story, his father rebelled against Argos, which then dominated the whole Peloponnese, and killed the Argive king Apis.

Revenge soon followed: Aegyrus died at the hands of Argus Panoptes, the giant with a hundred eyes, who thus avenged the death of his king. The throne then passed to Aegyrus's son Thurimachus. The tradition offers no further deeds for Aegyrus, and the episode works mainly as a link in a chain of rivalries between cities.
