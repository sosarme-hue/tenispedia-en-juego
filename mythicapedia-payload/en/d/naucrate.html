wife of Daedalus in Greek mythology

Naucrate

Naucrate is a figure of Greek mythology, the wife of Daedalus and mother of Icarus, two characters tied to Crete. Her role is minor, though her name survives in the place names of Greek Egypt.

The myth

According to tradition, Naucrate was a slave of Minos, king of Crete. She fell in love with Daedalus, drawn by his cleverness and intelligence, and with him she had Icarus.

In the stories that have come down to us, she usually stays in the background, overshadowed by the exploits of her husband and son, which shape the myth.

Legacy

One reference links her to the western part of the Nile Delta, which she is said to personify. The Greeks founded their first colony in Egypt about 50 years before Solon's visit and named it Naucratis.

Trivia
