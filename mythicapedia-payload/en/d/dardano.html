Illyrian hero, eponymous founder of the Dardani

Dardanus

Dardanus is a hero of Greek mythology, son of Illyrius and eponymous founder of the Dardani, an ancient Illyrian people. His figure explains the origins of several peoples of the Balkans.

The myth

According to Appian of Alexandria, Dardanus founded the Dardani, an ancient Illyrian tribe that inhabited much of present-day Kosovo and lands in what is now Serbia. As a son of Illyrius, he was placed within a genealogy of heroes that gave their names to the peoples of the region.

Accounts of the family differ: some make Illyrius a son of Cadmus and Harmonia, others a son of Polyphemus and Galatea. Dardanus had five brothers, each founding a people: Enchelus, Autarieus, Maedus, Taulas and Perrhaebus. He also had three sisters, Partho, Daortho and Dassaro, linked to the Parthini, the Daorsi and the Dassaretii.

Ancient sources: Appian of Alexandria is the source that presents Dardanus as the founder of the Dardani.

Trivia

Founding brothers. Each of Dardanus's brothers appears as the founder of an Illyrian people, such as the Enchelei, the Autariatae or the Taulantii.
A disputed genealogy. Ancient authors do not agree on Illyrius's parents: some make him a son of Cadmus and Harmonia, others of Polyphemus and Galatea.
