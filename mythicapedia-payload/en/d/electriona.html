heroine of Rhodes, daughter of Helios

Electryone

Electryone (also Electrione) is a heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Helios and Rhodos and sister of the Heliadae. She died young and was honoured on Rhodes with heroic cult, which makes her a notable local figure of the island.

The myth

According to tradition, Electryone was a daughter of the sun god Helios and the sea goddess Rhodos, and thus a younger sister of the Heliadae. The surviving accounts say little more about her: she died while still a maiden, and the people of Rhodes granted her honours usually reserved for heroes.

Her name appears in two forms. The Doric version, Alectrona, is related to the Greek word for "rooster", while the Attic Electryone is connected with elektron, "amber", the golden colour of sunrise.

Worship and religion

She had a temple and sacred precinct at Ialyssos on Rhodes, and the rules for its visitors survive in an inscription on a marble tablet from the 3rd century BC. Some coins from the island show her wearing a solar crown, suggesting she was a local goddess of Rhodes.

Trivia

Solar crown. Some coins from Rhodes show her wearing a solar crown, a detail that links her to her father, Helios.
Rooster and amber in her name. Her name plays on two different Greek words: one for rooster and one for amber, depending on whether the Doric or Attic form is used.
