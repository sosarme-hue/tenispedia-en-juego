Mycenaean prince in Greek mythology

Gorgophonus

Gorgophonus is a Mycenaean prince in Greek mythology, son of King Electryon and brother of Alcmena, mother of the hero Heracles. His name means "Gorgon-killer", a title that links him to the world of the Gorgons.

The myth

According to the tradition, Gorgophonus was one of the many sons of King Electryon and Anaxo, daughter of Alcaeus. Together with his brothers he belonged to a royal line of Mycenae that also included Alcmena, who would become the mother of Heracles. His brothers included Stratobates, Phylonomus, Celaeneus, Amphimachus, Lysinomus, Chirimachus, Anactor and Archelaus.

The legend tells that he and his brothers were killed by the sons of the Taphian king Pterelaus. They claimed the kingdom of their ancestor Mestor, son of Perseus and brother of Electryon, and would not accept that the crown should remain in Electryon's line.
