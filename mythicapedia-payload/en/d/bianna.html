Bianna

Bianna is a young Cretan woman from Greek legend, from Biennus (modern Viannos, on Crete). Her story explains the origin of a place name: Bienna, a site in Gaul that later became the capital of the Allobroges.

The myth

According to Stephanus of Byzantium, a famine in Biennus forced much of the population to emigrate. Bianna was among those who fled to a region of Gaul along the Rhône. While she was dancing, the ground opened beneath her feet and swallowed her.

According to tradition, her companions named the spot after her, Bienna, which later became the capital of the Celtic Allobroges, in the Dauphiné, today Vienne.

Legacy

Her story survives mainly in the name of Vienne, the French city that inherited the place name linked to the legend.

Ancient sources: Stephanus of Byzantium, who records her in discussing the origin of Biennus.

Trivia

A name that lasts. According to the legend, the place where Bianna vanished took her name, and it later gave rise to the Allobroges' capital.
A late witness. Her story reaches us through Stephanus of Byzantium, who mentions it while discussing the origin of Biennus.
