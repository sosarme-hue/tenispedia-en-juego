culture hero of Inuit mythology

Apanuugak

Apanuugak is a culture hero of Inuit mythology, the traditions of the Indigenous peoples of the North American Arctic. Some stories portray him as an error-prone warrior who lives to old age, while others cast him as a despicable villain.

The myth

In Inuit oral tradition, the tales about Apanuugak do not form a single fixed cycle; they vary from one storyteller and community to another. In some versions he appears as a clumsy warrior whose misadventures may make listeners laugh, yet who manages to survive into old age.

In others his portrait is much darker: he is presented as a villain of base conduct. This duality is common among culture heroes in many peoples' traditions, who mix cleverness, folly and transgression, and who teach their audiences through both their successes and their failures.
