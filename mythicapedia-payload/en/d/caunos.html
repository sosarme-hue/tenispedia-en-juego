hero of Greek mythology, son of Miletus

Caunus

Caunus is a hero of Greek mythology, son of Miletus and brother of Byblis. His story, marked by his sister's unrequited passion and by his flight from home, is tied to the legendary origin of the city of Caunus in Lycia, which bears his name.

The myth

According to the story, Byblis fell passionately in love with her brother Caunus. The sources do not agree on who made the first move: some say he was the first to feel affection for her, while others describe Byblis' feelings as unrequited.

All versions agree, however, that Caunus chose to flee from home in order to avoid committing incest with his sister. Byblis followed him relentlessly until she was completely exhausted by grief, and she died, according to some accounts of sorrow and according to others by suicide.

Caunus eventually reached Lycia, where he married the Naiad Pronoe and had a son, Aegialus. In time he became king of the land. After his death, Aegialus gathered the people from scattered settlements into a newly founded city, which he named Caunus after his father.
