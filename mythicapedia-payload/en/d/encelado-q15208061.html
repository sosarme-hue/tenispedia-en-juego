Egyptian prince in Greek myth

Enceladus

Enceladus is an Egyptian prince of Greek mythology, a son of King Aegyptus and brother of Lynceus, Proteus, Busiris, Lycus and Daiphron. His story is bound up with the Danaids and with the fate of his brothers on their wedding night.

The myth

According to tradition, Enceladus was a son of King Aegyptus and Argyphia, a woman of royal blood, which made him a full brother of Lynceus, Proteus, Busiris, Lycus and Daiphron. Some accounts give him a different mother: Eurrhoe, daughter of the river-god Nilus, or Isaie, daughter of King Agenor of Tyre.

Like his brothers, apart from Lynceus, he was killed on his wedding night by his own wives, who obeyed the command of their father, King Danaus of Libya. Depending on the version, Enceladus married either the Danaid Trite or Amymone, daughter of Danaus and Europe.
