Kuru princess, daughter of Dhritarashtra and Gandhari

Duhsala

Duhsala is the only daughter of King Dhritarashtra and Gandhari, and sister of the hundred Kaurava brothers in the Mahabharata, the great Hindu epic. Her marriage to the king of Sindhu ties her to the war between the Kurus and the Pandavas.

The myth

According to the Mahabharata, Gandhari, wife of the Kuru king Dhritarashtra, received a promise of a hundred sons from the sage Vyasa. When she gave birth to a single ball of flesh, Vyasa divided it into a hundred portions, each placed in a vessel of clarified butter to become a son. Gandhari also asked for a daughter, and the sage set aside one extra portion from which Duhsala was born.

She grew up in the palace of Hastinapura alongside her hundred Kaurava brothers and her Pandava cousins, and the Kuru elders married her to Jayadratha, king of Sindhu. When Jayadratha tried to abduct Draupadi during the Pandavas' exile, Yudhishthira asked that his life be spared so Duhsala would not be widowed. They shaved his head as a humiliation and let him go.

In the Kurukshetra War, Jayadratha took part in the killing of Abhimanyu, son of Arjuna, and Arjuna fulfilled his vow to kill him before sunset. In the Stri Parva, Duhsala appears as a young widow overwhelmed by grief, searching the battlefield for her husband's head. Years later, during Arjuna's ashvamedha campaign, she came before him with her grandson, son of Suratha, and pleaded for mercy; Arjuna agreed, and the boy was later crowned king of Sindhu.

Legacy

She appears in Sanskrit drama: Bhasa portrays her as a noble, compassionate figure in Dutaghatotkacha, and Bhatta Narayana places her, silent, in Venisamhara (11th century), according to scholar Ratnamayidevi Dikshit.

Ancient sources: The Mahabharata, in its Adi, Sabha, Vana, Stri and Ashvamedhika Parvas.

Trivia

One portion too many. Gandhari asked for a daughter besides the hundred sons, and Vyasa set aside an extra piece of the flesh so that Duhsala would be born.
Searching for a head. In the Stri Parva, Duhsala runs across the battlefield searching for the severed head of her husband Jayadratha.
