mythical king of Messenia

Penthilus

Penthilus is a mythical king of Messenia in Greek tradition, a figure who links the royal house of this Peloponnesian region with that of Athens through his grandson Melanthus.

The myth

According to the most widespread version, Penthilus was the son of Periclymenus and father of Borus. Earlier accounts, however, present him as the son of Borus and Lysidice, so his genealogy varies depending on the source. His role in the story is minor, but it serves to connect the family trees of different Greek regions.

Penthilus married Anchirhoe, and their son was Andropompus. Andropompus was the father of Melanthus, king of Athens, so the Messenian line reaches the Athenian throne. In the myth, this connection explains the bond between two of the great kingdoms of legendary Greece.
