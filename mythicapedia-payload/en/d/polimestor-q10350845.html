mythical king of Arcadia

Polymestor

Polymestor is a mythical king of Arcadia in ancient Greece, son and successor of Eginetes. His reign is linked to the first conquest of Tegea by the Laconians, led by Charilaus.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Polymestor was the son of Eginetes and inherited the throne of Arcadia. During his reign, the Laconians, led by Charilaus, conquered the city of Tegea for the first time, one of the main settlements of the region. The sources that record this report do not describe the campaign or the causes of the clash in detail. The city of Tegea, located in central Arcadia, is the setting of this episode.

Polymestor died without children, so the throne passed to his nephew Echemus, who became the next king of Arcadia. With this succession the account the sources preserve about this monarch ends.
