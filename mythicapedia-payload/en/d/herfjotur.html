valkyrie of Norse mythology

Herfjötur

Herfjötur is a valkyrie in Norse mythology, one of the thirteen listed in the Poetic Edda poem Grímnismál. Her name, roughly meaning 'war-fetter' or 'fetter of the army', points to the valkyries' role in determining fortune in battle.

The myth

The sources say very little about her beyond her name and her place in the lists of valkyries. The Poetic Edda includes her among the thirteen in Grímnismál, and the Prose Edda records her in the longer of its two name lists, the Nafnaþulur. No specific episodes of her life or any genealogy survive.

According to Rudolf Simek, the name is kenning-like and most likely refers to the valkyries' power to decide fortune in combat. Simek also links it to the idisi in one of the two Merseburg Incantations, beings who could hamper enemies and who, in his view, were a kind of valkyrie.

Ancient sources: The Poetic Edda poem Grímnismál and the longer of the two Nafnaþulur lists in the Prose Edda.
