king of the nagas in Hinduism

Vasuki

Vasuki is the king of the nagas, serpent beings, in Hinduism. His image as a serpent coiled around Shiva's neck has made him one of the most recognizable figures among India's sacred snakes.

The myth

According to tradition, Vasuki is a son of the sage Kashyapa and Kadru. He played a key part in the churning of the Ocean of Milk: gods and asuras wrapped him around Mount Mandara and used him as a rope to extract the amrita, the nectar of immortality.

In the Mahabharata, Kadru curses him after he refuses to help her cheat her sister Vinata in a wager. Later, when King Janamejaya's snake sacrifice threatens his brothers, Vasuki seeks counsel from his siblings. The plan ends with his sister marrying the sage Jaratkaru, and their son Astika brings an end to the persecution of the nagas.

In another episode, Bhima, poisoned by his cousins the Kauravas and thrown into the Ganges, reaches Nagaloka, where Vasuki welcomes him with riches and a drink that gives him the strength of a thousand elephants.

Worship and religion

Vasuki is venerated in several temples of southern India. The Vasuka temple near Haripad in Kerala and another in Visakha district, Andhra Pradesh, are dedicated to him. A regional legend of the Kukke Subramanya temple in Karnataka says the god Kartikeya protected him from Garuda, Vishnu's mount. In Buddhism he is one of the naga kings who protect and honour the Buddha, and his naga priest is Tatig Naga.

Legacy

His figure coiled around Shiva is one of the most repeated motifs in Hindu iconography. In China and Japan he appears among the eight Great Dragon Kings of Buddhism, alongside Nanda, Upananda, Sagara, Takshaka and others.

Ancient sources: The Mahabharata recounts his curse, his role in the churning of the ocean and the episode of Janamejaya.

Trivia

A gem on his head. He is described with a gem called nagamani, or serpent's ornament, on his head.
Protected by Kartikeya. A regional legend from Karnataka tells how the god Kartikeya defended him from an attack by Garuda.
