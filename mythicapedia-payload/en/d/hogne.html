king of Östergötland and father of the valkyrie Sigrún

Högne

Högne was a king of Östergötland in what is now Sweden, and the father of the valkyrie Sigrún. He appears in medieval sources and in the Poetic Edda.

The myth

According to Snorri Sturluson in the Heimskringla, Högne ruled Östergötland alongside Granmar, king of Södermanland, and together they withstood the cruel Ingjald. Högne and his son Hildur raided Ingjald's provinces until Högne died peacefully in his own kingdom.

In the Volsunga saga and the Poetic Edda, Högne's daughter Sigrún is promised to Granmar's son Hothbrodd. The suitor Helgi Hundingsbane attacks Granmar, and in the battle Helgi kills Högne, his son Bragi and Hothbrodd. Sigrún mourns her kin and ends up marrying Helgi. Later, Högne's surviving son Dagr asks Odin for revenge and kills Helgi with a spear the god grants him.

Ancient sources: Snorri Sturluson's Heimskringla, the Volsunga saga, and the poems Helgakviða Hundingsbana I and II of the Poetic Edda.
