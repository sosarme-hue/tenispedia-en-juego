Cantabrian goblin who finds lost things

Zahorí goblin

The zahorí goblin is a small goblin from Cantabrian folklore who, unlike most goblins, does not play tricks on people. He is also known as the miraculous finder or the goblin of lost things.

The myth

According to Cantabrian folk tradition, when someone loses an object they can call out for the zahorí goblin to help them. If the caller has a good heart, the goblin arrives quickly, listens closely to the description of the lost item, and signals for the person to follow him.

He does not head straight for the object but takes many detours. If he sees that his companion is growing impatient and doubting him, he suddenly vanishes. Alone, he then recovers the item and hands it to someone in need.

He is a small, dark-skinned goblin with a round face, a long, sharp nose, big black eyes and blond hair. His voice is hoarse, as if he were angry, though he is actually cheerful, and his laugh is long and mocking. He wears a red sheepskin jacket and never stops running back and forth.
