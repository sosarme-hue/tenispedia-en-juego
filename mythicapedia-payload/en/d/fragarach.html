sword of the sea god in Irish mythology

Fragarach

Fragarach is a sword from Irish mythology, known as "The Answerer" or "The Retaliator", that belonged to the sea god Manannán mac Lir. Its importance lies in its power: according to the tradition, no mail or armour could withstand it.

The myth

According to the stories, Manannán owned the sword and later gave it to Lugh, his foster son. When Nuada, the first high king, lost an arm in the first battle of Mag Tuired and was no longer fit to rule, he chose Lugh as provisional king for the second battle. Lugh arrived bearing magical gifts from the Celtic Otherworld: the horse Enbarr, Manannán's magical boat, and Fragarach. Using a spear and a sling that Manannán had also given him, he defeated the Fomorians and their king, Balor.

Legacy

The sword has passed into modern popular culture. Patricia Kennealy Morrison features it in her Keltiad series, where several protagonists wield it; Kevin Hearne puts it in the hands of his protagonist in The Iron Druid Chronicles; it appears in the Dungeons & Dragons module The Temple of Elemental Evil, in the Digimon franchise, carried by Slayerdramon, and in Type-Moon's Fate Hollow/Ataraxia, as the Noble Phantasm of Bazett Fraga McRemitz.

Trivia

A name that describes its role. Fragarach is known as "The Answerer" or "The Retaliator", names that refer to its nature as a weapon that strikes back at whoever wields it against its bearer.
A weapon that weakens its foes. Besides piercing any armour, the sword weakened its opponents, which made it a decisive advantage in combat.
