seductive hero and shaman of Finnish mythology

Lemminkäinen

Lemminkäinen is a hero of Finnish folklore, a young charmer, shaman and sorcerer who appears in the Kalevala. His figure blends traits of several heroes from oral poetry, and his story of death and resurrection makes him one of the most vivid characters in Finnish tradition.

The myth

According to tradition, Lemminkäinen was a handsome young man with wavy blond hair who could sing and enchant sand until it turned into pearls. He was a cunning, somewhat vengeful trickster, close to a rogue, and he was mainly associated with water. His great quest was to win one of the daughters of Louhi, mistress of Pohjola, a feat Ilmarinen had already tried in vain.

To achieve it he had to face dangerous tests, such as capturing or killing the black swan that nests in the river of Tuonela, the underworld of the dead. There Lemminkäinen drowned. His mother searched sky, sea and land until she found his remains on the riverbed and his clothes on the shore. Through a long magical ritual and a series of spells, she brought him back to life.

Other versions tell a different ending: the revived hero cannot become a man again, since his body has already rotted. Some have him killed by Väinämöinen or by a mocked rival. In traditional tales he is also called Kaukomieli, and some sources identify him with the deity Ahti.

Legacy

His story of death and resurrection inspired Jean Sibelius, who wrote the Four Legends, op. 22, also known as the Lemminkäinen Suite, with one movement devoted to the Swan of Tuonela and another to Lemminkäinen's return. He also appears in the Kalevala, Finland's national epic poem.

Ancient sources: The Finnish and Estonian runic songs collected from oral tradition, such as the Song of Lemminkäinen and the Death of Lemminkäinen, on which the Kalevala was later built.

Trivia

Greek parallel. His mother's desperate search has been compared to Demeter's search for her daughter Persephone in Greek mythology.
Blond or red hair. The Kalevala describes him as blond and wavy, but other traditions give him red hair.
Fire and love. His name is connected with fire and erotic love, much like the name of Lempo.
