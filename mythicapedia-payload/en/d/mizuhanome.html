goddess of water in Japanese mythology

Mizuhanome

Mizuhanome is a water deity of Japanese mythology, a child of Izanagi and Izanami. Her figure links the worship of waterways with Shinto tradition and with present-day popular culture.

The myth

According to tradition, Mizuhanome was born from the urine of Izanami, one of the primordial deities of Japan. She thus appears as a goddess born from a bodily act of her mother, in a story that explains the origin of some of the divine beings of the Japanese world.

Her name is explained as "water snake woman" or "water dragon woman", linking her with rivers, springs and the powers attributed to them. Mitsu or mizu means water, and me is a feminine suffix.

Worship and religion

She is venerated at several shrines dedicated to her: Atago Shrine, Ashiya Shrine and Niukawakami Shrine. The sources do not describe specific rites or festivals, but these shrines show that her worship remains tied to sacred places of water.

Legacy

The director Makoto Shinkai has said that the name of Mitsuha Miyamizu, the protagonist of the film Your Name, comes from this goddess.

Trivia
