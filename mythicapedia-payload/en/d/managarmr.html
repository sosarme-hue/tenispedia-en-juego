moon wolf in Norse mythology

Mánagarmr

Mánagarmr, “Moon-Hound”, is a wolf from Norse mythology that, according to the tradition, will swallow the Moon at Ragnarök. Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda links it with Hati, another wolf of the same brood.

The myth

According to Snorri Sturluson, Hati Hróðvitnisson, son of Fenrir, chases the Moon across the night sky, just as Sköll chases the Sun by day. His mother is a giantess who lives east of Midgard, in the Ironwood, and bears giant wolves. At Ragnarök, they will devour the heavenly bodies.

Snorri also names Mánagarmr, “Moon's Dog”, as the most powerful of this brood. The Völuspá prophesies that a son of the giantess will snatch the Moon, while the Vafþrúðnismál says Fenrir himself will destroy the Sun. Mánagarmr is probably another name for Hati or Sköll, which Snorri took from folklore.

Ancient sources: Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda, and the Poetic Edda poems Völuspá, Grímnismál and Vafþrúðnismál.

Trivia

A name meaning "hate". Hati, the name of this wolf, means "He Who Hates" or "Enemy".
Spelling variants. Mánagarmr can also be written as Managarm, Manegarm or Mánagarm.
Another name for Fenrir. Hróðvitnir, “Famous Wolf”, is an alternate name for Fenrir, the father of Hati according to his patronymic.
