Vedic god of wealth and fortune

Bhaga

Bhaga is a Vedic god of wealth, good fortune and marriage, and one of the Adityas, the sons of the goddess Aditi. His name gave rise to words such as bhagavān and bhagya, and its root appears in the Slavic bog, 'god', too.

The myth

In the Rigveda, Bhaga appears as the god who distributes goods and the share of fortune that each person receives according to their merits. The hymns present him as sustainer of the world, giver of wealth and chief leader of rites, and ask him for cattle, horses, offspring, happiness and riches. Hymn 7.41 is devoted almost entirely to him and to the deities around him, such as Agni, Indra, Mitra-Varuna and Soma, and Bhaga is invoked there about sixty times.

His link with the sun appears in some passages: the dawn goddess Ushas is described as his sister, and his eye is adorned with rays. Later, the Nirukta describes him as the god of the morning. According to a later legend, the mighty Virabhadra, a giant created by Shiva, blinded him. In the Bhagavata Purana he reappears among the twelve Adityas, by then solar gods.

Worship and religion

The Vedic hymns invoke him in rituals to ask for specific goods, and his role as giver of fortune made him a focus of prayers. The sources consulted do not describe temples or festivals dedicated to him, and it is not clear whether his cult continues today.

Legacy

Bhaga's name survives in several words: bhagavān, 'one who possesses wealth', came to mean 'god' or 'lord', and bhagya means 'destiny'. His root is found in the Slavic bog, 'god'. Likewise, English lord comes from hlaford, 'bread-warden', and the name of Baghdad derives from Middle Persian bag-dād, 'given by god'.

Ancient sources: The Rigveda, with its hymn 7.41 devoted to Bhaga, and the Nirukta, which describes him as the god of the morning; he also appears in the Bhagavata Purana.

Trivia

Destroyed eyes. A later legend says that Virabhadra, created by Shiva, blinded him, an episode that does not appear in the oldest hymns.
A city with the same root. The name of Baghdad derives from Middle Persian bag-dād, 'given by god', which shares its origin with the name Bhaga.
