creator god of the Kuba people

Mbombo

Mbombo, also called Bumba, is the creator god in the religion and mythology of the Kuba people of Central Africa, in what is now the Democratic Republic of the Congo. His myth describes the creation of the universe from nothing.

The myth

According to the myth, in the beginning Mbombo, a white giant, was alone while darkness and primordial water covered the earth. After feeling a sharp pain in his stomach, he vomited the sun, the moon and the stars; the sun's heat dried the water and dry hills rose out of it.

He then vomited nine animals, including the leopard Koy Bumba, the eagle Ponga Bumba and the crocodile Ganda Bumba, who went on to create the world's creatures. Mbombo's sons followed: Nyonye Ngana died after making the white ants, Chonganda created the first plant, and Chedi Bumba made the last bird, the kite. Tsetse Bumba was chased into the sky and became the thunderbolt. Finally Mbombo handed the world over to humankind and retreated into the heavens.

Trivia

Origin of lightning. Tsetse Bumba caused trouble on earth, so Mbombo chased her into the sky, where she became the thunderbolt.
The ants and Nyonye Ngana. The ants went deep into the earth for dark soil to bury Nyonye Ngana, and in doing so transformed the barren sands on the surface.
