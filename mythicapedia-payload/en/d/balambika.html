Hindu goddess of knowledge and children

Balambika

Balambika is a goddess worshipped in Hinduism, mainly in South India. Her name means "Goddess of Knowledge" or "Child Goddess," and she is associated with wisdom, education and prosperity.

The myth

According to tradition, Balambika is described in the Balambika Dasakam, a sacred text originally written in Sanskrit. She is shown with four arms and a red circle on each palm, holding a sacred book and a japamala with two of her hands.

She is considered a child and behaves like one, yet devotees believe she bestows true knowledge, education, wisdom, power and prosperity for a better life. For this reason she is sometimes called the goddess of children.

Worship and religion

Her temple stands in Kamarasavalli, in Ariyalur district of Tamil Nadu, and is said to be between one and two thousand years old. Its walls carry sculptures of Karkodga worshipping Shiva with Vinayaka and Nandi. It is said that people born under the zodiac sign of Cancer should pray there for relief. Devotees invoke her with a short root mantra whose syllables are linked to learning, attraction and prosperity.

Ancient sources: Her sacred text is the Balambika Dasakam, originally written in Sanskrit.
