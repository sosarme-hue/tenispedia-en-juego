Inuit deity, father of the sea goddess

Anguta

Anguta is the father of Sedna, the sea goddess, in Inuit religion. According to some Greenlandic Inuit myths he is the creator god and the supreme being, while in others he appears as a mere mortal, and he is also considered a god of the dead.

The myth

His name, meaning "man with something to cut", refers to the mutilation of his daughter, an act that, according to the myth, ultimately made her a goddess. This episode appears in both versions of the story, the one that presents him as creator and the one that shows him as a mortal.

As a psychopomp, Anguta ferries souls from the land of the living to Adlivun, the underworld ruled by his daughter. There the souls must sleep near him for a year before moving on to Qudlivun, "those above us", where they enjoy eternal bliss. In some versions only unworthy souls remain with him, and he torments them by pinching them.

Trivia
