Egyptian prince in Greek mythology

Diocorystes

Diocorystes is an Egyptian prince in Greek mythology, a son of King Aegyptus. His fate is tied to that of the Danaids, the daughters of Danaus who killed their husbands on their wedding night.

The myth

According to tradition, Diocorystes was the son of an Arabian woman and had many brothers, including Lynceus. Some accounts make him the son of other mothers: Eurryroe, daughter of the river-god Nilus, or Isaie, daughter of King Agenor of Tyre.

Diocorystes married Hippodamia, daughter of Danaus, king of Libya. On their father's orders, the wives of his brothers killed them on their wedding night, and all of them died except Lynceus. Diocorystes suffered the same fate. Other accounts say Hippodamia was the daughter of the hamadryads Atlanteia or Phoebe.
