Mesopotamian deity

Gareus

Gareus is a deity of late Mesopotamia, worshipped in the city of Uruk (in present-day Iraq) thanks to the Parthians. Little is known about him, and he is considered an example of religious blending between Hellenistic and Babylonian traditions.

The myth

No myths about Gareus survive. The available sources do not allow us to reconstruct his origin, family, deeds or the end of his story. What is known comes from the remains found around his temple, which barely show how he was imagined.

Worship and religion

The Parthians introduced his cult to Uruk, where a temple attributed to this deity was built around 110 AD. The remains found in its surroundings suggest that Gareus combined elements of Hellenistic and Babylonian tradition, though the details of how he was worshipped are not well known.
