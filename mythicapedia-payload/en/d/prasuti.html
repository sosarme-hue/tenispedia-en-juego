Hindu goddess, consort of Daksha and mother of Sati

Prasuti

Prasuti is a goddess in Hinduism, the consort of the patriarch Daksha and mother of a large line of daughters, among them Sati. She matters because through her descendants she links several great deities and the stories told in the Puranas.

The myth

According to the tradition, Prasuti is the daughter of Svayambhuva Manu, the first Manu, and Shatarupa. She married Daksha, and the myth presents her mainly as the mother of many daughters.

The Vishnu, Linga and Padma Puranas agree that the couple had numerous daughters, though the number varies from 16 to 60 depending on the account; most scholars settle on 24. Among them are Shraddha, Bhakti, Svaha, Svadha, Khyati and Sati. The Padma Purana says Daksha, unsatisfied with twenty-four, produced sixty more maidens, although other texts attribute these to Asikni.

Ancient sources: The Vishnu Purana, the Linga Purana and the Padma Purana mention her as the wife of Daksha and mother of his daughters.
