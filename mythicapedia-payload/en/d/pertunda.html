minor Roman goddess of marriage consummation

Pertunda

Pertunda is a minor goddess of Roman mythology associated with the consummation of marriage. Her figure is linked to rites meant to secure the union of the newlywed couple at the start of their life together.

The myth

Little of her story survives in extended form; tradition presents her mainly as a deity who presided over the consummation of marriage. Like other minor deities, she appears more in custom than in narrative.

That is why her figure shows how Roman religion attended even to the most intimate moments of domestic life.

Worship and religion

Custom required that a small statuette of the goddess, shown nude, be placed in a prominent spot in the bridal chamber. It was believed to bring good omens to the new couple. The rite took place in the private setting of the bridal chamber, not in a temple.
