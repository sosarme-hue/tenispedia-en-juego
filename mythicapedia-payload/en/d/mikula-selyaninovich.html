Mikula Selyaninovich

Mikula Selyaninovich is a Russian epic hero, a bogatyr who works as a plowman and comes from the bylina cycle of the Novgorod Republic. He matters because he shows that the greatest strength does not always belong to warriors.

The myth

Mikula stands apart from the warrior heroes who dominate most of these songs: he is not a fighter but a farmer who plows the land.

A recurring plot in the byliny shows him outmatching other warrior-type bogatyrs. When they try to pull his plow out of the soil, lift his bag or race him, they fail, because Mother Earth loves Mikula and lends him her strength. His power thus comes from his bond with the land rather than from the sword. His daughters, Vasilisa and Nastasya Mikulishna, complete his family.
