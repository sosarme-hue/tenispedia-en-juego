Spartoi hero of Greek mythology

Echion

Echion is one of the five surviving Spartoi, the warriors born from the dragon's teeth sown by Cadmus. His story links him to the founding of Thebes and to the royal family.

The myth

His name comes from the Greek word for serpent, echis, a nod to his origin from the dragon's teeth. According to tradition, Cadmus valued his courage above that of all the others except himself, so he made him his son-in-law by giving him his daughter Agave in marriage. Their son was Pentheus.

Echion also helped Cadmus build Thebes. He had another child, a daughter named Epirus, after whom the region of Epirus was named.

Worship and religion

He was said to have dedicated a temple to Cybele in Boeotia.

Legacy

His daughter Epirus lent her name to the region of Epirus.

Trivia

A serpent name. His name derives from the Greek echis, meaning serpent, linked to his origin from dragon's teeth.
A region named after a woman. According to tradition, the region of Epirus took its name from Epirus, daughter of Echion.
