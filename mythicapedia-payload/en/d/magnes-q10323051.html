hero of Greek mythology, son of Argos

Magnes

Magnes is a hero of Greek mythology, son of Argos and Perimele, linked to Thessaly. His name gave rise to the region of Magnesia.

The myth

According to the story, Magnes was a son of Argos, who was himself a son of Phrixus, and of Perimele, daughter of Admetus. He lived in Thessaly, in the land that people came to call Magnesia after him. With the Muse Calliope he had a son of remarkable beauty, Hymen.

The god Apollo fell in love with Hymen as soon as he saw him and would not leave the house of Magnes. To lure him away, Hermes devised a scheme to seize Apollo's cattle, which grazed in the same pasture as those of Admetus.

Trivia

A name that lasts. The region of Magnesia in Thessaly was named after the hero, according to the tradition recorded in the reference text.
