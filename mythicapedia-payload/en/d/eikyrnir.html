stag of Norse mythology

Eikþyrnir

Eikþyrnir is a stag of Norse mythology who stands upon Valhalla, the hall of the slain warriors. His name is usually understood to mean «the one with oak-thorn antlers».

The myth

According to the myth, the stag Eikþyrnir stands on top of Valhalla and, like the goat Heiðrún, feeds on the foliage of the tree Læraðr. Snorri Sturluson describes him in the Prose Edda, right after Heiðrún, though his main source seems to be Grímnismál in the Poetic Edda.

The origin of his name remains debated. Anatoly Liberman suggests that it derives from Heiðþyrnir, the name of the lowest heaven, which was split in two to form the names of the heavenly stag Eikþyrnir and the heavenly goat Heiðrún. The link with thorns is probably a folk etymology.

Legacy

He has made his way into video games: he appears in Fire Emblem Heroes in several versions, as a character in Arknights, as a boss called Eikthyr in Valheim, and as the Eikthyrdeer Pals in Palworld.

Ancient sources: Grímnismál in the Poetic Edda, and Gylfaginning in Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda.

Trivia

A name split in two. According to Anatoly Liberman's hypothesis, the names of Eikþyrnir and Heiðrún may come from a single term for the lowest heaven, divided into two halves.
Five versions in one game. In Fire Emblem Heroes, Eikþyrnir has five variants, including a winter and a summer version, as well as the Brave version.
