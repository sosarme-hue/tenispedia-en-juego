Spartan hero of Greek myth

Perileos

Perileos is a hero of Greek myth, son of Icarius and brother of Penelope, associated with Sparta. Other figures in different stories share his name, so it is worth telling them apart.

The myth

According to tradition, Perileos was a Spartan, son of Icarius and the naiad Periboea. His siblings included Penelope, Thoas, Damasippus, Imeusimus and Aletes. Another version changes the mother to Asterodia, daughter of Eurypylus, and adds other brothers such as Amasichus and Phalereus.

His best-known episode links him to the House of Atreus: he accused Orestes of murdering his cousin Clytemnestra, though the sources do not say how that case ended.

He should be distinguished from namesakes: a Perileos, son of Ancaeus of Samos, a Trojan defender killed by Neoptolemus, and a Perilaus credited with inventing the brazen bull.
