one of the Danaides, lover of Zeus

Isonoe

Isonoe is one of the Danaides, the daughters of Danaus, in Greek mythology. She is remembered for her bond with Zeus and for being turned into a spring after her death.

The myth

According to the Greek tradition, Isonoe was one of the Danaids, the fifty daughters of Danaus, and is best known as a lover of Zeus. From this union a son, Orchomenos, was born, although some accounts give him another name, Chryses. Her name also appears as Isione or Hesione, which sources present as variants of the same figure.

The end of her story tells that, after she died, the god turned her into a spring, so that her memory became linked to water. As with other figures of Greek mythology, the details of her life vary depending on the account consulted.
