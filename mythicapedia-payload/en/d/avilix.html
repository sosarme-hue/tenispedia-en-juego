Maya goddess of the moon and night

Awilix

Awilix is a moon and night goddess of the Postclassic K'iche' Maya of the Guatemalan highlands, though some scholars treat the deity as male. She was the patron of the Nijaib noble lineage in the capital Q'umarkaj.

The myth

According to the myth, Awilix was linked to the underworld, sickness and death, and was a patron of the Mesoamerican ballgame. Sources connect her with the eagle, the totem animal of her lineage, and with the jaguar, linked to her nocturnal side. She appears often in the Popol Vuh, where Ixbalanque, one of the Hero Twins, is presented as her incarnation.

Worship and religion

Her temple in Q'umarkaj stood facing that of Tohil across a plaza, and its imagery was dominated by eagles. The high priest, called Ajaw Awilix, was chosen from the Nijaib lineage. Today, Nijaib descendants in Momostenango honor syncretised forms of the goddess, said to be lovers of the town's patron saint, Santiago.

Legacy

She appears as an assassin in the video game Smite, among several Maya goddesses.

Ancient sources: The Popol Vuh and the Título de los Señores de Totonicapán both mention her.

Trivia

A heroic incarnation. According to the Popol Vuh, Ixbalanque, one of the Hero Twins, was an incarnation of Awilix.
Assassin in a video game. She appears as an assassin in the video game Smite, alongside other Maya goddesses.
