king of Gwynedd in Welsh mythology

Math fab Mathonwy

Math fab Mathonwy is a king of Gwynedd in Welsh mythology and the central figure of the fourth of the Four Branches of the Mabinogi. His defining trait was that he had to rest his feet in the lap of a virgin, unless he was at war, or he would die.

The myth

According to the tale, Math had to rest his feet in the lap of a maiden, his footholder, whenever he was not at war. His nephew Gilfaethwy became obsessed with Goewin, the woman who held this role, and his brother Gwydion, a magician and skilled storyteller, devised a trick to make her available. He persuaded Pryderi of Dyfed to trade pigs, an animal new to Wales, for horses and dogs he had conjured with magic. When the deception came out, Pryderi went to war, and while Math was away at battle Gilfaethwy raped Goewin. After Gwydion killed Pryderi in single combat, Math took Goewin as his wife to save her honour and punished his nephews by turning them into animals for three years.

Ancient sources: He appears in the Four Branches of the Mabinogi, medieval Welsh tales.
