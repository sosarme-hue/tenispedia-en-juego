legendary king of Arcadia

Aepytus

Aepytus was a legendary king of Arcadia, son of Hippothous and father of Cypselus, who succeeded him on the throne. His story matters because it places the arrival of Orestes in Arcadia during his reign, within the wider saga of the House of Atreus.

The myth

According to tradition, Aepytus inherited the throne from his father Hippothous and ruled Arcadia at a time when Orestes, acting on an oracle's advice, left Mycenae and settled in the region.

At Mantineia stood a sanctuary of Poseidon that, the story goes, no mortal was ever allowed to enter. Aepytus broke the sacred rule and crossed its threshold, and was at once struck blind. He died soon after, and the throne passed to his son Cypselus, who was, in turn, the great-grandfather of another Aepytus.
