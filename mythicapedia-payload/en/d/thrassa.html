heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Ares

Thrassa

Thrassa is a figure of Greek mythology, the daughter of Ares and Tereine and the mother of Polyphonte. Her lineage ties her to Thrace, the region the tradition associates with her husband and his family.

The myth

Thrassa was the daughter of Ares and Tereine, herself a daughter of the river god Strymon. She married Hipponous, son of Triballos, the eponym or god of the Thracian Triballoi, and they had a daughter, Polyphonte.

According to the myth, Polyphonte scorned the rites of Aphrodite and devoted herself to hunting in the mountains as a companion of Artemis. To punish her, the goddess of love made her fall in love with a bear and drove her mad. Polyphonte eventually coupled with a bear and bore the ursine giants Agrius and Oreios.
