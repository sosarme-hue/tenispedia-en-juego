Japanese goddess, wife of Ōkuninushi

Nunakawa-hime

Nunakawa-hime is a goddess in Japanese Shinto, wife of Ōkuninushi and mother of Takeminakata. She matters mainly for the story of her marriage, in which the union is sealed through an exchange of songs.

The myth

According to tradition, Ōkuninushi arrived at Nunakawa-hime's home and asked for her hand by singing her a song. She answered with a song of her own, and the two were married the next day.

Their union produced Takeminakata, who is named as the couple's son. The episode is brief and centres on how the two seek and accept each other, with sung words serving as the means of courtship rather than trials or heroic deeds.
