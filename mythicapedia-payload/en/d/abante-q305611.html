Greek centaur

Abas

Abas is a centaur from Greek mythology, son of Ixion and the cloud Nephele. He is best known for his role in the wedding of Pirithous and the clash between Lapiths and centaurs.

The myth

According to the tradition, Ixion tried to assault Hera, but Zeus tricked him by giving a cloud the appearance of the goddess. From that strange union came nearly all the centaurs, except Chiron and Pholus.

Like his brothers, Abas was invited to the wedding of Pirithous and Hippodamia. The feast ended in a violent fight between Lapiths and centaurs, in which several of them died. Afterwards, Abas and his brothers had to leave Thessaly and settled in the dense woods of Mount Pelion. The tale gives him no individual deeds, and his role blends into that of his brothers.
