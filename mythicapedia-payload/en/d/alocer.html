demon of medieval and early modern grimoires

Alocer

Alocer is a demon listed in several demonological grimoires, such as the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, the Liber Officiorum Spirituum and the Lesser Key of Solomon. He is described as a great duke of hell with traits of both a soldier and a lion.

The myth

The grimoires do not agree on his appearance or powers. In the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum he is the sixty-third spirit: a lion-headed soldier who breathes fire and rides a horse, sometimes with dragon's feet. He is credited with teaching astronomy and the liberal arts, granting familiars, and commanding thirty-six legions of demons. In the Liber Officiorum Spirituum he appears under other names, Allogor or Algor, as a spear-carrying knight who answers questions, advises on plans and commands thirty legions.

According to Thomas Rudd, he is opposed by the angel Imamiah. The Dictionnaire infernal describes him dressed as a knight, with a flushed complexion and fiery eyes, and says he makes those he protects happy within their families.

Legacy

He appears in the anime and manga series Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun as a lion-headed boy named Allocer Schneider. In the video game Romancing SaGa 3 he is one of the Four Sinistrals who rule the Abyss, lord of Earth.

Ancient sources: Demonological grimoires of the 17th and 18th centuries, such as the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, the Liber Officiorum Spirituum and the Lesser Key of Solomon.

Trivia

Shifting names. The same demon appears under different names depending on the grimoire: Allogor or Algor in the Liber Officiorum Spirituum, and Alloces or Allocen in other sources.
Two faces of one demon. Depending on the grimoire, he is either a lion-headed soldier or a spear-carrying knight, with very different powers.
