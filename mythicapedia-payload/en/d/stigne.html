Danaid of Greek mythology, daughter of Danaus

Stygne

Stygne was one of the Danaides of Greek mythology, daughter of King Danaus and Polyxo. Her story is among the darkest in the cycle, showing how obedience to a father led her to kill her own husband.

The myth

According to the tale, Danaus, who reigned first in Libya and later in Argos, ordered his daughters to marry their cousins, the sons of Aegyptus. Stygne obeyed and wed Polyctor, son of Aegyptus and Caliadne, who was both her cousin and her husband.

Tradition holds that, on her father's orders, Stygne eventually murdered Polyctor. The episode sets filial duty against violence toward a spouse, and it is one of the stories that give the tragic fate of the Danaides its meaning in Greek myth.
