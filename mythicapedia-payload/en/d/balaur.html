hydra-dragon of Romanian folklore

Balaur

The balaur is a creature of Romanian folklore, similar to a European dragon, with several serpent heads and often wings. In many tales it embodies evil and is typically defeated by a hero such as Făt-Frumos.

The myth

Folk accounts describe the balaur as so vast that, when it opens its jaws, one jaw touches the earth and the other the sky. It usually has three heads, though some tales give it seven or twelve, which is why it is called a hydra-dragon.

The tradition also tells how it comes into being: many serpents gather in a damp, lonely place and blow together until foam forms at their mouths. The foam turns into a diamond, and if a serpent swallows it and stays seven years in darkness unseen, it emerges as a balaur.

In folktales it typically seizes maidens or the princess, and the hero Făt-Frumos must defeat it to free her. Some versions have it guard treasure, make precious stones from its saliva, or dwell in a well demanding sacrifices, while others confuse it with the zmeu.

Legacy

The name was given to a theropod dinosaur, Balaur bondoc. It appears as an enemy in the games Aion and Wizard101, and in the 2020 series Dracula the Count uses the alias "Mr. Balaur".

Trivia

Stones from saliva. According to some traditions, the balaur's saliva can form precious stones, a belief that Mircea Eliade links to cultures from England to China.
Forgiveness for slaying. It is said that whoever manages to slay a balaur is forgiven one sin.
