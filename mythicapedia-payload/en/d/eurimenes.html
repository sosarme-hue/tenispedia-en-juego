prince of Pylos in Greek mythology

Eurymenes

Eurymenes is a prince of Pylos, son of King Neleus and Chloris, daughter of Amphion. His story is tied to the destruction of his family at the hands of Heracles during the sack of the city.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Eurymenes was one of the many sons King Neleus had with Chloris. His brothers included Pero, Taurus, Asterius, Pylaon, Deimachus, Eurybius, Epilaus, Phrasius, Evagoras, Alastor, Nestor and Periclymenus, a royal house of considerable size.

The story tells how Heracles attacked Pylos and sacked it. On that occasion he killed Neleus along with most of his sons, Eurymenes among them. Only Nestor survived, and the myth presents him as the single survivor of that generation.
