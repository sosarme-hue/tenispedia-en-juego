Egyptian fish god of weeping

Rem

Rem, also written Remi, is an Egyptian fish god associated with weeping. According to tradition, he is the personification of the tears of the god Ra, and his importance lies in the fertility his tears brought to the land.

The myth

According to the reference account, Rem lives in Rem-Rem, the realm of weeping, and his name is linked to the verb to weep. He is described as a fish god whose main deed was fertilizing the earth with his tears.

From those tears came vegetation and the reptiles. For this reason he is usually considered the personification of Ra's tears, and the stories name Ra as his father.
