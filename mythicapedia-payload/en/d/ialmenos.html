Greek hero, son of Ares

Ialmenus

Ialmenus was a hero of Greek mythology, a son of Ares and twin brother of Ascalaphus. He is remembered for sailing with the Argonauts, for his place among the suitors of Helen, and for fighting in the Trojan War, from which he alone of the two brothers returned alive. His story links two great cycles of heroic tales: the quest for the Golden Fleece and the siege of Troy.

The myth

According to tradition, Ialmenus was a son of Ares and Astyoche, daughter of Actor, although some versions name Pernis as his mother. He was the twin of Ascalaphus, and the two shared many adventures.

Both brothers sailed with the Argonauts and counted among the suitors of Helen. During the Trojan War they led the contingent from Orchomenus. Ascalaphus died in the fighting, while Ialmenus survived.

According to the account, he ended up in Colchis, where he founded a colony whose inhabitants were later called the 'Achaeans of Pontus'.
