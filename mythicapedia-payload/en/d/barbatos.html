Barbatos

Barbatos is a demon listed in early modern grimoires and occult texts, where he holds the titles of count and duke of Hell. He has no place in an ancient pantheon; his figure comes from Renaissance demonology and was passed down through occult literature.

The myth

In grimoire tradition, Barbatos commands thirty legions of demons, and four kings serve him as companions who lead his troops. He is credited with the power to understand the speech of animals, such as birdsong or dogs barking, and with knowledge of past and future events.

The lore also gives him influence over human affairs: he can win friends and the favour of rulers, and he is said to lead summoners to treasures hidden by magicians' enchantments, always at the price of the summoner's soul. According to the Book of Saint Cyprian, he is one of three demons under Satanachia, alongside Pruslas and Aamon; other versions make him an assistant to Astaroth or subordinate to Beelzebub. His name is linked to Latin barbatus, 'bearded', and he is often pictured as an elderly philosopher.

Legacy

Barbatos has found a second life in modern popular culture, especially in video games, comics and anime. He appears as an enemy or ally in the Shin Megami Tensei series, lends his name to the lead mobile suit of Mobile Suit Gundam: Iron-Blooded Orphans, and is the mentor of a character in Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun. In the DC universe he is a primeval villain in Dark Nights: Metal, and in Genshin Impact his name is given to the Anemo archon of Mondstadt.

Ancient sources: He appears in Renaissance grimoires such as The Lesser Key of Solomon and the Grand Grimoire, and in the Book of Saint Cyprian.

Trivia

A Latin-derived name. His name seems to derive from Latin barbatus, 'bearded', which fits his image as an elderly philosopher.
A fallen angel in tradition. According to The Lesser Key of Solomon, before belonging to the demons he was part of the angelic order of Virtues.
