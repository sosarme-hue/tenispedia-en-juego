lavish abandoned house in Japanese folklore

Mayoiga

The mayoiga is, in Japanese folklore, a lavish and well-kept but abandoned house found in remote mountain wilderness. Its story became one of the best-known legends of the Tōno region.

The myth

According to the legend, the mayoiga, or Mayoi-ya, is a mysterious house that bestows wealth and honour on everyone who visits. A visitor may take any one item from inside, but not everyone can enjoy this blessing.

The folklorist Kunio Yanagita introduced the tale in chapters 63 and 64 of Tales of Tono (1910), based on a story told to him by Kiyoshi Sasaki, a native of Tsuchibuchi Village in Iwate Prefecture. In one account, the wife of the Miura family prospers because she was selfless; in another, a young man who guided a greedy villager loses all his wealth.

Legacy

The novel The House of the Lost on the Cape by Sachiko Kashiwaba takes its title house's name, Mayoiga, from this folklore.
