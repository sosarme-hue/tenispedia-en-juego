Greek nymph of the Underworld

Orphne

Orphne is a nymph in Greek mythology who, according to tradition, lived in Hades, the realm of the dead. She is also known as Styx or Gorgyra, and her name has been linked to the Roman goddess Caligo, whose name means darkness.

The myth

In the myth, Orphne lives in Hades. She is the partner of Acheron, the river of the underworld, and with him she became the mother of Ascalaphus.

Her connection with the name Styx adds complexity to her figure, since that name is also associated with another character of the underworld. The accounts do not fully agree on these details, which reflects the variety of traditions about the inhabitants of the Greek underworld.
