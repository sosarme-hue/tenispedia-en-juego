Hindu goddess, daughter of Daksha and wife of Bhrigu

Khyati

Khyati is a goddess in Hinduism, one of the daughters of the patriarch Daksha and his wife Prasuti. She matters mainly as the wife of the sage Bhrigu and the mother of Lakshmi, the goddess of fortune and prosperity.

The myth

According to tradition, Daksha had twenty-four daughters with his wife Prasuti, and Khyati was one of them. Her sisters include Sati and Anasuya. The sources do not agree on how many children Daksha had: another version credits him with sixty-two more daughters, born to his wife Panchajani, also known as Virini or Virani.

Khyati married the sage Bhrigu, and the couple had two sons, Dhata and Vidhata, and a daughter, Lakshmi.
