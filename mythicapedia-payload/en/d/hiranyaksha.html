asura, brother of Hiranyakashipu in Hindu mythology

Hiranyaksha

Hiranyaksha is an asura of Hindu mythology, described as a demon king who submerged the earth and terrorised the three worlds. He matters chiefly because his defeat at the hands of Varaha, the boar avatar of Vishnu, gives rise to one of the best-known accounts of the rescue of the earth goddess.
