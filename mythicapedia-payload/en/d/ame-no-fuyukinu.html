Japanese deity of legend

Ame no Fuyukinu

Ame no Fuyukinu is a figure of Japanese legend, a fifth-generation descendant of Susanoo and the father of Ōkuninushi. He matters mainly because he connects the line of Susanoo with the god who would later rule the land.

The myth

According to tradition, Susanoo ordered him to deliver a sword to Takamagahara, the heavenly realm. He was the son of Omizunu and Futemimi, and he married Sashikuniwaka-hime, with whom he had Ōkuninushi and the so-called Eighty Gods.

His story is brief and centres on the sword and on his place in a genealogy that links him to Susanoo, without major episodes of adventure.

Worship and religion

Legend holds that he founded Hinomisaki Shrine.

Legacy

The Ono clan claims descent from him.

Trivia

Hinomisaki Shrine. Legend credits this deity with founding Hinomisaki Shrine.
Ono clan. The Ono clan claims descent from him, linking a family to the myth.
