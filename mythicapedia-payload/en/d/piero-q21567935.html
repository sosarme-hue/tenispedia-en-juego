king of Emathia and eponym of Pieria

Pierus

Pierus is a king of Emathia in Macedonia, eponym of the region of Pieria and Mount Pierus. Tradition credits him with being the first to write in praise of the Muses.

The myth

According to Marsyas of Pella, Pierus was a son of Makednos by a local woman and brother of Amathus. Solinus, however, makes him unrelated to Makednos and older than him. The Suda calls him a son of Linus and father of Oeagrus, making him grandfather of the musician Orpheus, while Antoninus Liberalis says he sprang from the soil. His wife is usually the nymph Methone, though some call her his sister.

Most accounts give him the Pierides as daughters, born to Antiope or Euippe. His nine daughters, the Emathides, took the names of the Muses and challenged them to a contest. Defeated, they were transformed into birds.

Worship and religion

According to Pausanias, Pierus migrated from Thrace to Boeotia and established the worship of the Muses at Thespiae.

Ancient sources: The ancient references come from Marsyas of Pella, Solinus, the Suda, Antoninus Liberalis and Pausanias.

Trivia

Grandfather of Orpheus. The Suda makes him the father of Oeagrus, which makes him the grandfather of the musician Orpheus.
First poet of the Muses. Tradition credits him with being the first to write in praise of the Muses.
