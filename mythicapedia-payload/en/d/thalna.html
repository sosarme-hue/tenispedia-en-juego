Etruscan goddess of birth and childbirth

Thalna

Thalna is, in Etruscan myth, the goddess of births and childbirth and the wife of Tinia. She is often identified with Uni, whose Greek and Roman equivalents were Hera and Juno.

The myth

According to Etruscan tradition, Thalna was the wife of Tinia, the chief god of the pantheon. Her domain was birth and labour, and she was shown as a young woman.

Her gender is not always fixed: in some scenes she appears as a male figure, or seems to be identified as one because of how the names are placed around the image. Besides childbirth, she was also linked with friendship and prophecy.
