Navajo creation spirit

Changing Woman

Asdzą́ą́ Nádleehé, usually called Changing Woman in English, is one of the creation spirits of Navajo tradition. She is considered the mother of the first Navajo people and of the hero twins, and her figure is linked to life, renewal and youth.

The myth

According to Navajo tradition, Asdzą́ą́ Nádleehé created the Navajo people from the old skin of her own body. Using the soil from her mountain soil bundle, a bag of four pieces of buckskin that her father had brought from the underworld, she made four couples, the ancestors of the four original Navajo clans.

She also helped create the sky and the earth. With the Sun she had the twins Monster Slayer and Born for Water, who feature in many stories. Her parents were Long Life Boy and Happiness Girl, who represent the means by which all life passes through time.

Worship and religion

She is associated with a young Navajo woman's entry into puberty, marked by a four-day rite. She is also celebrated in the Blessing Way, a Navajo prayer ceremony that seeks good fortune and long life.
