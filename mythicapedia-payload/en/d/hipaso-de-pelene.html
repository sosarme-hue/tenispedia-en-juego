Hippasus of Pelene

Hippasus was a ruler of the polis of Pelene, in Achaea, who appears in Greek myth mainly as the father of several Argonauts. His genealogy varies across traditions, reflecting the many versions of the stories about the heroes of Jason's expedition.

The myth

According to the tradition preserved in the sources, Hippasus has no adventures of his own; his importance lies in his offspring. He is said possibly to have been a son of Pelops and Hippodamia, though this parentage is only a hypothesis, and he probably belonged to the line of the Aeolids because of his close connection with the Argonauts.

Among his sons, the accounts name Asterius of Pelene and his brother Amphion, though other authors make them sons of Hyperasius. Actor also appears, perhaps identified with the son of Deion, along with Iphitus of Phocis and Naubolus, also of Phocis, whose father in other versions was Ornytus. A different tradition presents him as a son of Ceyx and a companion of Heracles, and gives him a son named Actor.
