Minyan prince, son of Clymenus

Azeus

Azeus is a Minyan prince in Greek mythology, the youngest son of King Clymenus of Orchomenus. He matters mainly as the father of King Actor and grandfather of two Orchomenian leaders who fought in the Trojan War.

The myth

According to tradition, Azeus was the youngest son of Clymenus and Bouzyge, probably a daughter of Lycus, although some accounts give his mother the name Boudeia. His brothers included Erginus, Stratius, Arrhon and Pyleus, and his sisters Eurydice and Axia.

The myth tells that Clymenus was killed by the Thebans during a festival of the Onchestian Poseidon. To avenge him, Azeus marched against Thebes with his brothers, under the command of Erginus.

Azeus's line continued through his son, King Actor. Actor's daughter Astyoche bore children to Ares, namely Ascalaphus and Ialmenus, who led the Orchomenians in the Trojan War.
