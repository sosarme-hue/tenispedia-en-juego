Russian epic hero of the byliny, famed for his elegance

Churilo Plenkovich

Churilo Plenkovich is a hero of the byliny, the Russian oral epic poems, belonging to the Kievan cycle, also called the Galician cycle. He is remembered above all for his elegance, as the tales present him as the best-dressed man in all of Russia.

The myth

According to the oral tradition preserved in the byliny, Churilo, also spelled Churila Plyonkovich or Plenkovich, is a figure of the Kievan court who is known more for his appearance than for his deeds. The tales describe him as the best-dressed man in all of Russia.

His best-known episode places him at a wedding, where he appears among the guests and draws attention through his finery. His presence in these stories helps paint a picture of court life and the feasts of the Russian heroes.
