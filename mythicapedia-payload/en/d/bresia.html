Cypriot princess in Greek mythology

Bresia

Bresia is a Cypriot princess in Greek mythology, daughter of King Cinyras. Her story is part of the tale of her family, shaped by Aphrodite's revenge.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Bresia was a daughter of King Cinyras, ruler of Cyprus. Her mother, Metharme, daughter of Pygmalion, rashly claimed that her daughter Smyrna, also called Myrrha, was even more beautiful than the goddess Aphrodite. To take revenge, the goddess made Smyrna fall in love with her own father.

Bresia and her sisters Laogore and Orsedice also suffered the consequences of the punishment: they joined with foreigners and set off for Egypt. Her brothers are also named as Adonis and Myrrha. The sources do not detail what became of them in Egypt.
