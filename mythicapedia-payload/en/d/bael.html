first king of hell in the grimoires

Bael

Bael is a demon of grimoire demonology, described as the first king of hell and ruler of the East. Some writers suspect his figure descends from the ancient Canaanite god Baal, an idea that remains debated.

The myth

In grimoires such as the Ars Goetia and the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, Bael appears as the first infernal king and as one of the spirits who rule the East. The sources disagree on his power: some give him sixty-six legions of demons, others eighty-two, totalling about 456,000 demons. According to the Book of Saint Cyprian, he is subordinate to Lucifuge Rofocale, and the Grand Grimoire places him in the same position.

His appearance also varies by source. Tradition shows him with three heads, of a toad, a crowned man and a cat, with a hoarse voice and a sturdy torso ending in spider legs. Those who summoned him were said to gain the power to become invisible and the cunning to use it. He is described as wise, frank and sober, and as one who never lies. His power is also believed to grow in October, especially during Samhain, the Celtic festival.

Legacy

He appears in the grotesque horror story The Nazarene of the White Cat by the Costa Rican writer Ariel F. Cambronero Zumbado, and in the Mexican telenovela El maleficio, where he possesses the protagonist. In the video game Genshin Impact, the god Makoto goes by the name Baal, and in Diablo II: Lord of Destruction he is the main antagonist. He also appears in the film The Rite (2011) and in the anime series Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun.

Ancient sources: He does not appear in ancient texts. His presence comes from later grimoires of demonology, such as the Ars Goetia, the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum and the Book of Saint Cyprian.

Trivia

Possible Canaanite origin. The writer Jacques Collin de Plancy wondered whether Bael was the same as the Canaanite god Baal, and considered this a reasonable assumption.
A king with fewer legions. The Sloane MS 3824 gives him 250 legions, while other versions list different numbers altogether.
