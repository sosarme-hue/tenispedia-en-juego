heroine of Greek myth, daughter of Nyctimus

Phylonome

Phylonome is a heroine of Greek myth, daughter of Nyctimus, king of Arcadia, and mother of the twins Parrhasius and Lycastus, fathered by Ares according to the story. Her tale is woven into myths about Arcadia's early kings.

The myth

According to the myth, Phylonome was the daughter of Nyctimus and a member of Artemis's retinue, roaming the woods on hunts with the goddess. Ares, disguised as a shepherd, seduced her; when she became pregnant she fled to the forest of Erymanthus, where she gave birth to twins. Fearing her father, she threw the babies into the river.

The newborns were carried to a hollow oak, where a she-wolf with cubs of her own nursed them. They were found by the shepherd Telephus, or Gilifus in other versions, who raised them under the names Parrhasius and Lycastus. Years later, after the transformation of Arcas, the twins inherited their grandfather's throne.
