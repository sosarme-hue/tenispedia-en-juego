Hindu queen, earthly avatar of Lakshmi

Archi

Archi is a Hindu queen regarded as an earthly avatar of the goddess Lakshmi and the consort of King Prithu. Her story appears in the Bhagavata Purana, which describes her bond with the king and her end. A minor but striking figure of Hindu mythology, she is remembered mainly for her role as a devoted wife.

The myth

According to the Bhagavata Purana, Archi emerged from the body of King Vena, just as Prithu did. Both are incarnations: she of Lakshmi and he of Vishnu.

As a consort, she followed her husband into the forest when he took up sannyasa, a life of renunciation. When Prithu died, the tradition holds that Archi, dutifully, immolated herself on her husband's funeral pyre. The account presents her loyalty as an example of devotion to her husband. It thus places her beside Prithu from their origin to the end of their lives.

Ancient sources: The Bhagavata Purana presents her in its account of Vena and Prithu.
