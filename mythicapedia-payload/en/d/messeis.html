Greek nymph of a spring near Argos

Messeis

Messeis is a nymph of Greek mythology, a naiad associated with a spring linked to the city of Argos in the Peloponnese. A daughter of Inachus, she belonged to the Inachides, alongside her sisters Io, Amymone and Hyperia.

The myth

According to tradition, Messeis was one of the Inachides, the nymphs born to Inachus. She shared her lineage with her sister Io, whose stories are better known, and with Amymone and Hyperia.

As a naiad, she was imagined as the spirit of a spring tied to the territory of Argos. The available texts give few details about her life, so her role in myth is mostly limited to her family ties and her connection to the spring she inhabited.
