yōkai spirit of poverty in Japanese mythology

Ao-nyōbō

Ao-nyōbō is a yōkai from Japanese mythology, a spirit of poverty and misfortune that appears as an ancient court lady. Its name, meaning "blue lady", refers to inexperience rather than to the colour of its skin.

The myth

According to popular Japanese tradition, the Ao-nyōbō is a blue-skinned, ogre-like spirit that takes the form of an ancient court noblewoman. She wears many layers of old kimono, now torn and moth-eaten, and her face is white, with high painted eyebrows and blackened teeth.

Court ladies served in the palaces of noble families until they married a worthy suitor. The Ao-nyōbō lives in abandoned houses once owned by fallen nobles and ruined families, where she constantly applies her makeup and fixes her hair, waiting for a lover who has lost interest or a husband who has abandoned his wife. No guest ever arrives. If an intruder appears, she devours them and goes back to waiting in vain.

Legacy

The Ao-nyōbō appears in the television series Shuriken Sentai Ninninger, where the villain Ariake no Kata is based on her. In Power Rangers Ninja Steel she is adapted as "Badonna".

Trivia

An insulting name. The term was originally used disparagingly for court ladies who failed to marry well or escape poverty, hence the comparison with the "ugly stepsisters".
