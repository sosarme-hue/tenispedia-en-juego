one of the Graces of Greek mythology

Auxo

Auxo is, in Greek mythology, one of the Graces, figures associated with charm and grace, according to a local tradition of Athens. Her name means "the one who makes grow" and relates to the growth of plants.

The myth

According to the tradition, Auxo represented spring, while her companion Hegemone embodied autumn. Some versions make her a daughter of Zeus and Themis, although ancient texts do not always agree on her genealogy.

Hyginus, by contrast, counts her among the Horae, the goddesses of the seasons and natural order. That the same figure appears in two different groups shows how fluid the tradition was in grouping these goddesses linked to nature.

Worship and religion

In Athens she was honoured alongside Hegemone, and an ancient passage states that the Athenians had paid them tribute since early times. The sources do not describe specific temples or rites, and there is no evidence about the survival of her cult.

Ancient sources: Hyginus mentions her among the Horae, and an ancient passage on Athenian worship names her among the Graces.

Trivia

A pair of Graces. Auxo and Hegemone appear together in Athenian worship, honoured as two Graces since early times.
A misplaced name. The ancient passage states that the name Carpo belongs to a Hora rather than a Grace, showing how these figures were confused.
