Eurymedusa

Eurymedusa is a young woman in Greek mythology, linked to Arcadia, who became the mother of the hero Myrmidon. Her story, in which Zeus disguises himself as an ant, explains the name of her son.

The myth

According to tradition, Eurymedusa was the daughter of Clitor of Arcadia, though her true father was said to be the river god Achelous. Her mother is not known. Zeus, who came down from Olympus to be with her, transformed himself into an ant.

Perhaps the young woman was also turned into an ant. From this union Myrmidon was born, whose name relates to myrmēx, the Greek word for «ant», because of that trick.

Another version says that her husband was called Myrmex or Myrmeco, and that Zeus took that form to join with her. However it happened, the etymology of the name Myrmidon remains the same.
