eighth Manu in Hindu mythology

Savarni Manu

Savarni Manu is the eighth Manu in Hindu mythology, the first man of an age known as the manvantara. He belongs to the fourteen Manus that, according to the Vishnu Purana, succeed one another across the cosmic cycles of time.

The myth

According to the Puranas, the Manu of the present age is Vaivasvata Manu, the seventh to hold the title. He will be succeeded by his half-brother Sarvabhauma, who will be called Savarni Manu.

Tradition holds that he was born to Surya, the god of the sun, and Chhaya, one of his consorts. The authorship of the Brahma Vaivarta Purana is attributed to this Manu, who recounts it to the sage Narada.

According to the Srimad Bhagavatam, his sons were Nirmoka, Virojaksha and other unnamed sons. His reign would be ruled by Bali, son of Virochana, and its seven sages would include Galava, Diptiman, Ashvatthama, Kripa, Rishyashringa, Vadrayana and Parashurama.

Worship and religion

During his reign, the gods Surya and Vishnu were said to be venerated.

Ancient sources: Vishnu Purana, Brahma Vaivarta Purana and Srimad Bhagavatam.
