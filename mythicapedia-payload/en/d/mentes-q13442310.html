king of the Cicones in Greek mythology

Mentes

Mentes is, in Greek mythology, the king of the Cicones. He is remembered mainly because, in the Iliad, the god Apollo takes his appearance to speak to Hector in the middle of battle.

The myth

In Book XVII of the Iliad, Apollo disguises himself as Mentes to urge Hector to face Menelaus. He mocks him: Hector is chasing horses he will never catch, those of Achilles, descendant of Aeacus, which no one but Achilles can control, since an immortal mother gave him birth. Meanwhile, Menelaus, son of Atreus, standing beside Patroclus, has just killed Euphorbus, son of Panthous, the best of the Trojans.

The disguise works as a narrative device: the god acts without revealing himself and pushes the Trojan hero into the fight at a decisive moment of the battle.

Ancient sources: Homer's Iliad, Book XVII.
