Oceanid of Greek mythology

Cerceis

Cerceis is one of the Oceanids, nymph daughters of the sea Titans Oceanus and Tethys, in Greek mythology. She belongs to the large line of sea nymphs born to that couple, and she is mainly known for the meaning of her name and for her beauty.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Cerceis was one of the Oceanids, nymph daughters of the sea Titans Oceanus and Tethys. As a member of this family of sea divinities, her origin placed her in the watery world.

Her name admits two readings: one links it to the weaving shuttle, the other to the idea of a gorgeous form. Tradition describes her mainly through her loveliness and bearing, without assigning her deeds or episodes of her own in the available references.
