hero of Greek mythology, father of Argonauts

Hyperasius

Hyperasius is a hero of Greek mythology from Pellene in Achaea, son of Pellen, the eponymous founder of that city. He matters mainly as the father of the Argonauts Asterius and Amphion.

The myth

According to the tradition preserved in the sources, Hyperasius came from Pellene, the Achaean city founded by his father Pellen and named after him. He appears as a link in the local genealogy rather than as the protagonist of great deeds.

His wife was Hypso, of whom little else is known. With her he fathered the twins Asterius and Amphion, who according to some accounts sailed with the Argonauts, though the texts say little about their adventures.

Other traditions give the father of these two men a different name, Hippasus. It is unclear whether this is the same figure under another name or a separate character in different versions of the myth.
