wife of Amythaon, mother of Melampus and Bias

Idomene

Idomene is a figure of Greek myth, wife of Amythaon and mother of Melampus and Bias. She matters mainly because Apollodorus gives two different accounts of her father.

The myth

In the tradition preserved by Apollodorus in his Library, Idomene is the wife of Amythaon and the mother of their children, at least Bias and Melampus. The story says little about her life but more about her ancestry, and here the author wavers. First he says she was a daughter of Pheres; later he makes her a daughter of Abas, without giving any reason for the change.

No ancient author names her mother, so her figure is defined almost entirely by her marriage and her descendants within a family of heroes.

Ancient sources: Apollodorus's Library, which records her as the wife of Amythaon and mother of Bias and Melampus.

Trivia

Two fathers. Apollodorus makes her a daughter of Pheres in one passage and of Abas in another, without explaining the change.
