Māori god of the sea and waters

Tangaroa

Tangaroa is the Māori god of the sea, lakes, rivers and the creatures that live in them, especially fish. He belongs to the wider Polynesian tradition, where related figures such as Tangaloa appear, and he is counted among the major gods.

The myth

In the most widespread Māori version, Tangaroa is the son of Rangi, the sky, and Papa, the earth. Together with his brothers Rongo, Tū, Haumia and Tāne, he helped force their parents apart, as they were locked in an embrace. Afterwards he was attacked by Tāwhirimātea, the god of storms, and had to hide in the sea.

From then on he became the father of many sea creatures. His son Punga had two children: Ikatere, ancestor of the fish, and Tū-te-wehiwehi, ancestor of the reptiles. Terrified by Tāwhirimātea, the fish sought refuge in the sea and the reptiles in the forests. For this reason Tangaroa holds a grudge against Tāne, the god of forests, for giving shelter to his runaway children.

Another version, that of the Kāi Tahu, makes Tangaroa the son of Temoretu. Papa was unfaithful to Rangi and, in the fight that followed, Tangaroa pierced Rangi's thighs with his spear. Papa and Rangi then married.

Worship and religion

The Māori regarded the sea as Tangaroa's domain, so offerings were made to him before fishing trips or voyages. In the Cook Islands he was depicted as a male figure with a swollen belly and a prominent genital organ, a symbol of fertility. As Tangaroa-whakamau-tai, he was also credited with control over the tides.

Legacy

In the Cook Islands, wooden carvings of Tangaroa remain very popular. His name has relatives in other languages: in Hawaii the god of the ocean is called Kanaloa, and in Vanuatu related religious terms such as taŋaroa appear.

Trivia

A god who can be a whale. In some places Tangaroa is not shown in human form but as a whale.
The fire of Manihiki. In the tradition of Manihiki, Tangaroa is the origin of fire, and Māui goes to him to obtain it for humankind. After a struggle, Māui kills him and then brings him back to life with incantations.
