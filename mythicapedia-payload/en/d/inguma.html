Basque god of dreams and nightmares

Inguma

Inguma is the god of dreams in Basque mythology and religion. He is regarded as a malevolent force that slips into houses at night and torments residents with nightmares, sometimes killing them.

The myth

According to Basque tradition, Inguma enters houses during the night to torment those who sleep. His visits bring nightmares and, in some accounts, can end the lives of his victims. In the Baigorri area he is known as Mauma, a variant that shows how the figure went by different names depending on the village.

The sources consulted do not describe his origin or family, so his character is defined mainly by his nighttime role and by the threat he poses to anyone asleep.

Legacy

Inguma appears in the third film of Dolores Redondo's Baztán trilogy, where his presence adds to the story's unsettling atmosphere.
