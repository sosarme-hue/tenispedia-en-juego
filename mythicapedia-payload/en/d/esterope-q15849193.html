Sterope

Sterope is a princess of Greek mythology, daughter of King Cepheus of Tegea in Arcadia. Her story is known for the gift from Heracles that helped her defend her city.

The myth

According to the tale, King Cepheus set out to fight alongside Heracles against the sons of Hippocoon and left his daughter in charge of Tegea's defence. Before leaving, the hero gave her a bronze vessel containing a lock of the Gorgon Medusa's hair.

Sterope's task was simple: climb onto the city walls and shake the vessel three times. When she did, the enemies fled in terror, and the city was saved without a battle.

Thanks to this gesture, Heracles won the friendship of Cepheus, who kept a bond of loyalty with him after the victory. The name Sterope comes from the Greek steropē, meaning 'lightning'.
