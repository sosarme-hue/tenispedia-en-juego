water creature of Galician and Asturian folklore

Perro de Urco

The Perro de Urco is a creature of Galician and Asturian folklore: a huge black dog with great horns and ears that rises from the sea dragging chains. Its appearance is considered an ill omen, often a sign of an imminent death.

The myth

According to tradition, the animal emerges from the sea in the form of an enormous black dog, with large horns and ears, dragging chains that rattle along the coast. Whoever sees it usually takes it as a dire portent, often read as a warning of a death soon to come.

Its traits resemble those of British water spirits such as the kelpie, which according to tales lured unwary people to drown them, or whose mere sighting was also taken as a bad omen. In Galicia it is known as Urco, can do Urco and Can do Mar, while in Asturias it is called Güercu; the name Huerco also appears in the sources.
