giant of Antwerp folklore

Druon Antigoon

Druon Antigoon is a mythical giant from Flemish folklore who, according to tradition, lived in Antwerp. His legend, according to folklore, explains the origin of the city's name.

The myth

Antigoon guarded a bridge over the river Scheldt and demanded a toll from anyone who wanted to cross. Those who refused had one of their hands severed and thrown into the water.

The tale ends when Brabo, a young Roman soldier, slays the giant. Brabo cuts off the giant's own hand and flings it into the river, putting an end to his abuses.

Legacy

In the square in front of Antwerp's town hall, a statue shows Brabo holding the giant's severed hand. According to folklore, the legend explains the city's name, Antwerpen, which supposedly comes from Dutch hand werpen, meaning to throw a hand.

In art

Trivia

A name with a hand. Dutch hand werpen is related to Old English hand and wearpan, meaning to throw. Over time the verb evolved into today's warp.
