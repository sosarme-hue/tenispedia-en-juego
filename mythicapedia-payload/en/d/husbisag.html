Sumerian goddess of the netherworld

Hušbišag

Hušbišag is a netherworld goddess of Sumerian mythology, wife of Namtar and mother of a daughter called Hemdikug. She appears mainly in the Epic of Gilgamesh, where she is described as a handmaid of the dark gods.

The myth

In the Standard Akkadian version of the Epic of Gilgamesh, Hušbišag appears after the death of Enkidu, the hero's companion. Grieving his friend, Gilgamesh offers her a golden necklace so that she will welcome him in the Netherworld and walk by his side there.

In his plea, Gilgamesh asks the gods to accept the offering and welcome Enkidu, so that his friend may not be sick at heart in death. In the sources consulted, the goddess's role is limited to this episode, with no further deeds of her own.

Ancient sources: The Epic of Gilgamesh, in its Standard Akkadian version (tablet VIII).

Trivia

A necklace for the underworld. Gilgamesh does not offer the necklace for himself but for his dead friend, asking the gods to welcome him.
A little-known daughter. The sources name her as the daughter of Hušbišag and Namtar, though almost nothing more is said about her.
