female warrior of the Trojan War

Epipole of Carystus

Epipole is a figure of Greek mythology, the daughter of Trachion of Carystus in Euboea, who disguised herself as a man to join the Greeks in their war against Troy. Her story is little known and survives only through a brief account preserved in late sources.

The myth

According to the account handed down by Ptolemaeus Chennus, Epipole was the daughter of a man named Trachion, from Carystus on the island of Euboea. Determined to take part in the war against Troy, she disguised herself as a man and joined the Greek army.

The tradition holds that Palamedes, a hero known for his cleverness, discovered she was a woman. Once her true sex was revealed, the Greek soldiers stoned her to death.

Ancient sources: Her story survives in a passage by Ptolemaeus Chennus, quoted by Photios in his Bibliotheca (cod. 190).
