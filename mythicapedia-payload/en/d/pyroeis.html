Greek god of the planet Mars

Pyroeis

Pyroeis is, in ancient Greek religion, the god of the wandering star Areios, identified with the planet Mars. He matters because he personifies one of the five planets visible to the naked eye that Greek astronomers observed.

The myth

According to tradition, Pyroeis was the son of Astreus and Eos, the dawn. His name comes from the Greek word for 'fire' and means 'the fiery one'. He is also known as Mesonyx, meaning 'midnight'.

Astronomers of the time gave these 'planets' names, linked them to different gods and attributed qualities to their apparent behaviour in the sky. Pyroeis was the god of Areios, the wandering star we now call Mars. Like the other planetary deities, he is understood within this way of ordering the heavens, where the planet's changing motion was observed with close attention.

Trivia

An alternative name. Besides Pyroeis, the god is also called Mesonyx, meaning 'midnight'.
Five planets to the naked eye. Greek astronomers knew five planets visible to the naked eye, and each was assigned a god and certain qualities.
