Haitian Vodou loa of life and death

Maman Brigitte

Maman Brigitte is a loa of Haitian Vodou, rooted in African traditions, especially Yoruba, and linked to the cycle of life and death. She is the consort of Baron Samedi and the mother of the Guédé spirits.

The myth

In Haitian tradition, Maman Brigitte watches over the souls of the newly born and guides those who die. Her husband, Baron Samedi, is unfaithful to her with mortal women. She is imagined as a very young woman with a face painted white like a skull, a bridal gown and bare feet. At night she wanders through cemeteries, drinks rum with hot pepper and decorates her hair with flowers, where butterflies land.

Some versions say that, tired of these betrayals, she has tied a blindfold over her eyes, since Life and Death are blind.

Worship and religion

She is venerated across Haiti. When a woman becomes pregnant, black roosters are sacrificed in her honour so that she will look after the child.

Legacy

She appears in the 1988 cyberpunk novel Mona Lisa Overdrive, in the video game Cyberpunk 2077 and in the TV series American Gods.

Trivia

A white loa. Maman Brigitte is one of the few loa depicted as white.
Graves of women. The first graves of women are considered offerings dedicated to her, while those of men are dedicated to Baron Samedi.
