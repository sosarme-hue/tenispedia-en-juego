Hindu folk goddess of South India

Maisamma

Maisamma, also known as Mesai or Mesko, is a folk goddess of Hinduism revered as a mother in South India. Her devotion is concentrated in the rural areas of Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra.

The myth

No extensive mythical narrative about Maisamma has been fixed in ancient texts. Her figure lives mostly in oral tradition and local devotion, where she is seen as a protective mother of her community. For this reason, the stories told about her may vary from place to place, and her maternal character is the trait that recurs most often.

Worship and religion

She is mainly venerated as a mother goddess in the rural areas of South India, especially in Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra. She is also called Amma in Telugu and Aai in Marathi, both meaning "mother", and her worship remains alive among her devotees.
