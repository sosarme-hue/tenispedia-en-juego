wife of Megareus and mother of Hippomenes

Merope of Megara

In Greek mythology, Merope was the wife of King Megareus of Boeotia and the mother of Hippomenes. Her husband gave his name to the city of Megara.

The myth

According to tradition, Merope was the wife of King Megareus, and the city of Megara was named in his honour. Together they had Hippomenes, a young man who would later face an unusual challenge.

Atalanta, a warrior who wished to remain a virgin, asked her father that her suitors compete with her in a race. If they caught her, she had the right to kill them; if they beat her, she would marry the winner. Hippomenes won through a clever trick: with Aphrodite's help he carried golden apples, dropping them along the course, and Atalanta stopped to pick them up, losing her lead.

Trivia

The golden apples. The apples that decided the race were a gift from Aphrodite to Hippomenes. Atalanta could not resist stopping to pick them up.
