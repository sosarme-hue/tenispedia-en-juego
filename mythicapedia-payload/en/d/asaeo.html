Achaean warrior of Greek mythology

Asaeus

Asaeus is an Achaean warrior of Greek mythology who, according to tradition, was killed in the Trojan War by the Trojan hero Hector. His story is very brief, but it illustrates the many nameless fighters that accounts of Troy mention.

The myth

According to tradition, Asaeus was an Achaean warrior, one of the Greeks who fought against Troy. During the war, the conflict between the Achaeans and the city of Ilion, he fell in combat at the hands of Hector, son of King Priam and Queen Hecuba. Hector was Troy's chief defender and one of the most feared warriors in the story.

Very little survives about this figure: his Greek name, Ἀσαῖον, and the fact of his death. The sources offer no details about his family, his origins or the circumstances of the fight, so he remains little more than a name within the great Trojan cycle.
