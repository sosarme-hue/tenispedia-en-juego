horse of Manannán and Lugh in Irish mythology

Enbarr

Enbarr (also Aonbharr) is a marvellous horse from the Irish Mythological Cycle, able to cross both land and sea and faster than the wind. It belonged to the sea god Manannán mac Lir, who lent it to Lugh, and it matters because it shows the bond between sea deities and heroes in Celtic tradition.

The myth

In the tale Oidheadh Chloinne Tuireann (The Fate of the Children of Tuireann), the Tuatha Dé Danann, oppressed by the tribute imposed by the Fomorians, gathered an assembly on a hill. Lugh arrived riding Enbarr at the head of an army the tradition calls the "Fairy Cavalcade from the Land of Promise". The horse was swifter than the "naked cold wind of spring" and could travel over land or sea with equal ease. Whoever sat on its back could not be killed, and Lugh also wore armour lent by Manannán, which added to his invulnerability.

Later, the sons of Tuireann asked to borrow the horse. Lugh refused, arguing that it would be a loan of a loan. Yet by refusing he was eventually forced to lend them instead the self-navigating boat Sguaba Tuinne, the "Wave-sweeper".

Legacy

Enbarr appears in the 2013 video game Final Fantasy XIV, where it can be obtained as a random drop. It is also a minor character in Shayne Silvers' Nate Temple series, where it is the horse that removes the protagonist from the Otherworld and takes her to Fae. An Irish company producing equine health management software, StablePro, takes its name from the horse. In the 2019 video game Fire Emblem: Three Houses, "Enbarr" is the Imperial Capital of the Adrestian Empire.

Ancient sources: It appears in Oidheadh Chloinne Tuireann, a medieval Irish tale of the Mythological Cycle, where the forms Aenbharr and Aonbharr occur.
