hero who founded Hermione in Greek myth

Hermion

Hermion is a figure of Greek myth, credited as the founder of the city of Hermione on the southeastern coast of the Peloponnese. Son of Europs, he belongs to the heroic tradition of the region.

The myth

According to the tradition, Hermion was the brother of Telchis. His story is mainly linked to the founding of Hermione, a city near Troezen on the southeastern coast of the Peloponnese.

The city was later settled by the Dorians, a Greek people who established themselves in the area. Hermion thus appears as the legendary origin of a community that became a notable settlement in the Peloponnese, and his name became linked to the city he founded.

Worship and religion

Hermione became a major centre of worship of Demeter after Chthonia, Hermion's aunt, settled there.
