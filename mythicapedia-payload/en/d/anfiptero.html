winged serpent of European heraldry

Amphiptere

The amphiptere is a winged serpent found in European heraldry, described with bat-like or bird-like wings depending on the source. Its name, of Greek origin, refers to its two pairs of wings, though it appears mainly as a heraldic device rather than in ancient myth.

The myth

Heraldic and European folklore sources describe the amphiptere as a legless, serpentine dragon with large wings. Some accounts give it light, sunrise-colored feathers, greenish tones and a long tail like a wyvern's, while others picture it entirely feathered, with a spiked tail, bird-like wings and a beaked snout.

Its appearance shifts from one account to another, and no single tale fixes its origin or its deeds.

Legacy

It was used rarely in heraldry, appearing on the arms of the House of Potier and as supporters for the Dukes of Tresmes and Gesvres. In modern fiction it features in the Dragonology books, the Digimon franchise, the 2021 film Godzilla vs. Kong and the board game Wyrmspan.

Trivia

Name. Its name is often glossed as meaning «two-winged», referring to its pair of wings.
Godzilla vs. Kong. In the 2021 film, Kong fights amphiptere-type creatures called Warbats in the Hollow Earth.
