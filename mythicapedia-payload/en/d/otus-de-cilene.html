Epeian hero of Greek mythology

Otus of Cyllene

Otus of Cyllene was a hero of Greek mythology from Cyllene, in the region of Elis. He took part in the Trojan War, fighting alongside Meges, commander of the Epeians.

The myth

According to tradition, Otus went to the Trojan War at the head of a contingent of Epeians, together with Meges. His name is tied to Cyllene and to the Epeians' part in the conflict against Troy.

His story is brief: tradition remembers him above all for his death at the hands of Polydamas, a Trojan warrior. The account fits other combat episodes of the war, in which heroes fall one after another to enemy weapons. The surviving sources give no further detail about his family or his deeds.
