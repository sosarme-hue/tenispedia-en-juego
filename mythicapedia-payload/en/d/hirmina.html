Elean princess in Greek mythology

Hyrmine

Hyrmine is an Elean princess of Greek mythology, best known as the mother of several heroes who took part in the voyage of the Argo. She gave her name to the town of Hyrmine, founded by her son Actor.

The myth

According to Pausanias, Hyrmine was the daughter of Epeius of Elis and Anaxiroe, though other authors name Neleus or Nycteus as her father, and some traditions make her the sister of Alector or an only child.

She married Phorbas, son of Lapithus, and had several children. Tiphys became the pilot of the Argo, while Actor founded the town that bears his mother's name. Augeas, another of her sons in some accounts, is given Helios or Poseidon as his father in other tellings, and the sources do not agree on whether Hyrmine was his mother.

Ancient sources: Pausanias mentions her in his Description of Greece, and later traditions record her genealogy with variations.
