Zulu goddess of rain, the rainbow and beer

Mbaba Mwana Waresa

Mbaba Mwana Waresa is a goddess of the Zulu people of southern Africa, linked to the rainbow, rain, farming and beer. She is credited with teaching her people to sow, harvest and brew beer, which made her one of the most revered deities among the Zulu.

The myth

According to the tale, she is the daughter of Umvelinqangi, the sky god, and lives in the clouds in a round hut built from rainbow arches. When her thunder drum rolls, people know she is about to pour rain from her heavenly home.

Finding no suitable husband among the gods of the sky, she came down to the lands of southern Africa in search of a mortal husband. To test whether he truly loved her, she sent a beautiful bride in her place while she disguised herself as an ugly old witch. The man was not fooled and recognised her at once. The two married, and they now live together in her rainbow-covered house in the sky.

Worship and religion

The Zulu revered her as a rain goddess, and the sound of her drum was a sign that water would fall on the fields.
