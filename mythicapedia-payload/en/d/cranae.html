Athenian princess in Greek mythology

Cranae

Cranae is an Athenian princess from Greek mythology, daughter of King Cranaus. Her name means ‘rocky’ in ancient Greek, and she matters mainly as a link in the royal house of Athens.

The myth

According to tradition, Cranae was the daughter of King Cranaus and Pedias, who was herself a daughter of Mynes of Sparta. This union tied the Athenian royal house to a Lacedaemonian family. Her name, based on a word for stones, is the most discussed aspect of her figure, though few stories illustrate it.

She had two sisters, Cranaechme and Atthis. Her profile also lists her as the partner of Amphictyon. The surviving accounts give her no deeds or episodes of her own, so her role remains limited to lineage and marriage, a sign of how briefly ancient writers treated her.
