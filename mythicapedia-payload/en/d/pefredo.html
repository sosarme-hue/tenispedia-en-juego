Pefredo

Pefredo is one of the three Graeae, pre-Olympian deities of Greek mythology and daughters of Phorcys and Ceto. Her role is limited to the story of Perseus, in which she helps the hero find the Gorgons.

The myth

Pefredo, whose name is interpreted as 'alarm', belongs to the trio of the Graeae alongside her sisters Enyo and Deino. She is one of the so-called Phorcides, that is, the female descendants of Phorcys.

Her only appearance in the tradition is in the myth of Perseus. When the hero must face Medusa, the mortal Gorgon, it is the Graeae who point him to the cave of their sisters, the Gorgons. Within the story she is a minor but essential character, because without that information Perseus could not reach his goal.

Trivia

A name with meaning. The name Pefredo is linked to the word 'alarm', a hint at the watchful or warning character attributed to her in the story.
Daughter of the Phorcides. Pefredo belongs to the Phorcides, the female descendants of Phorcys, a lineage that gathers several monstrous figures of Greek mythology.
