maiden turned bird in Chinese mythology

Jingwei

Jingwei is a young woman in Chinese mythology, daughter of Emperor Yandi, who was reborn as a bird to try to fill up the Eastern Sea. Her story gave rise to a famous expression of perseverance.

The myth

According to the Shanhaijing, Jingwei drowned in the Eastern Sea after setting out in a small boat to watch the sunrise. A storm wrecked her vessel and the sea swallowed her. Her father tried to recover her, but he found only the remains of the boat.

The young woman was reborn as a bird that carried twigs and pebbles in its beak to fill the ocean. The sea mocked her, claiming she would not manage it even in a million years. She replied that it would take ten million or a hundred million years, so that no one else would suffer her fate.

Legacy

The myth gave rise to the chengyu Jīngwèi tián hǎi, meaning dogged determination in the face of seemingly impossible odds. A mural of her legend was uncovered in 1988 at Tianjin railway station, she is a playable heroine in Smite, and geneticist Manyuan Long named a fruit fly gene after her.

Ancient sources: The Shanhaijing (Classic of Mountains and Seas) and the poems of Tao Qian, who mentions her in his Thirteen Poems.

Trivia

A gene with her name. Geneticist Manyuan Long named a fruit fly gene jgw after her, because like the princess it is 'reborn' with a new function and structure.
A mural in a railway station. In 1988 a dome mural depicting Jingwei's legend was revealed at Tianjin railway station.
