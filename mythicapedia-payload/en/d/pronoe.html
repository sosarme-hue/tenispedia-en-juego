Lycian naiad of Greek mythology

Pronoe

Pronoe is a naiad, a nymph of a river in Lycia, in Greek mythology. Her story is linked to Caunus and his sister Byblis and explains how the line that ruled the territory came about. She acts as an adviser whose counsel shapes the fate of a kingdom.

The myth

According to the tradition, Byblis, sister of Caunus, took her own life, and it was Pronoe who told Caunus what had happened. The naiad did not simply inform him; she persuaded him to stay by her side instead of mourning the sister he had lost.

Caunus agreed in exchange for rule over Lycia or Caria, two lands the story offers as possible rewards. Their son Aegialus was born from this union and inherited the kingdom after his father's death, continuing the family line.
