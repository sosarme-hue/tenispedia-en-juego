Turkic and Mongolic god of death and the underworld

Erlik

Erlik, also called Erlig or Erlik Khan, is the god of death and the underworld in Turkic and Mongolic mythology. Lord of Tamag, the hell beneath the earth, he brings forth death, plague and evil spirits to torment humans and claim their souls.

The myth

According to Siberian accounts, Erlik was the first creation of Ülgen, the creator god, but his pride led him to want equality with him. For this he was cast down to the underworld, to the ninth layer of the earth, where he rules as judge of the dead and opposes the realm of light.

The versions differ on his role in creation. In some he takes part in making humankind; in another he is the first man, whom Kayra casts out of heaven for hiding a handful of soil. In another tale, people were immortal until a crow suggested summoning Death, and they called Erlik. Among the Altai, his spirits were created while he was still in heaven. Erlik slew the messenger-god Maidere and is considered a teacher of sin, sometimes represented as a totemic bear. In shamanic prayers he appears as an old man with a sturdy body and the face and teeth of a pig.

His nine sons, the Karaoğlanlar, embody evils such as darkness, chaos and defeat, while his nine daughters, the Karakızlar, try to turn shamans away from their path.

Worship and religion

Erlik was worshipped in some traditional religions in Siberia and Central Asia, among them by the Buryats. Animal sacrifices were offered to him to rid people of illness or to secure a good place in the underworld. Shamans who invoked him, known as Black Shamans (kara kam), were regarded with suspicion for dealing with demonic spirits.

Legacy

The dinosaur Erlikosaurus is named after him. In Hungarian mythology, he is equated with Ördög.

Ancient sources: He is already mentioned in the Orkhon inscriptions of the early Turkic peoples.

Trivia

A dinosaur named for him. The dinosaur Erlikosaurus was named in honour of this god of death.
Mammoths in the underworld. According to the Dolgans, Erlik took mammoths down to the underworld, and those who try to return to the surface freeze to death as punishment.
