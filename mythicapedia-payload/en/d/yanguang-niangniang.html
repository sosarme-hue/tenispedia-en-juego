Taoist goddess of eyesight

Yanguang Niangniang

Yanguang Niangniang is a Taoist goddess of eyesight and vision, part of the Chinese religious tradition. She was one of the most popular Taoist goddesses in China during the Ming and Qing dynasties, and her figure is associated with protecting the eyes.

The myth

The reference texts do not record stories about her origin, her family or her deeds, so her figure is known mainly through the role attributed to her. She was believed able to cure blindness and other ailments related to sight.

In Taoist imagery she appears as an accompanying figure, alongside Bixia Yuanjun and other attendant goddesses. Her image in paintings and sculptures always places her within a wider group of deities, which shows she belonged to a network of Taoist divinities.

Worship and religion

She was believed to cure blindness and other eye ailments, so people prayed to her with that intention during the Ming and Qing dynasties.
