Greek nymph who was turned into islands

Perimela

Perimela is a young woman from Greek mythology, daughter of Hippodamas, whose love for the river Achelous led to her transformation into a group of islands. Her story, a forbidden love ending in metamorphosis, explains the origin of the Echinades, which lie at the mouth of that river.

The myth

According to the myth, Perimela was the daughter of Hippodamas and the lover of the river god Achelous. When her father discovered the affair, he threw her into the sea from the top of a cliff. Achelous caught her in midair before she could crash into the water, and then begged Poseidon not to let the young woman die and to allow him to hold her forever.

His plea was granted. Perimela was transformed into one of the Echinades, islands at the mouth of the Achelous, and she remained endlessly encircled by the river's arms.

Trivia

The Echinades. They are a group of islands at the mouth of the Achelous river, according to the story in which Perimela becomes one of them.
An eternal embrace. In the story, the river Achelous asks Poseidon to let him hold Perimela forever, and the transformation makes that wish come true.
