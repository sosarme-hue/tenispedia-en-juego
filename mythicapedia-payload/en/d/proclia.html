mythical mother of Tenes, daughter of Laomedon

Proclia

Proclia is a figure of Greek myth linked to the royal house of Troy and the mother of Tenes, the hero associated with the island of Tenedos. Her story matters mainly because it connects the Trojan line with the god Apollo through her son.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Proclia (or Proclea) was a daughter of Laomedon, king of Troy. Some sources instead make her a daughter of Clytius, son of Laomedon, and therefore a sister of Caletor.

The accounts marry her to Cycnus, king of Colonae, and she bore him two children, Tenes and Hemithea. The same tradition, however, says that Tenes was really the son of Apollo, so the god was his true father while Cycnus was his legal one.
