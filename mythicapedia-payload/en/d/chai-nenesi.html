Turkic water spirit

Chai nenesi

Chai nenesi is a water spirit from Turkic tradition, most often linked to creeks and still ponds. She is feared as a dangerous presence that lures those who come too close to the water's edge.

The myth

According to the stories, Chai nenesi pulls people down into swamps and lakes, and kills the animals that come near still waters. She is described as a white, naked woman with tousled hair who harasses travellers and brings misfortune to those who drink too much.

In most versions she is a restless being, associated with the "unclean force". She usually comes out of the water at night to climb a tree and sing songs, or to sit on a jetty combing her hair. Her figure gathers into a single being the dangers that tradition linked to motionless waters.

Trivia

Name in an ancient script. The name of this spirit is written in Old Turkic, using its own runic-type alphabet.
