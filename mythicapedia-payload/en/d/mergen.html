Turkic god of wisdom and abundance

Mergen

Mergen is a Turkic deity of abundance and wisdom, associated with the bow, the white horse and the colour white. He is considered the son of Kayra and the brother of Ulgan.

The myth

According to tradition, Mergen is the son of Kayra and the brother of Ulgan, and he lives on the seventh floor of the sky. He was portrayed as a young man wearing a helmet and carrying a bow, riding a white horse.

Rather than for deeds, his figure is linked to thought and intelligence. He is sometimes described as a strong and powerful bull, a symbol of profundity. His main attribute is the bow and arrow, which he holds in one hand.

Legacy

His name survives in language: mergen means archer or bowman in many Turkic languages, marksman or sniper in Bashkir, and wise or genius in Khalkha, the Mongolian language.

Trivia

A name that changes meaning. The word mergen means archer in Turkic languages, but in Mongolian Khalkha it came to mean wise or genius, a striking shift in meaning between neighbouring peoples.
White as a sign. The colour white and the white horse are constant symbols of Mergen, who is shown riding a steed of that colour.
