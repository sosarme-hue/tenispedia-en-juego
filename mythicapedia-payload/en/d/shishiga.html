female creature of Russian folklore

Shishiga

The shishiga is a female creature of Russian folklore, similar to the wood-goblin, that haunts swamps and forests. In tradition she brings misfortune to those who encounter her.

The myth

Russian lore describes her as white, naked and tousled. She is said to harass people and bring misfortune, especially to drunkards. She is also known as leshenka, a feminine form linked to the leshy.

Among the Komi people she has a place of her own. According to their tradition she lives in the Kama River and sometimes comes to the bank to comb her hair. Anyone who sees her will soon drown or die by some other cause.

Legacy

The name was also given to the GAZ-66, a two-tonne Soviet army truck informally nicknamed Shishiga.

Trivia

A truck with her name. The GAZ-66, a two-tonne Soviet army truck, is informally nicknamed Shishiga.
A presence among the Komi. The Komi people also know this creature, whom they place in the Kama River, and whose encounter is considered fatal.
