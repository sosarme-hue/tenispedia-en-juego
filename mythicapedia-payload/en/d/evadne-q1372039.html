heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Poseidon

Evadne

Evadne is a heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Poseidon and mother of the seer Yamo, born from her relationship with Apollo. Her story is tied to Arcadia and to the origins of a line of seers.

The myth

According to the tradition, Evadne was the daughter of Poseidon and Pitane, a daughter of the river god Eurotas. Soon after her birth, her father arranged for her to be raised by King Epitus, who ruled Fesana, a territory in Arcadia near the river Alpheus. There Apollo fell in love with her and got her pregnant. When Epitus learned of this, he consulted the oracle at Delphi, which told him that the child was Apollo's, that he would have the gift of prophecy and that he would found a famous line. Meanwhile, Evadne gave birth but, ashamed, abandoned the newborn. Yet the boy survived, as serpents fed him with honey. When Evadne discovered he was still alive, she took him back and named him Yamo, because she had found him lying among violets.

Trivia

Origin of the name. Evadne named her son Yamo because she found him lying among violets, according to the story.
Raised by serpents. According to the myth, the newborn survived thanks to serpents that gave him honey.
