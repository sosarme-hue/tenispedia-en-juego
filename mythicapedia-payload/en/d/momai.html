regional Hindu goddess of Gujarat and Kutch

Momai

Momai, also called Momai Mata and Dashama, is a regional Hindu goddess widely venerated in Gujarat, especially in the desert region of Kutch. She is the principal deity of the Rabari people and a protector of many clans.

The myth

Tradition places her first appearance in the village of Momai Mora, near Rapar in Kutch, which is now a place of pilgrimage.

She is shown riding a camel, with four arms: she holds a sword and a trident in her upper hands, and a lotus and armour in her lower hands.

Worship and religion

Her great festival is held at the Momai Mora temple on the eve of Navratri. She is a kuldevi, a protective family goddess, for clans such as the Jadeja, Parmar and Sodha, as well as for the Rabari, the Bardai Brahmins and several Mistri sub-clans. Each year the Dashama Vrata, a ten-day vow observed mainly by women for family prosperity and good fortune, is kept, usually in July or August.

Trivia

A ten-day vow. The Dashama Vrata is observed in Gujarat and Diu, and in some cases also in the month of Chaitra, in March.
A goddess of many clans. She is venerated as a kuldevi by warrior and ruling clans of the former state of Kutch, making her a deity shared by very different groups.
