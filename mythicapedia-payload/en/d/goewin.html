maiden of the Welsh Mabinogi

Goewin

Goewin is a figure of Welsh mythology who appears in Math fab Mathonwy, one of the Four Branches of the Mabinogi. Her role is brief, but it is decisive for the outcome of the story.

The myth

According to the tale, King Math fab Mathonwy can only stay alive if his feet rest always in the lap of a virgin, unless he is at war. Goewin is the young woman who fulfils this duty. His nephews Gwydion and Gilfaethwy take advantage of the king's absence, tricking him into going to war, and during that time they assault Goewin.

When Math returns, she reveals the crime. The king punishes the two young men by transforming them into a series of animals. To make amends, he marries Goewin and gives her power over his kingdom. The ending shows how the tale resolves the wrong through compensation as well as punishment.
