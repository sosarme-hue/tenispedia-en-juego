heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Autesion

Argia

Argia, also called Argea, is a heroine of Greek mythology and the daughter of the Theban king Autesion. She matters mainly as the mother of the twins Eurystheus and Procles, who are linked to the kingship of Sparta.

The myth

According to the tradition, Argia, daughter of Autesion, married Aristodemus, king of Sparta. From this union were born twin sons, Eurystheus and Procles, who became the center of the story of their lineage.

After Aristodemus died, the boys were placed under the guardianship of their uncle Teras, Argia's brother, who looked after them while they grew up.

Thus Argia appears above all as a mother and a link to a royal line, rather than as the protagonist of deeds of her own. The accounts place her at the origin of the twins' family, whose role in Spartan tradition unfolds later.
