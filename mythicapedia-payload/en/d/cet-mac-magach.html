Connacht warrior of the Ulster Cycle

Cet mac Mágach

Cet mac Mágach is a warrior of Connacht in the Ulster Cycle of Irish mythology. He matters mainly for his rivalry with Conall Cernach, a hero of Ulster.

The myth

According to the tales of the Ulster Cycle, Cet mac Mágach came from Connacht, the neighbouring kingdom of Ulster, and his story is defined above all by his clash with Conall Cernach, one of Ulster's most prominent warriors. The two appear as rivals within the same heroic world, and their confrontation adds tension to the story of the two kingdoms.

The versions do not agree on their kinship. In some, Cet is the brother of Conall's mother, which makes him Conall's uncle. His sister, Findchóem, is named as Conall's mother, so the rivalry is combined with a family bond, something the tales use to show the complicated loyalties between the two peoples.
