prince of Pylos in Greek mythology

Pylaon

Pylaon was a prince of Pylos, son of King Neleus and Chloris, and brother of Nestor. He is among the sons of Neleus whom the story counts as victims of Heracles' sack of Pylos.

The myth

According to the Greek tradition, Pylaon was the son of King Neleus and Chloris, daughter of King Amphion of Orchomenus. He belonged to a large royal household in Pylos, with brothers including Pero's siblings Taurus, Asterius, Evagoras, Deimachus, Eurybius, Epilaus, Phrasius, Eurymenes, Alastor, Nestor and Periclymenus.

The story tells how Heracles attacked Pylos and killed Neleus along with most of his sons, Pylaon among them. Nestor was the only brother to survive the slaughter, which leaves him as the last surviving member of Neleus' line.
