last king of Sicyon

Lacestades

Lacestades was the last king of Sicyon, son of Hippolytus and, in Greek tradition, a descendant of Heracles. His reign marked the end of Sicyon as a free city, when the Heraclids took control of it.

The myth

Like his father Hippolytus, Lacestades ruled Sicyon as a subject of Agamemnon. During his reign the Heraclids returned to the Peloponnese, attacked the city by night and seized control. Their leader, Falces, chose to share the throne with Lacestades, arguing that he too was a descendant of Heracles.

According to the tradition, this episode corresponds to the Dorian invasion, which turned Sicyon into one more territory of Argos, as had already happened in the time of Adrastus. With Lacestades, the era of Sicyon as an independent city came to an end.
