magician, hero and trickster of Welsh mythology

Gwydion fab Dôn

Gwydion fab Dôn is a magician, hero and trickster in Welsh mythology, a central figure in much of the Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi. His name is usually interpreted as "born of trees".

The myth

Gwydion is the son of Dôn and brother of Arianrhod and Gilfaethwy. In the Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi, Gilfaethwy becomes obsessed with Goewin, the maiden who holds the feet of his uncle, King Math of Gwynedd. Gwydion starts a war by stealing otherworldly pigs from Pryderi, king of Dyfed, and afterwards Gilfaethwy rapes Goewin. Gwydion defeats Pryderi in single combat and, as punishment, Math turns the brothers into animals for three years.

Later Gwydion proposes his sister Arianrhod as a new virgin. After she gives birth, she places three tynghedau on her son Lleu, but Gwydion tricks her twice. To counter the last curse, Math and Gwydion make Blodeuwedd from flowers. Blodeuwedd and Gronw Pebr plot Lleu's death, and he turns into an eagle before Gwydion sings an englyn that brings him back to human form. Gwydion then turns Blodeuwedd into an owl.

In the Battle of the Trees, Gwydion helps his brother Amaethon in a conflict with Arawn, king of Annwn, and guesses the name of an enemy warrior.

Legacy

His castle, Caer Wydion, was the traditional Welsh name for the Milky Way. Robert Graves identified him with the Germanic Wōden, a modern reading that does not appear in the ancient sources.

Ancient sources: The Mabinogi, the Welsh Triads, the Book of Taliesin and the Stanzas of the Graves.

Trivia

The Milky Way. Caer Wydion, the castle of Gwydion, was the traditional Welsh name for the Milky Way.
Golden shoemakers. The Welsh Triads name him one of the "Three Golden Shoemakers of the Island of Britain", alongside Manawydan and Caswallawn.
