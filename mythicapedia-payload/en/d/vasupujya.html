twelfth Jain tirthankara

Vasupujya

Vasupujya is the twelfth tirthankara of Jainism in the present age of the cosmic cycle (avasarpini). According to Jain belief, he attained liberation after destroying all his karma, and he remains one of the most revered figures in this Indian religion.

The myth

According to Jain tradition, Vasupujya was born to King Vasupujya and Queen Jaya Devi in Champapuri, within the Ikshvaku dynasty. He remained celibate and never married.

Texts give him a lifespan of 7,200,000 years and a height of 70 bows. Traditions stress a unique feature: his five auspicious moments (conception, birth, renunciation, omniscience and liberation) all took place in Champapuri, unlike most tirthankaras, who attained nirvana on Mount Shikharji.

Worship and religion

Champapuri, in today's Bhagalpur district of Bihar, is his main pilgrimage site. A colossal monolithic statue of him was inaugurated in 2014 at the temple complex in Nathnagar. His worship has spread with the Jain diaspora, with temples in Kerala, Ludhiana and Delhi.

Legacy

In Jain art he is shown in a meditative posture, with a red complexion and the water buffalo as his emblem.
