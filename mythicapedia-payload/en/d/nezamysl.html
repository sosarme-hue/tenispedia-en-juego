first mythical prince of Bohemia

Nezamysl

Nezamysl is, according to Czech tradition, the first of seven legendary princes of Bohemia who came before the first historical rulers of the Přemyslid dynasty.

The myth

According to Cosmas's chronicle, Nezamysl was the first of seven mythical princes who came between Přemysl the Ploughman and Bořivoj, the first historical ruler. He is the son of Přemysl and Libuše and the father of Mnata. His name is thought to mean "not thinking", the opposite of Přemysl, much like the Roman Simplicius.

These names appear in most historical works up to the 19th century. The frescoes in the Znojmo rotunda have been linked to this list, although Anežka Merhautová argues they show members of the dynasty of their own time. Záviš Kalandra believed the seven names were Slavic days of the week, and Vladimír Karbusický suggested that Cosmas may have built them from a misread ancient message.

Legacy

In the video game Crusader Kings II he can be played as king of Bohemia, despite the doubtful existence of the character.

Ancient sources: Cosmas's chronicle is the source that first records his name.

Trivia

Day of the week. Záviš Kalandra proposed that Nezamysl was Sunday, the day when one does not think about working.
In a video game. He can be chosen as ruler of Bohemia in Crusader Kings II, even though his existence is doubtful.
