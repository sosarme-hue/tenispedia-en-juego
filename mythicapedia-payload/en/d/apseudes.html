Greek Nereid, sea nymph

Apseudes

Apseudes is one of the fifty Nereids, sea nymphs and daughters of the sea old man Nereus and the Oceanid Doris. Her name means «she who never lies», and with it she inherits the truthfulness attributed to her father.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Apseudes was a daughter of Nereus, the Old Man of the Sea, and of Doris. Like her father, a sea divinity who always told the truth, she bore a name meaning «she who never lies», which links her to her sister Nemertes, who shares that quality inherited from their father.

Apseudes appears in the episode of Achilles' grief over the death of his friend Patroclus. When Thetis cries out in sympathy for her son's suffering, Apseudes and her sisters come to the goddess to share her sorrow. The scene shows the sea world joining Thetis in mourning, since she herself was one of the Nereids.
