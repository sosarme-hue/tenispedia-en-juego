Argeus

Argeus is a legendary king of Argos from the Abantiad dynasty, best known as the father of Anaxagoras and, according to one ancient scholium, an ancestor of Capaneus. He is a shadowy figure, and some traditions leave him out altogether.

The myth

According to the tradition, Argeus was the son of Megapenthes and ruled Argos in the Abantiad line. An ancient scholium presents him as an ancestor of Capaneus, one of the Seven against Thebes.

The best-known episode involving his family comes during the reign of his son Anaxagoras: the women of Argos were struck by a madness that drove them out of their homes to wander the countryside. The affliction ended when Melampus, son of Amythaon, cured them.

Argeus barely appears in the stories, and some versions skip him, making Anaxagoras a direct son of Megapenthes.

Ancient sources: An ancient scholium names him as an ancestor of Capaneus.
