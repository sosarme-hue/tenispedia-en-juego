creator god in African mythology

Akongo

Akongo is a creator god in African mythology, worshipped by the Ngombe people of the Congo. His story explains the origin of humankind through his daughter Mbokomu.

The myth

According to tradition, Akongo originally lived among people, but left them after their constant fighting and withdrew to heaven. There his daughter Mbokomu caused trouble, so Akongo lowered her to earth in a basket together with her two children. She became the ancestor of all people.

The accounts describe him as a personal being, distinct from the impersonal force known as mana. He is not benevolent in every case, which sets him apart from the image of an always protective father.

Worship and religion

The Ngombe worship him as creator and clearly distinguish him from mana. The sources consulted do not detail temples, rites or festivals.
