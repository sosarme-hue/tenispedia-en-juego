Naglfari

Naglfari is a figure in Norse mythology who appears as one of the husbands of Nótt, the personification of night, and as the father of their son Auðr. He is known from the Prose Edda compiled by Snorri Sturluson.

The myth

Naglfari is attested in a single passage, the Gylfaginning section of Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda, written in the thirteenth century. There he is named as one of three successive husbands of Nótt, the goddess of night.

Together they had a son, Auðr. The text offers no further episodes, deeds, appearance, origin or fate for Naglfari.

Ancient sources: He appears only in the Gylfaginning of Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda, a medieval work that preserves earlier Norse traditions.
