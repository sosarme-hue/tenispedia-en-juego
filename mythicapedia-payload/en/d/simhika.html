rakshasi of the Ramayana, foe of Hanuman

Simhika

Simhika is a rakshasi, a demonic being in Hindu tradition, daughter of Hiranyakashipu and sister of Prahlada. Her best-known story appears in the Ramayana, where she confronts the vanara Hanuman.

The myth

In the Ramayana, Hanuman was crossing the ocean towards Lanka, the kingdom of the rakshasa king Ravana, when Simhika, hiding beneath the waves, seized his shadow as he flew overhead. Forewarned of this danger by Sugriva, Hanuman grew in size; she did the same, but he shrank, let himself be swallowed, tore her apart from within, and then resumed his journey.

Other versions say a single kick was enough to kill her.

Legacy

The actress Sudha Chandran played her in the Indian epic drama series Karmaphal Daata Shani (2016-2018).

Ancient sources: The Ramayana, where she is set against Hanuman.
