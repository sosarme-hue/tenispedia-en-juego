demon king of the Ars Goetia

Paimon

Paimon is a demon from early modern grimoires, chiefly the Ars Goetia included in The Lesser Key of Solomon, where he appears as an infernal king and ruler of the west. Rooted in esoteric tradition, he is now a familiar figure in fictional demonology.

The myth

According to the Ars Goetia, Paimon is one of the kings of hell and the most obedient of them to Lucifer. Accounts differ on the size of his forces: two hundred legions in the Ars Goetia and in Weyer, but only twenty-five in the Livre des Esperitz. When he appears he roars with a loud voice and must be asked to moderate his tone before he answers clearly. The summoner faces west or northwest, depending on the version, and if Paimon is called alone, an offering must be made, after which he is usually joined by the kings Beball and Abalam.

He is described as a man with a beautiful face, though some authors speak of an effeminate one, who rides a dromedary and wears a crown. Before him come demons playing trumpets, cymbals and other instruments. In exchange for the offering he promises to teach arts, philosophy, sciences and the secrets of earth, waters and winds, to grant titles and to help defeat enemies. In the book of Abramelin he is also credited with visions, raising the dead and summoning spirits.

Legacy

Paimon is the main antagonist of Ari Aster's film Hereditary (2018), where his sigil appears several times alongside other signs of his presence. In the game Genshin Impact, a companion named Paimon accompanies the protagonist throughout. In the web series Helluva Boss he is the father of Stolas, and he appears as a boss in the game Infernax.

Ancient sources: His name appears in the Ars Goetia of The Lesser Key of Solomon, in Johann Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, in the Livre des Esperitz and in the book of Abramelin, all early modern grimoires.

Trivia

Riding a dromedary. According to these sources, he is depicted as a crowned man riding a dromedary, preceded by musicians playing trumpets and cymbals.
