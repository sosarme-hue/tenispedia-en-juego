Yellow Wind Demon

The Yellow Wind Demon is a rat spirit from the Chinese novel Journey to the West who rules Yellow Wind Ridge and challenges Sun Wukong with his powerful sandstorm wind.

The myth

According to the novel, the Yellow Wind Demon was originally a rat living at the foot of Lingshan. After stealing oil from a lamp before the Buddha, he fled to Yellow Wind Ridge and became a demon. The bodhisattva Lingji captured him, spared his life and ordered him not to harm living beings.

His follower, the Tiger Vanguard, captures Tang Sanzang, and the demon ties him to a post while he waits for his pursuers to give up. After a fight in which his Samadhi Divine Wind fills the sky with yellow sand and blinds Wukong, Lingji throws his Flying Dragon Staff, which traps him. The demon returns to his rat form and is taken to Lingshan to answer for his crimes.

Legacy

In the 2024 video game Black Myth: Wukong he appears as the Yellow Wind Sage, a character who alternates good and evil deeds. Some players linked his theft of Lingji's head to the looting of Chinese cultural objects by foreign powers in the late Qing dynasty, and others to the idiom shǔqiègǒudào ("petty theft like rat and dog").

Ancient sources: He appears in the 16th-century Chinese novel Journey to the West, in chapters 20 and 21.

Trivia

A wind that darkens the sky. In chapter 21 his Samadhi Divine Wind is described as strong enough to shake mountains, darken the sky and disturb the gods.
False earth. In the novel's Five Elements symbolism he represents "false earth", while Sha Seng represents "true earth".
