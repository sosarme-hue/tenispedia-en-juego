mythological king of Argos

Cylarabes

Cylarabes is a mythological king of Argos, son of Sthenelus, who reunited a kingdom that had been divided since the time of Anaxagoras. After his death without an heir, the throne passed to Orestes, king of Mycenae.

The myth

According to myth, Cylarabes inherited the throne from his father Sthenelus upon the latter's death. During his reign Argos was once again united into a single kingdom, since the territory had been split into three parts since Anaxagoras: Anaxagoras kept the central region and gave one third to Melampus and another to Bias.

Cylarabes regained the portion held by the descendants of Bias after the death of Cyanippus; the share of Melampus had already returned to Sthenelus after the death of Amphilocus. When Cylarabes died without an heir, the throne was left vacant and was seized by Orestes, king of Mycenae.
