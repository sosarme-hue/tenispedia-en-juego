Egyptian warrior goddess of the underworld gates

Qed-her

Qed-her is a minor goddess of ancient Egypt, a warrior and guardian of the gates of the underworld. She is recognized by her fierce iconography, which links her to protection in the afterlife.

The myth

Qed-her appears in Egyptian tradition as a minor deity tied to the world of the dead. Her main role was that of guardian of the gates of the underworld, through which souls had to pass on their journey after death.

The sources describe her as a warrior. She is depicted as a woman with the head of a lioness, crowned with two snakes and holding a knife in each hand, features that emphasize her defensive and fearsome character.
