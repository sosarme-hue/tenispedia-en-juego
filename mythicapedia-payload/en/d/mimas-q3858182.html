Trojan hero of Greek mythology

Mimas

Mimas is a Trojan hero who appears in Virgil's Aeneid. The son of Amycus and Theano, he was a companion of Paris and travelled with Aeneas to Italy.

The myth

According to Virgil's Aeneid, Mimas was the son of Amycus and Theano, and he had been born on the same day as Paris, with whom he was regarded as a companion. He was a noble Trojan, and the tradition places him among those who followed Aeneas on his journey to Italy, one of the great undertakings of the poem.

On Italian soil, Mimas took part in the fighting against the local peoples and was killed by Mezentius, one of the Trojans' opponents. His story is brief within the poem, but it shows how the companions of the heroes shared the expedition's fate to the end.

Ancient sources: Virgil's Aeneid, which presents him as a companion of Paris and Aeneas.
