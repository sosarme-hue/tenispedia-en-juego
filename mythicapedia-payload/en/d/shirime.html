Japanese yōkai with an eye on its bottom

Shirime

Shirime is a yōkai in Japanese folklore that looks like a human with no facial features, somewhat similar to the noppera-bō. Its trademark is an eye where its anus should be. It is considered harmless, since its only pleasure is scaring travellers.

The myth

The legend tells of a samurai walking at night down the road to Kyoto when he heard someone calling for him to wait. Turning around, he found a man stripping off his clothes and pointing his bare buttocks at him. Then a huge, glittering eye opened where the man's anus should have been.

Few sightings have ever been recorded, so some believe Shirime may simply be a variant of the noppera-bō, or a tanuki or kitsune that changed shape to play a prank on people.

Legacy

The haiku poet and painter Buson included it in many of his yōkai paintings. In 2023 the PC game Shirime: The Curse of Butt-Eye was released, based on the legend and starring the creature.

Trivia

A favourite of Buson. The haiku poet and painter Buson liked Shirime so much that he included it in many of his yōkai paintings.
A game with its name. In April 2023 the PC game Shirime: The Curse of Butt-Eye was released, based on the legend.
