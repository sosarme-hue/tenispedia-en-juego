Trojan warrior in Homer's Iliad

Menon

Menon is a figure of Greek mythology who, according to Homer's Iliad, fought on the Trojan side during the Trojan War. His death at the hands of Leonteus is one of the minor episodes of the battle the poem narrates.

The myth

Menon is known mainly from Book XII of the Iliad, where he appears among the fighters who assault the Greek wall. Homer presents him as one warrior in a large army, without a lengthy personal story: his role in the narrative is limited to falling in combat.

Leonteus's name appears here as the victor, a warrior on the opposing side, and the episode illustrates the choral character of the poem's battles, in which many minor heroes see their deaths told in just a few lines.

Ancient sources: Homer's Iliad, Book XII.
