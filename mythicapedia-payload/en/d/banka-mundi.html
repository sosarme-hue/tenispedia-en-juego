Khond goddess of the hunt and fertility

Banka-Mundi

Banka-Mundi is the goddess of the hunt and fertility of the Khond people of India. She is regarded as a protector against the wild animals of the forests and as one who removes fear. She matters as an example of faith tied to hunting and forest life in India.

The myth

According to Khond tradition, Banka-Mundi watches over those who venture into the forest and whose paths are haunted by wild beasts.

She is also credited with driving fear from the hunter and granting fertility. No details survive of her genealogy or specific episodes, so her image centres on protection.

Worship and religion

The Khonds worship her seeking protection from the wild animals of the forests. No specific temples, festivals or rites are described. Her worship is closely tied to the forest environment in which hunting took place.
