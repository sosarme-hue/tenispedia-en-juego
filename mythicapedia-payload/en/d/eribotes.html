Locrian hero of the Argonauts

Eribotes

Eribotes was a Locrian hero, son of Teleon, counted among the Argonauts. He is remembered mainly as the expedition's physician, and his death in Libya is one of the episodes of the return journey.

The myth

According to the tradition, Eribotes was the son of Teleon and Zeuxippe, and he sailed among the Argonauts who accompanied Jason in quest of the Golden Fleece. The sources credit him with the skills of a physician: in the Argonautica he tends to Oileus when the latter is wounded by a feather from a Stymphalian bird.

On the return voyage, Eribotes and Canthus died in Libya. According to the story, they were slain by the shepherd Cephalion, son of the nymph Tritonis and Amphithemis, while they were plundering his flocks.

Ancient sources: He appears in the Argonautica of Apollonius of Rhodes, the Hellenistic poem about Jason's expedition.

Trivia

A physician among the Argonauts. Among the heroes who sailed with Jason, Eribotes stands out for his healing skills, since he tends to Oileus after a wound caused by a feather of the Stymphalian birds.
A death in Libya. Eribotes and Canthus lost their lives in Libya at the hands of the shepherd Cephalion, son of a nymph, while trying to steal his flocks.
