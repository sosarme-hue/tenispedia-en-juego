Turkic and Mongolian creator deity

Ülgen (Bai-Ülgen)

Bai-Ülgen or Ülgen is a creator deity of Turkic and Mongolian tradition, usually distinct from Tengri though sometimes identified with him. He protects humankind from Erlik and is the patron of shamans.

The myth

According to tradition, Ülgen created the earth, the sky and all living beings, as well as the rainbow. He lives on the sixteenth floor of the sky, in a golden house above the stars, the sun and the moon, a place no mortal can reach except shamans.

He is the enemy of Erlik, god of evil and darkness, and is credited with goodness and abundance. He had seven sons and nine daughters, whose names are unknown; the daughters inspire shamans.

Worship and religion

Animals, especially horses, were offered to him, and the birch was regarded as sacred to him. Once every third, sixth, ninth or twelfth year, a shaman could sacrifice a white horse and, riding its soul, climb through the layers of heaven, guided by Yayık, his servant, who reported whether the offering had been accepted.

Trivia

Origin of the name. His name is linked to Old Turkic bay, "rich", and ülgen, "magnificent".
A cosmic tree. The birch was seen as a cosmic axis linking earth and sky.
