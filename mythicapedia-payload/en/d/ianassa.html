Nereid of Greek mythology

Ianassa

Ianassa, also spelled Janassa, is one of the fifty Nereids of Greek mythology, sea nymphs born to Nereus and the Oceanid Doris. She is best remembered for the sympathy the Nereids show for Achilles' grief.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Ianassa was one of the fifty Nereids, sea nymphs daughters of Nereus, the so-called Old Man of the Sea, and of the Oceanid Doris. The sources that mention her give her no deeds of her own or lengthy episodes; her role is simply to belong to the chorus of marine sisters.

Her best-known moment comes when Thetis, mother of Achilles, mourns the suffering of her son after the death of his friend Patroclus. Ianassa and her sisters appear to her to share her weeping, showing the solidarity of the Nereids both with the goddess and with the hero's sorrow.
