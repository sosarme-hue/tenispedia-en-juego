Shinto term for female deities or kami

Himegami

Himegami is the general Shinto term for a female deity, or female kami. Sometimes her individual identity is known; in other cases she is named only by her role in relation to a male kami enshrined at the same shrine, such as wife, daughter or another important figure.

The myth

A himegami is usually not a character with a story of her own, but a category covering goddesses of the Japanese tradition. When her name is known, she appears paired with a male kami, such as Amenokoyane-no-Mikoto, and with offspring, such as Ame no Oshikumone. Her figure is understood mainly through these family relationships.

Worship and religion

In Shinto, these deities are worshipped at shrines (jinja), and they often share the precinct with a male kami to whom they are linked. Their presence at the shrine is part of religious practice.
