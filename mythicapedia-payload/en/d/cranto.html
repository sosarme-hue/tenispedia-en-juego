Nereid, sea nymph of Greek mythology

Cranto

Cranto is one of the fifty Nereids of Greek mythology, sea nymphs and daughters of Nereus, the Old Man of the Sea, and the Oceanid Doris. Her name is one of many that make up the large chorus of sea-born daughters said to fill the waters of Greece, which makes her a good example of that long tradition.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Cranto belonged to the large group of the Nereids, the daughters Nereus had with Doris, one of the Oceanids. Nereus was known as the Old Man of the Sea, a figure of calm and wise waters, and his daughters made up his court across the seas.

The ancient sources preserve no deed or story of her own. Her presence is limited to the genealogy: she appears as one more among the fifty Nereids, with no individual traits to set her apart from her sisters.

Ancient sources: Hesiod, in his Theogony, lists the Nereids as daughters of Nereus and Doris, Cranto among them.
