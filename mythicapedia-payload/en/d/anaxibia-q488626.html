daughter of Cratieus in Greek mythology

Anaxibia

Anaxibia is, in Greek mythology, a daughter of Cratieus about whom very little is known. Some traditions present her as the wife of Nestor and the mother of nine children, although the more widespread version gives that role to Eurydice.

The myth

According to the tradition, Anaxibia was a daughter of Cratieus, a figure about whom nothing further is told. Some ancient sources place her as the wife of Nestor and credit her with a large family: Pisidice, Polycaste, Perseus, Stratius, Aretus, Echephron, Pisistratus, Antilochus and Thrasymedes.

However, it is more common to regard Eurydice as Nestor's wife and the mother of these same children, so the name Anaxibia remains a minority variant within the story.

Trivia

One wife, two versions. The sources that name Anaxibia as Nestor's wife are a minority; most give that place to Eurydice.
A large family. The children credited to her include Antilochus, Thrasymedes and Pisistratus, as well as Perseus and Polycaste.
