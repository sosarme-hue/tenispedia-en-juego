modern folklore creature of South Carolina

Lizard Man of Scape Ore Swamp

The Lizard Man is a creature said to inhabit the swamplands of Lee County, South Carolina, according to local folklore. First reported in the late 1980s, it is a modern legend rather than an ancient tradition, but it shows how a rumor can grow into a story with wide media reach.

The myth

The story begins in July 1988, when the sheriff's office investigated a car damaged overnight outside a home near Bishopville, on the edge of the swamp. Soon after, teenager Christopher Davis claimed that, after a flat tire on his way home from a night shift, a green, scaly creature with three fingers and red eyes chased his car until he braked and the creature rolled off.

The account drew reporters, T-shirts, tourists and monster hunters. In August, an airman from a nearby air force base claimed he had shot the creature, but two days later he recanted, admitting he had made it up to keep the legend alive. Sightings gradually faded, and local officials suggested a bear was the likely cause.

Legacy

The legend appears in the children's book Maniacal Monsters and Bizarre Beasts, in Linda Godfrey's American Monsters and in Lyle Blackburn's book on the creature. It was also featured on Destination Truth and in an episode of Fact or Faked, and in 2017 a state agency joked about it during a solar eclipse.

Trivia

A confessed hoax. A man who claimed to have shot the creature later admitted the whole episode was invented.
Million-dollar reward. A local radio station offered one million dollars to anyone who could capture the creature alive.
