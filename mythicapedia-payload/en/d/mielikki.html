Finnish goddess of forests and the hunt

Mielikki

Mielikki is the goddess of forests and the hunt in Finnish mythology. Usually seen as the wife of Tapio, lord of the forest, or sometimes as his daughter-in-law, she was invoked by hunters and by those who gathered mushrooms and berries.

The myth

According to Karelian runic songs and the Kalevala, Mielikki is the partner of Tapio and the mother of Tuulikki, Tellervo and Nyyrikki. The sources do not fully agree: in some songs Nyyrikki is Tapio's son, despite the feminine form of his name.

Tradition gives her a role in the creation of the bear, which she raised under a spruce. She is also a skilled healer. She tends the paws of animals that escaped traps, helps chicks that have fallen from their nests, and treats wood grouse after their mating displays. She knows the healing herbs and will help anyone who knows how to ask. Her appearance foretells the hunter's luck: if she appears beautiful and richly adorned, game will be plentiful; if she is ragged and wears twig ornaments, the prey will be scarce. In the Kalevala, the hero Lemminkäinen offers her and Tapio prayers, gold and silver so he can catch the elk of Hiisi.

Worship and religion

She was invoked more often than her husband Tapio. People prayed to her especially when hunting hares and birds, and she was honoured both by small-game hunters and by those gathering mushrooms and berries. Offerings were made at a spruce whose top had been cut off. The sources do not say whether her worship survives today.

Legacy

A mountain on Venus, Mielikki Mons, is named after her. The Finnish band Nightwish mentions her in the song Elvenpath, and a deity with her name appears in the Forgotten Realms, a campaign setting for Dungeons & Dragons.

Ancient sources: Karelian runic songs and the Kalevala, compiled in the nineteenth century from Finnish and Karelian folklore.

Trivia

Healer and huntress. In the songs she heals animals hurt by traps, so her role is not limited to hunting.
Metal song. The Finnish band Nightwish names her in Elvenpath, where Tapio appears as Bearking, ruler of the forest.
