Golgos

Golgos is a founding hero of Greek mythology linked to Cyprus. Son of Aphrodite and Adonis, he gave his name to the city of Golgi and is remembered as the leader of the colonists who settled it.

The myth

According to tradition, Golgos was the only son of Aphrodite and Adonis, the youth of eternal beauty. He had a sister, Beroe, and both share the lineage of the goddess of love. Accounts present him as the leader of a group of colonists who came from Sicyon. On reaching Cyprus he founded a city that took his name, Golgi, and dedicated it to his mother. No extended narrative of his deeds survives, and his figure is mainly tied to the founding of that city.

Worship and religion

It was said that Aphrodite was worshipped at Golgi before she was in the city of Paphos, another of her cult centres on Cyprus.
