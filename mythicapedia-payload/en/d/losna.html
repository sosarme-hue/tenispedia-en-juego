Etruscan goddess of the Moon

Losna

Losna is the Etruscan goddess of the Moon, an ancient deity also associated with the oceans and the tides. She is often compared with the Greek goddess Leucothea.

The myth

The surviving sources say very little about Losna and do not describe specific episodes from her story. What is known comes from her domains: she is linked to the Moon, the sea and the rhythm of the tides, which places her in a sphere similar to that of other lunar and marine deities.

The most common comparison is with Leucothea, the Greek goddess associated with the sea. Both share a marine character, although there is no evidence that their stories coincide. Her Roman equivalent would be Luna, goddess of the Moon, which shows how Mediterranean cultures recognised similar celestial figures.
