realm of fire in Norse mythology

Muspelheim

Muspelheim is the realm of fire in Norse mythology, home to the fire giants and to Surtr, its guardian, who wields a flaming sword. It plays a central role both in the creation of the world and in its final destruction at Ragnarök.

The myth

According to the myth, Muspelheim was a hot, glowing land of fire, home to the fire giants and guarded by Surtr. Sources place it above Asgard, the home of the Æsir, and further south lies Jötunheim, the realm of ice and the jötnar, although the descriptions do not fully agree on its exact position. Its name means world or home of fire, and its etymology remains uncertain; one suggestion links it to a phrase meaning world-destroyers.

In the Prose Edda, long before the Earth existed, the realm of Niflheim already stood, with a well called Hvergelmir from which rivers known as the Élivágar flowed. Their venom hardened into ice, which spread over the primordial void of Ginnungagap. When the heat and sparks from Muspelheim met that ice, it began to melt; from its drops arose Ymir, the first being, while the sparks became the Sun, the Moon and the stars.

At Ragnarök, according to the Prose Edda, the sons of Muspell will break the Bifröst bridge. The sky will split open, and Surtr will march out of Muspelheim with all his giants toward Asgard, destroying everything in his path.

Legacy

The realm appears in Hans Christian Andersen's fairy tale "The Marsh King's Daughter", where it is called "Surt's sea of fire". In the Marvel universe it is a realm shown in Thor: The Dark World (2013) and Thor: Ragnarok (2017), depicted as a Dyson sphere with rocky terrain. It also features in God of War, where players complete its six Trials, and in God of War: Ragnarök, where Kratos confronts Surtr himself. Other appearances include the Dawn of Ragnarök expansion for Assassin's Creed Valhalla, Fire Emblem Heroes, the MMORPG Runescape, which has a planet called Muspell, and the game Puzzle & Dragons, which features monsters bearing its name.

Ancient sources: The Prose Edda, which describes its role in the creation of the world and in Ragnarök.

Trivia

A sea of fire. Hans Christian Andersen mentions "Surt's sea of fire" in his fairy tale "The Marsh King's Daughter".
A realm to explore. In God of War players can visit Muspelheim and complete its six Trials, which bring Kratos and Atreus closer to the top of a large volcano.
