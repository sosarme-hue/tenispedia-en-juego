legendary minister who founded music in ancient China

Ling Lun

Ling Lun is the legendary founder of music in ancient China and a minister of the Yellow Emperor. He matters because, according to tradition, he set the notes of the ancient Chinese five-tone scale.

The myth

According to the myth, Ling Lun made bamboo flutes whose sounds imitated the calls of many birds, including the mythical fenghuang. With them he established the five notes of the ancient Chinese scale: gong, shang, jiao, zhi and yu, equivalent to do, re, mi, sol and la.

Tradition adds that the sounds of eight musical instruments were fixed through these flutes, and that the Yellow Emperor ordered bells cast in tune with them. Other accounts credit the invention of music to Kui, a culture hero often confused with a one-legged monster of the same name.

Legacy

In the video games Civilization IV and Civilization V he appears as a great artist. In the 2020 film Bill & Ted Face the Music, the character Ling Lun, played by Sharon Gee, is one of the musicians in the heroes' band.

Ancient sources: The third-century BCE Lushi Chunqiu credits the invention of music to Kui, not to Ling Lun.
