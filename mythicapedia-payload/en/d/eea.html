mythical island of the sorceress Circe

Aeaea

Aeaea is a mythical island in the Mediterranean that Greek tradition places as the home of the sorceress Circe. It matters because in Homer's Odyssey it is where Odysseus spends a year before continuing his journey home to Ithaca.

The myth

In the Odyssey, a storm drives Odysseus and his companions to Aeaea, where he lives for a year with Circe and has children with her. Speaking to Alcinous, king of the Phaeacians, Odysseus recounts his stay and that of his crew while trying to return home. Homer describes the island as covered with pasture and dense woodland of oak and beech, with high bluffs from which the sea could be seen in every direction, and Circe's stone house set in a forest that could be seen from afar.

In Apollonius of Rhodes's Argonautica, the Argonauts reach Aeaea on their return voyage, and Circe purifies them of the murder of Apsyrtus, brother of Medea, who was the sorceress's niece. Apollonius places the island more precisely, south of Aethalia (Elba) and within sight of the Tyrrhenian coast of Italy. Homer's geography is considered more mythic than literal, so the island's location has been proposed in many different places.

Legacy

The island appears in Richard Aldington's novel All Men Are Enemies (1933), where it is the setting for his heroes' meeting. In John Banville's Ghosts (1993), a character suggests that the island where a boating party is shipwrecked is Aeaea. Madeline Miller's novel Circe (2018) portrays it as a place of woods and flowers where the heroine lives in exile. The video game Assassin's Creed Odyssey places a fictional atoll off the coast of Paxos as its location.

Ancient sources: Homer's Odyssey and Apollonius of Rhodes's Argonautica.

Trivia

Another island with the same name. Calypso was also said to have lived on an island called Aeaea, located in the straits between Italy and Sicily.
An island that stopped being one. Cape Circeo, identified with Aeaea by the Romans, looked like an island because of the marshes around it. Dionysius of Halicarnassus wrote that it was already a peninsula in his time.
A modern proposal. Robert Graves proposed identifying Aeaea with the island of Lošinj, near the Istrian Peninsula in the northern Adriatic.
