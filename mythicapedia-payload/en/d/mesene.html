heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Triopas

Messene

Messene is a heroine of Greek mythology, daughter of Triopas, king of Argos, to whom tradition credits the name of the region of Messenia. Her story, passed down mainly by Pausanias, portrays her as an ambitious woman who drove the founding of a new kingdom.

The myth

According to the story, Messene was proud of her father, who led the Greeks of his day in rank and power, and she felt it unworthy to marry a man without a title. She wed Polycaon, son of King Lelex of Laconia. His brother Myles, the eldest, had inherited the throne, so the couple decided to carve out a kingdom of their own.

They gathered troops from Argos and Lacedaemon and invaded a neighbouring territory. From then on the land was called Messenia, in her honour. They founded several cities, including Andania, where they built their palace.

Pausanias notes that, despite searching several sources, nobody recorded any descendants of the couple. In his estimate, Messene's line ruled Messenia for no more than five generations, after which the Messenians summoned Perieres, son of Aeolus, to take the throne. Another version makes her the daughter of Phorbas and sister of Triopas instead.

Worship and religion

Glaucus, son of Aepytus and grandson of Cresphontes, established a hero cult in her honour. In Messenia stood a heroon with a statue of gold and Parian marble. The story is placed in the 10th century BC by tradition, but the sources do not say whether her cult survived to the present day.

Legacy

Her name is bound to the region of Messenia, in the southwest of the Peloponnese.

Ancient sources: Pausanias, in his book on Messenia, records her story and names as his sources the Great Ehoiai and the Naupactica, and the works of Cinaethon and Asius of Samos.

Trivia

A region named after a woman. According to the myth, the region of Messenia was named after Messene following the conquest she carried out with her husband.
No recorded descendants. Pausanias searched several ancient works for the couple's descendants but found no information about them.
