Buddhist heaven of the Kāmadhātu, abode of Maitreya

Tushita

Tushita is one of the six deva-worlds of the Buddhist desire realm, a heaven described as a place where meditation comes more easily and where future buddhas dwell. It matters mainly because bodhisattvas destined for enlightenment reside there, among them Maitreya, the future Buddha.

The myth

According to Buddhist tradition, Tushita lies between the Yāma heaven and the Nirmāṇarati heaven. It is a pure land where meditation is easier and where one can reach buddhahood. Before being reborn as Siddhartha Gautama, the bodhisattva Śvetaketu ("White Banner") dwelt there; now the bodhisattva Nātha ("Protector") lives there, and he will be reborn as the Buddha Maitreya. The Buddha Manjushri, the buddha of wisdom, also dwells there and teaches emptiness.

Pali texts say time runs differently in this heaven than on Earth. In Mahayana thought, all bodhisattvas destined to become buddhas in their next life stay there for a time. Tushita belongs to the same world-system as Earth and is therefore considered relatively close, unlike Amitabha's Pure Land (Sukhavati), which is a separate world-system.

Worship and religion

Tushita is a focus of devotion mainly in Mahayana Buddhism, where many believers vow to be reborn there to hear Maitreya's teachings and, with him, attain buddhahood. This aspiration remains alive in contemporary Buddhist practice.
