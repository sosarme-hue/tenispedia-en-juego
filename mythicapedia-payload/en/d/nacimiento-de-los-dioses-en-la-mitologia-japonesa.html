episode of the Japanese myth of the creation of the gods

Birth of the Gods in Japanese Mythology

An episode of Japanese mythology that follows the creation of the islands of Japan and tells of the birth of the kami, the descendants of Izanagi and Izanami. It is the story that gives rise to the three most important kami of Shinto: Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo.

The myth

According to the Kojiki, many kami were born from the union of Izanagi and Izanami until Kagutsuchi, the fire kami, burned his mother Izanami's genitals at birth and fatally wounded her. Izanagi, consumed by rage, killed Kagutsuchi with his sword, and from the blood and body of the slain god further kami emerged.

Izanagi travelled to Yomi, the land of the dead, to bring back Izanami, but found her a rotting corpse. He fled from her pursuers, the Yomotsushikome and the eight thunder gods, and escaped thanks to peaches and a boulder that blocked the path. The tradition holds that this separation explains the cycle of life and death.

Back in the world of the living, Izanagi purified himself through misogi in a stream, and new kami were born from his discarded garments. From the washing of his face came Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo, to whom he entrusted the rule of the world.

Ancient sources: The Kojiki, an 8th-century text, is the main source recounting this episode.
