one of the Nine Provinces of ancient Chinese legend

Bingzhou

Bingzhou, or Bing Province, is one of the Nine Provinces that, according to legend, Yu the Great set up after taming the flood. Its name was later given to many real regions in China, linking the legendary geography with the historical one.

The myth

According to tradition, when Yu the Great (c. 2100 BC) tamed the flood, he divided China into nine provinces. Bingzhou is counted among them, as one of the ideal divisions of the ordered land.

Historical texts such as the Rites of Zhou and the Treatise on Geography in the Book of Han also record this division and place Bingzhou around present-day Baoding in Hebei, and Taiyuan and Datong in Shanxi.

Legacy

The name Bingzhou was reused for centuries for different real regions, so the legendary province left a lasting mark on Chinese place names.

Ancient sources: The Rites of Zhou and the Book of Han record the division into Nine Provinces that includes Bingzhou.
