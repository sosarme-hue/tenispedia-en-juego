Oceanid of Greek mythology

Ianthe

Ianthe is one of the Oceanids, the water nymphs daughters of the Titans Oceanus and Tethys, in Greek mythology. Her name is usually rendered as "she who delights", and it places her among the companions of Persephone.

The myth

According to the tradition, Ianthe was one of the three thousand Oceanids, water nymphs born to Oceanus and his sister and spouse Tethys. Like her sisters, she accompanied Persephone, the daughter of Demeter, when Hades abducted her.

Her name allows several readings. It may derive from ianthên, "heat", or from ianthos, "violet", although the most widespread sense is "she who delights". Beyond that episode, the sources barely mention her, so she remains a minor figure within the vast group of the Oceanids.

Trivia

Three thousand sisters. Ianthe was one of the three thousand Oceanids, daughters of Oceanus and Tethys, which shows how large the family of water nymphs in Greek mythology was.
