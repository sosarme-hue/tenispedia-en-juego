Trojan prince, son of King Laomedon

Hicetaon

A Trojan prince, son of King Laomedon and brother of Priam, remembered for advising that Helen be returned to Menelaus to avert the Trojan War.

The myth

According to the tradition, Hicetaon was a son of King Laomedon of Troy and therefore a brother of Priam. He was one of the Trojan elders. When Paris abducted Helen, Hicetaon urged that she be returned to Menelaus to avoid war, advice that was not heeded.

His sons had different fates: Melanippus died in the very war his father hoped to prevent, while Critolaus married Aristomache, Priam's daughter. The Aeneid of Virgil also names Thymoetes as his son, whereas other accounts make him a brother. Some authors suggest Antenor may have been a son of Hicetaon as well.

Trivia

A namesake on Lesbos. Another Hicetaon was a prince of Methymna on the island of Lesbos, son of King Lepetymnus. According to the account, Achilles killed him when he attacked the islands close to the mainland.
Brothers of Priam. As a son of Laomedon, Hicetaon was a brother of Priam, the king who ruled Troy during the war against the Achaeans.
