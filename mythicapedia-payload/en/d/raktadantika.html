fierce form of the goddess Durga in Hinduism

Raktadantika

Raktadantika, whose name means "she whose teeth are red", is a fearsome form of the Hindu goddess Durga. She appears in the tradition as a warrior manifestation of the divine mother.

The myth

According to the Devi Mahatmya, Durga took the form of Raktadantika to slay and devour the danava children of the asura Viprachitti. Her teeth turned as red as pomegranate blossoms, which is why she received her name.

The same episode appears in the Markandeya Purana. Some accounts describe her as a form of Kali with an entirely red body, and she is said to live in a pomegranate tree, her red teeth likened to the red seed pods of the fruit.

Ancient sources: The Devi Mahatmya and the Markandeya Purana recount her episode against the danavas of Viprachitti.

Trivia

Teeth like pomegranates. Her name refers to the colour of her teeth, likened to pomegranate blossoms. Some accounts say she lives in a pomegranate tree, and her teeth recall the red seed pods of its fruit.
