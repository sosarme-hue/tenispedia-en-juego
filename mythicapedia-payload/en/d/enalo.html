Lesbian hero rescued by dolphins in Greek mythology

Enalus

Enalus is a man from Lesbos who, according to Greek tradition, saved his beloved from a sea sacrifice with the help of dolphins. His story is told as part of the founding of the island by its first settlers.

The myth

According to the myth, the Penthelides, the first settlers of Lesbos, received an oracle from Amphitrite ordering them to sacrifice a bull to Poseidon and a virgin to Amphitrite and the Nereids once they reached the rock Mesogeion on their voyage.

The colony's leaders had their daughters draw lots, and the chosen one was the daughter of Smintheus or Phineus. As she was about to be thrown into the sea, her lover Enalus embraced her and leapt into the depths with her. Dolphins rescued both of them.

Later, the sea around Lesbos rose in such huge waves that no one dared approach it. Enalus alone had the courage to go out. When he came back from the waters, octopuses followed him, and the largest carried a stone, which Enalus took and dedicated in a temple.
