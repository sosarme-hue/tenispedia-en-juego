Slavic spirit of the farmyard

Dvorovoy

The dvorovoy is a spirit of Slavic folklore who was linked to the grounds of a farmstead, including its yard and cattle shed. It is a close relative of the domovoi, the household spirit, but it was less kindly and was regarded with greater wariness by farmers.

The myth

In Slavic tradition, the dvorovoy watched over the outdoor areas of the homestead: the courtyard, the cattle shed and the stable. It was thought of as a spirit tied to one particular place, the farm plot itself.

Where the domovoi was associated with the house and generally seen as a guardian of the family, the dvorovoy had a more unsettling nature. Accounts describe it as less benevolent and more dangerous, able to threaten livestock, especially animals with white fur.
