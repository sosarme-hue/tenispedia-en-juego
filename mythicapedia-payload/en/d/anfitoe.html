Greek Nereid of the sea currents

Amphithoe

Amphithoe is a Greek Nereid, daughter of Nereus and Doris, associated with the sea currents and, according to some sources, identified with Amphitrite. She belongs to the company of sea nymphs who gather around Thetis during one of her moments of grief.

The myth

In Greek tradition, Amphithoe was a Nereid, a daughter of the Old Man of the Sea, Nereus, and the Oceanid Doris. Her domain was the currents of the sea, and her name appears alongside those of her sisters. One source suggests she may be the same figure as Amphitrite, although this is not certain.

The episode that recalls her has Thetis crying out in sympathy with Achilles over the death of his friend Patroclus. Amphithoe and her sisters then come to her side and share her lament from the depths of the sea.

Trivia

Possible identity with Amphitrite. Some sources suggest Amphithoe may be the same figure as Amphitrite, the wife of Poseidon, although not all texts confirm this.
Companion of Thetis. Amphithoe appears in the myth alongside her sister Nereids, who come to comfort Thetis when she mourns Achilles' grief.
