Roman goddess of birth and childbirth

Candelifera

Candelifera is a goddess in Roman mythology linked to birth and childbirth, and she belongs to the Dei Natalitii, the deities who watch over the arrival and early life of children. Her name, "the one who brings to light", refers to her role in delivery.

The myth

According to tradition, Candelifera lit a candle in her heavenly palace at the moment a birth took place. The flame signalled that the goddess was present for the child's arrival into the world.

The tradition records no further episodes or family ties, so her figure is limited to this protective role.

Worship and religion

During labour it was customary to light a votive candle so that the goddess would look favourably on the mother and the newborn. There is no information about her own temples or festivals, nor about whether this cult survives.
