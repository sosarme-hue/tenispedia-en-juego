Chinese dragon of hidden treasures

Fucanglong

The fucanglong is a dragon from Chinese mythology that guards buried treasure, both natural and man-made. It matters because it links the underworld with volcanoes and with the imagery of the Immortals.

The myth

In Chinese tradition, the fucanglong dwells underground and watches over buried treasure, whether mineral veins or riches laid down by people. When these dragons wish to report to heaven, they burst up through the earth with such force that volcanoes form.

The creature also appears on roof tiles and light reliefs, where it serves as a mount for the Immortals. Its most treasured possession is a magic pearl, which it guards jealously.

Legacy

Its image recurs in the decoration of roof tiles and light reliefs, where it is shown as a mount for the Immortals.

Trivia

Volcanoes as messengers. Tradition explains volcanic eruptions as the moment these dragons burst from the ground to report to heaven.
