Egyptian prince in Greek mythology

Chaetus

Chaetus was, in Greek mythology, one of the sons of King Aegyptus. His fate became bound to that of the Danaids, as he married one of them, the Danaid Asteria.

The myth

According to tradition, Chaetus was a son of Aegyptus and an Arabian woman, so he shared a mother with several brothers, including Istrus, Chalcodon, Agenor, Alces and Hippolytus. Some accounts make him a son of Aegyptus by Eurryroe, daughter of the river-god Nilus, or by Isaie, daughter of King Agenor of Tyre.

Chaetus suffered the same fate as his brothers, except Lynceus: on their wedding night, their wives, obeying the command of their father, King Danaus of Libya, killed them. Chaetus had married Asteria, daughter of Danaus, born to the hamadryads Atlanteia or Phoebe depending on the version.
