king of the Kambojas in the Mahabharata

Sudakshina

Sudakshina is a king of the Kambojas who appears in the Mahabharata, the great Hindu epic. He is remembered for his clash with the hero Arjuna during the battle of Kurukshetra.

The myth

In the epic's account, on the fourteenth day of the Kurukshetra War, Arjuna, with Krishna as his charioteer, tries to reach Jayadratha. Drona and Duryodhana place warriors in his path to stop him before sunset.

Sudakshina rallies a fleeing Kaurava akshauhini and challenges Arjuna. He hurls a spear that strikes him, and Arjuna slumps in his seat, bleeding. The Kaurava army erupts in cheers, believing him dead. But Arjuna recovers quickly and, enraged, invokes the Indrastra, which multiplies into countless arrows and shatters the Kaurava ranks. Sudakshina falls to one of these arrows.
