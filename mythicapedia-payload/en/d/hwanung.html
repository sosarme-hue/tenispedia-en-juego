divine hero of Korean mythology

Hwanung

Hwanung is the son of the Lord of Heaven who, according to the Korean founding legend, descended to earth to establish Sinsi and became the father of Dangun, the legendary founder of Gojoseon. His story is central to the account of the origins of the Korean people.

The myth

According to the Dangun founding legend, Hwanung, son of Hwanin, longed to live on earth among its valleys and mountains. His father allowed him to depart with three thousand followers, and they descended to a sandalwood tree on Mount Taebaek, where the Yalu and Tumen rivers rise. There he founded Sinsi, the "City of God", and named himself Heaven King, carrying the Three Heavenly Seals.

In a nearby cave a bear and a tiger asked to be turned into humans. Hwanung gave them garlic and mugwort and required them to stay out of sunlight for one hundred days. The tiger gave up, but the bear endured for twenty-one days and was transformed into the woman Ungnyeo, who later became Hwanung's wife and bore Dangun.
