Heliad nymph of Greek mythology

Merope

Merope is one of the Heliades, the nymph daughters of the sun god Helios in Greek mythology. Her story is tied to the mourning for her brother Phaethon and to the origin of amber.

The myth

Merope was one of the Heliades, nymph daughters of Helios. Accounts disagree about her mother: some name the Oceanid Merope, others Clymene. She belonged to the family of her brother Phaethon, whose death affected all his sisters.

Merope was among those who mourned Phaethon most. According to the myth, their lamentations lasted four months, until their tears hardened into amber. In the end, she and her sisters were turned into poplars, which still drip their amber into the river Eridanus.
