Myrmidon warrior of Greek mythology

Bathycles

Bathycles was a Thessalian Myrmidon warrior who fought in the Trojan War on the side of the Achaeans. Son of Chalcon, his death at the hands of Glaucus makes him one of the minor casualties of the Homeric account.

The myth

According to tradition, Bathycles came from Thessaly and belonged to the Myrmidons, the warrior band that joined the Greeks at Troy. His father was named Chalcon, a detail that the sources record when naming him.

During the Trojan War he fought alongside the Achaeans until Glaucus killed him. His death appears in book XVI of the Iliad, where the poem remembers him as one of many warriors who lost their lives in the fighting.

Ancient sources: Homer's Iliad, book XVI.
