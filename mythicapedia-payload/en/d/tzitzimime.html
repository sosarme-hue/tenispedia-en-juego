star goddesses of Aztec mythology

Tzitzimimeh

The Tzitzimimeh were celestial deities of Aztec mythology, linked to the stars and especially to solar eclipses. Although colonial writers called them demons, they were also tied to fertility and to the protection of women.

The myth

According to tradition, they were female star beings that tried to stop the Sun from rising, attacking it at dawn, at dusk and above all during eclipses. In the myth of the Fifth Sun, when the world comes to an end they would descend as skeletal beings to devour humanity.

They dwelt in Tzitzimitlan, a layer of Mictlan. Their leader is named differently across sources: some name Itzpapalotl, ruler of Tamoanchan, while others name Tenantzitzímitl, mother of Tlacatecólotl and Ehécatl.

Worship and religion

Midwives and women in childbirth venerated them. They were feared during the unlucky nemontemi days and at the New Fire ceremony that closed the 52-year calendar round, periods linked to the fear of change.

Legacy

They appear as antagonists in the series Victor and Valentino and in the tabletop RPG Pathfinder. In the Megami Tensei franchise, players can summon them as demons.

Trivia

A Scooby-Doo monster. They appear in the episode «Mysteries On The Disorient Express» of Be Cool, Scooby-Doo!, among eight monsters from around the globe.
A granny in Genshin Impact. Citlali, also known as Granny Itzli, features the Tzitzimitl Citlalin and Itzpapa in her kit, shown as large plush figures.
