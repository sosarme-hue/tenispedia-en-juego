Yoruba god of creation and purity

Obatala

Obatala is the king of the orishas in the Yoruba religion and, according to tradition, the creator of dry land and human beings. He is also the memory of a real king of Ile-Ife who was deified after death, which makes him one of the most revered deities in West Africa and the Afro-American diaspora.

The myth

According to Yoruba tradition, Obatala is the oldest of the orishas and was entrusted by Olodumare, the Supreme Deity, with creating the earth. In some versions, however, he drank palm wine and his younger brother Oduduwa seized the chance to use the satchel of creation and raise land above the primeval ocean.

He was then asked to create human beings. He climbed down a long golden chain carrying a snail shell full of sand, a white hen, a black cat and a palm nut. The hen scattered the sand to form mountains and valleys, and the nut grew into a forest of palms. Obatala shaped figures from clay but, drunk on palm wine, made some of them deformed. Once sober, he vowed never to drink again and to protect people with disabilities.

Worship and religion

His worship is widespread in Nigeria and is re-enacted each year in Ile-Ife, in the Obatala festival and the coronation rites of the Ooni. In Brazilian Candomblé his day is Friday, devotees dress in white, and Salvador celebrates the Festa do Bonfim. His offerings are white animals and are cooked in honey.

Legacy

In Cuban Santería he is linked with Our Lady of Mercy and Jesus of Nazareth, and in Brazil with Our Lord of Bonfim. The white clothing of his devotees has become a general symbol of Candomblé.

Ancient sources: The main sources are the oral traditions of Ife and those recorded in Africa and the Americas, not ancient written texts.

Trivia

A snail as offering. In Brazil the snail Achatina fulica is offered to Obatala, because it shares the local name igbin with the giant African snail used in Yorubaland.
No fixed sex. Some priests say Obatala has no sex, as Father of Creation, and he is the only male orisha who accepts female animals as offerings.
