Greek nereid of sea travel

Pontoporeia

Pontoporeia is a nereid of Greek mythology, associated with crossing the sea. She is one of the fifty marine daughters of Nereus and Doris, and her name means "the one who crosses the sea".

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Pontoporeia, also written Pontoporea or Pontopereia, was a daughter of Nereus, the Old Man of the Sea, and of the Oceanid Doris. Her name in Greek, Ποντοπόρεια, means roughly "the one who travels the sea", which is why she was linked to navigation and to the passage over the waters.

She belonged to the large group of Nereids, the fifty marine daughters of this couple, each with her own sphere of protection. Pontoporeia was associated with crossing the sea, that is, the journey of those who set out on the water by ship. She is known mainly through her genealogy and her role, since the accounts that mention her are brief and do not record major episodes of her life.
