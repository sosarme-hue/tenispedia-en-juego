nightmare daimon of Greek mythology

Epiales

Epiales is, in Greek mythology, the daimon who embodies nightmares and lurks over those who sleep. The name appears in several spellings, and his figure is tied to the idea of a spirit that weighs down on sleep.

The myth

According to tradition, Epiales was a nocturnal daimon who slipped into people's dreams to bring them anguish. In the sources his name is confused with that of a similar spirit, Epialtes or Ephialtes, which reflects how closely the two were linked.

Ancient texts show that the word also designated the feverish chill, and that the daimon and the sensation of nocturnal oppression shared the same root. Apollonius of Rhodes says that Epialtes, the nightmare daimon, is itself called Epiales, and that with a change of vowel it is also called Epioles.

Ancient sources: Aeschylus mentions them in the plural, as the "black dreams", in a passage where a woman calls on the Earth to drive away a nightmare. Homer and most writers use the form Epioles, and Alcaeus the form Epialos.
