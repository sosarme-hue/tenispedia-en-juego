hero of Greek mythology, great-grandson of King Nestor

Alcmaeon

Alcmaeon is a figure of Greek mythology, the great-grandson of King Nestor. His lineage gave its name to the influential Athenian family of the Alcmaeonidae, who claimed descent from him.

The myth

According to tradition, Alcmaeon was the son of Sillus, who was the son of Trasimedes, who was in turn the son of Nestor. His story is tied to the expulsion of Nestor's descendants from Messenia at the hands of the Heraclids, the descendants of Heracles who claimed those lands.

Alcmaeon, together with Peon, son of Antilochus and brother of Trasimedes, sought refuge in Athens. In the telling, that exile gave rise to two lineages: the Alcmaeonidae, descended from Alcmaeon, and the Peonidae, descended from Peon.

Legacy

His name survives above all in the Alcmaeonidae, one of the most important clans of Athens, who regarded themselves as his descendants. In this way, a mythical figure lent prestige to a real Athenian family.
