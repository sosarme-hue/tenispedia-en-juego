Elean general and one of the Moliones in Greek myth

Eurytus

Eurytus is an Elean general in Greek myth, one of the twin Moliones alongside his brother Ctesus. The twins are known for their great strength and for being joined at the waist, and their story is tied to Elis and to Heracles.

The myth

According to the tradition, Eurytus and Ctesus were called the Moliones after their mother, Molione. Accounts of their father differ: some make them sons of Actor, king of Elis, while others name Poseidon.

The brothers took part together in exploits such as the hunt for the Calydonian boar. When Heracles attacked Elis to punish King Augeas for an insult, the Moliones fought his army and could not be defeated in open battle. In the end the hero killed them, but only by means of an ambush.

Eurytus married Theraephone, daughter of King Dexamenus of Oleni, and became the father of Talpius, who fought in the Trojan War.

Trivia

Joined at the waist. Eurytus and his brother are described as joined at the waist, which made them inseparable in battle and in their exploits.
A son at Troy. Talpius, son of Eurytus, took part in the Trojan War, linking this family to the Trojan cycle.
