Hawaiian goddess of fertility and childbirth

Haumea

Haumea is the goddess of fertility and childbirth in Hawaiian mythology, mother or ancestress of many deities, including the volcano goddess Pele. Her name is also borne today by a dwarf planet in the Solar System.

The myth

According to Hawaiian tradition, Haumea could renew her youth again and again, passing from old woman to young girl with the help of a magic stick called the Mākālei. She periodically returned to her homeland to marry one of her descendants, and these unions produced generations of humans. When Kio discovered her true identity, the goddess withdrew from humanity.

She is also credited with a decisive gift: teaching humans to give birth naturally. In one story, she visited Muleiula, the daughter of a chieftain who was suffering a painful labour, since babies could only be born by cutting open the mother. Haumea made a potion from the kani-ka-wī tree (Spondias dulcis) that allowed the baby to be pushed out without surgery.

She was the sister of the gods Kāne and Kanaloa, and sometimes the wife of Kanaloa. With Kanaloa she had Pele and other children, some of whom were born from parts of her body, such as Hiʻiaka. According to the tradition, the hero Kaulu, known for challenging powerful supernatural beings, eventually killed her. She did not return this time, and the episode symbolises the end of an era linked to her power of renewal.

Worship and religion

Her worship is counted among the oldest on the Hawaiian islands. The sources do not describe specific temples or rites.

Legacy

In 2008 the International Astronomical Union named the fifth known dwarf planet in the Solar System after her, Haumea. Its two moons were named after her daughters Hiʻiaka and Namaka, who in the tradition appear as a goddess born from Haumea's mouth and a water spirit born from her body.

Ancient sources: The Hawaiian creation chant Kumulipo lists her descendants.

Trivia

Children born from her body. According to tradition, several of Haumea's children were born from different parts of her body, and only Pele was born in the normal way.
A death without return. Unlike her earlier renewals, Haumea did not come back after her defeat by Kaulu, which marks the end of her presence among the living gods.
