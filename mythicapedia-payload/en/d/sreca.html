Slavic personification of good fortune

Sreća

Sreća is, in the tradition of Serbs and Croats, the personification of good fortune, and her name simply means "luck". She belongs to the world of fate figures of the pre-Christian Slavic religion.

The myth

The sources that record these beliefs say little about Sreća as a character with a story of her own; her role is best understood by contrast with other figures of fate. Among the East Slavs, good fortune was personified as Dolya, whose name means "division" or "participation", while bad luck had its opposite in Nedolya.

Among Serbs and Croats, by contrast, good luck took the form of Sreća, whose name simply means "luck". Like other figures of Slavic destiny, she belonged to a world of forces that shaped each person's fortune, although the sources do not describe her in detailed tales.

Trivia

Two names for luck. Among the East Slavs good luck was called Dolya, and bad luck, its opposite, Nedolya. Sreća is the equivalent in the Serbian and Croatian sphere.
A simple name. Sreća literally means "luck", a name that makes her role clear: she is less a character of stories than fortune itself, personified.
