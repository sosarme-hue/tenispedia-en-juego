Oceanid of Greek mythology

Zeuxo

Zeuxo is one of the Oceanids, the three thousand water nymphs born to the Titans Oceanus and Tethys in Greek mythology. She is an almost anonymous figure, and what makes her interesting is how little has survived about her.

The myth

Her name appears in the catalogue of Oceanids that Hesiod gives in his Theogony, where the daughters of Oceanus and Tethys are listed, but no other surviving ancient literary work mentions her. The name is usually rendered as meaning "yoke" or "cart".

Her only known appearance in art is on a vase painted by the Brygos Painter, where she pours wine for a warrior named Chrysippus.

Legacy

The main belt asteroid 438 Zeuxo is named after her.

Ancient sources: Hesiod's Theogony, which lists her name among the Oceanids.

Trivia

An asteroid named for her. The main belt asteroid 438 Zeuxo is named after this Oceanid.
A cupbearer scene. On the Brygos Painter's vase she appears pouring wine, an unusual role for a water nymph.
