mythical king of the Molossians in Epirus

Aidoneus

Aidoneus is, in Greek tradition, a mythical king of the Molossians, a people of Epirus. His name is also one of the poetic forms used for Hades, the god of the dead.

The myth

Few stories about this king survive. What is recorded is his name, Aïdōneús, which appears in Greek poetry as a variant of Hades's name, alongside inflected forms such as Áïdos and Áïdi. It has traditionally been read as "the unseen one," though other meanings have been proposed.

Even in antiquity its etymology was debated. Plato, in the Cratylus, offers a different reading based on knowledge of noble things. Modern linguists have proposed a Proto-Greek root *Awides, while others suggest the name referred to the one who gathers the dead.
