king of Fernmag in the Ulster Cycle

Éogan mac Durthacht

Éogan mac Durthacht is a king of Fernmag (Farney, County Monaghan) in the Ulster Cycle, the body of heroic tales in Irish mythology. He matters mainly for his role in the tragic story of Deirdre.

The myth

According to the tradition, Éogan was an enemy of Ulster and its king, Conchobar mac Nessa, although he later made peace with them. At a feast given by Mac Da Thó, Cet mac Magach recounted how he had blinded Éogan's eye by hurling back the spear that struck his shield. Some time later, Conchobar ordered Éogan to kill Naoise, Deirdre's husband, and he obeyed. When Conchobar asked the grief-stricken Deirdre whom she hated most, she answered Conchobar himself and Éogan. Conchobar then decided she should spend a year living with Éogan, but the heroine chose death over that fate.
