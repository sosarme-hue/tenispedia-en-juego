Hindu goddess, previous birth of Sita

Vedavati

Vedavati is, in Hinduism, the previous birth of the goddess Sita and a manifestation of Lakshmi. Her story, which links devotion, austerity and destiny, explains the origin of Sita in the Ramayana and several Puranas.

The myth

According to tradition, Vedavati was the daughter of Kushadhvaja, son of Brihaspati, the guru of the devas. Her father, who had devoted his life to chanting and studying the Vedas, regarded her as the fruit of his devotion to Lakshmi, and she recited Vedic mantras as soon as she was born.

Her father wanted her to marry Vishnu, so he turned away many kings and celestial beings who sought her hand. The demon Sumbha, outraged, murdered her parents on a moonless night. Vedavati stayed in the ashram, meditating day and night to win Vishnu as her husband. The Ramayana describes her dressed in the hide of a black antelope, her hair matted in a jata like a rishi.

Ravana, king of Lanka, found her meditating and was captivated by her beauty. After she rejected his proposals, he tried to assault her. Vedavati cursed him, declaring that she would be reborn and cause his death, and then threw herself into a nearby havan fire. According to the Brahma Vaivarta Purana, Parvati had granted her a boon earlier: Vedavati asked for Narayana as her husband in each of his incarnations, and the goddess promised she would be the consort of Rama during the Treta Yuga. Lakshmi was then reborn as a child in the kingdom of Mithila, where King Janaka found her and raised her as his daughter Sita.

In other versions, such as the Devi Bhagavata Purana, Vedavati appears as Maya Sita, an illusory double. When Ravana was about to abduct Sita, she took refuge in the fire and left Maya Sita in her place, who was carried off to Lanka. After Ravana's death, during the trial by fire, Agni revealed Vedavati's identity. She asked Rama to marry her, and he declined, promising her his hand in another incarnation.

Ancient sources: The Ramayana, the Brahma Vaivarta Purana and the Devi Bhagavata Purana record her story and its variants.

Trivia

Maya Sita. In some versions, Vedavati is not only Sita's earlier life but the illusory double that Ravana carries off to Lanka.
Padmavati. According to some sources, Vedavati was reborn as Padmavati, daughter of Akasha Raja, and married Venkateswara.
