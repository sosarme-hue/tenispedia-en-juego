Aztec god of fire and Venus

Xocotl

Xocotl is a god of Mexica (Aztec) mythology linked to fire and to the planet Venus. The reference sources also present him as a personification of heat and of kinship, although they offer few details about his stories.

The myth

The texts consulted barely describe his stories, so his figure remains rather vague. His link to fire places him among the deities associated with warmth, light and domestic life, while his connection with Venus ties him to the morning and evening star.

One source also describes him as god of twins and of the stars, an attribution that does not appear in other descriptions. Versions vary and no clear genealogy can be taken as certain, so these traits are best treated as a poorly documented tradition.
