Hindu folk deity of Jammu and Kashmir

Baba Shivo

Baba Shivo, also known as Goran Baba, is a Hindu folk deity worshipped in Jammu and Kashmir and Himachal Pradesh. He is regarded as a warrior-hero and as an avatar of Rudra Ansh, and he lives on in the folklore of Jammu.

The myth

According to tradition, King Ladh Dev, Shivo's father, had no children. His astrologer warned him that his destiny allowed only one gift: either a kingdom or a child. On the advice of yogis, he gave up the throne to his younger brother and, with Queen Kalavati, withdrew to the Sauram hills and then to Samotha, where for twelve years they prayed to Gorakhnath.

The sage granted their wish: a son, an incarnation of Rudra, would be born and named Shiv Dev, or Shivo. As a grown man he served King Mal Dev of Jammu. A lion was terrorising the region, and the king offered a reward to whoever killed it; Shivo slew it with his sword, and the impressed king touched his feet and received spiritual teaching from him. Shivo played the dotara, and the women of the Kharadkhatri Rajputs of the Goran hills came out to listen. Jealous men of the clan murdered him while he played: his head stayed behind, and his body reached Goran, where his temple was later built. Tradition holds that his curse destroyed most of those Kharadkhatris.

Worship and religion

The Goran shrine is a pilgrimage site. Devotees who pray with sincere faith and determination make a vow and, once it is fulfilled, offer a bhandara, a community kitchen, at the temple; goats are also sacrificed in his name. He is the kuldev of the Sarmal clan of the Suryavanshi Dogra Rajputs, and Hindu and Muslim devotees visit, mainly on Sundays and Tuesdays. Each year a wrestling match is held in his honour, and the Baba Shivo Chhadi pilgrimage runs from Samotha to Goran.

Legacy

He remains alive in Jammu's folklore and local devotion rather than in wider literature or art.

Trivia

Offering after a wish. Devotees whose wish is granted offer a bhandara, a community meal, at the Goran temple.
Clan deity. He is the kuldev of the Sarmal clan of the Suryavanshi Dogra Rajputs, a family protective deity.
