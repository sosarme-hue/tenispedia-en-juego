Taoist goddess, embodiment of the Tao

Holy Mother the Original Lord

Holy Mother the Original Lord is a Taoist goddess regarded as the embodiment of the Tao. She personifies the feminine principle that gives birth to all existence and sustains it.

The myth

According to ancient Chinese classics, she leads the divine immortals and taught figures such as the Yellow Emperor, Lao Tzu, the Mysterious Goddess of the Ninth Heaven and the Heavenly Emperor. "Original Lord" is a respectful title Taoism gives to high-ranking female immortals, and she is also known by names such as Uncreated Original Empress or Supreme Original Lord.

The idea behind her comes from the Tao Te Ching, which often describes the Tao in feminine terms, as a mother of the universe who brings forth everything that exists and keeps it going. Holy Mother the Original Lord embodies that conception.

Ancient sources: Ancient Chinese classics present her as the leader of the immortals and the teacher of other Taoist deities.
