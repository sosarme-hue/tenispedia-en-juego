semi-legendary Swedish Viking chieftain

Refil Björnsson

Refil is a semi-legendary Viking chieftain of Sweden, son of King Björn Ironside and brother of King Erik. He belongs to the Norse tradition and matters mainly as a link in the genealogy of Swedish kings recorded in the sagas.

The myth

According to the Hervarar saga, written in the 13th century, Refil was the younger son of Björn and a "warrior king and sea king", a raider in the style of his father and grandfather. When his brother Erik died after a short reign, Refil was already dead, since the next ruler was his son Erik Refilsson, whom the saga praises as a great warrior.

Historians doubt that Refil ever existed. The saga is late and compiled from older material, and the name also appears in a list of legendary figures included in Snorri's Edda, in a section on sea kings with poetic names. Many of these names end in -ill, which suggests they were poetic inventions rather than real people.

Ancient sources: The Hervarar saga (13th century) and the Þula of sea kings included in Snorri's Edda.

Trivia

Sea kings with poetic names. Many of the sea kings in Snorri's list have names ending in -ill, which points to them being poetic inventions.
A dynasty tied to Ragnar. The saga places Björn, and therefore Refil, in a line that goes back to Ragnar Lodbrok, Refil's grandfather.
