ghost of Nigerian boarding schools

Madam Koi Koi

Madam Koi Koi is a vengeful ghost from Nigerian urban legend, one of the best-known haunting figures in boarding schools. Her name comes from the clicking sound of her heels, and the tale, told in several versions, has become a staple of school ghost stories across the country.

The myth

In the most widespread version, Madam Koi Koi was a stylish secondary school teacher who wore red heels and was famous for her beauty. Her shoes announced her arrival with a sharp “koi koi” in the hallways, but students remembered her as cruel: she beat them for no reason and once slapped a girl so hard she damaged her ear. After she was dismissed, she died in an accident on her way home, and before dying she swore revenge on the school. Soon students heard that same clicking of heels in the dormitory corridors after lights out.

Another version is darker. Exhausted by the management’s failure to discipline her, the students waited for her one night as she left the school, gagged her and beat her to death. To hide the crime they dumped her body over the back fence and pretended an armed robber had attacked her. Over time, the culprits vanished one by one, except the student who had struck her with a shoe. He insisted he heard heels clacking through his hostel every night, but nobody believed him, and when he went to find the source of the sound, he was beaten to death. The legend says she still roams the corridors, tormenting students and making anyone who looks at her disappear.

Legacy

The legend has inspired Simisayo Brownstone’s children’s book Feyi Fay and The Case of the Mysterious Madam Koi Koi, in which she is the antagonist. Netflix’s horror series The Origin is loosely based on her story, and the film Ms. Kanyin, on Amazon Prime Video, follows a teacher who becomes Madam Koi-Koi to take revenge on the students responsible for her death.

Trivia

Another name. In Ghana she is known as Madam Moke, and some tales also call her Lady Koi Koi.
A missing heel. She is often said to appear wearing only one red heel, since she demands the one that is missing.
