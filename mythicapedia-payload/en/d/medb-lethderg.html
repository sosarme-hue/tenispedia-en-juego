Irish goddess of sovereignty

Medb Lethderg

Medb Lethderg is a goddess of sovereignty associated with Tara in Irish mythology. She matters because she has been identified with, or considered the inspiration for, Medb of the Connachta in the Ulster Cycle.

The myth

According to tradition, Medb Lethderg was the wife or lover of nine successive kings, including Fedlimid Rechtmar, Art mac Cuinn and Cormac mac Airt. Her union with the ruler symbolises the legitimacy of power over the land, a role typical of sovereignty goddesses.

Her name means “red-side”, and she is linked to Rath Meave, a site south of the Hill of Tara. She has been identified with Medb of Connacht, or regarded as the inspiration for this figure of the Ulster Cycle.

Ancient sources: The Book of Leinster, a medieval Irish manuscript, ascribes a poem to her.
