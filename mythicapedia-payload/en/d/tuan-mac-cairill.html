legendary figure of Irish mythology

Tuan mac Cairill

Tuan mac Cairill is a figure of Irish mythology, a hermit who keeps the memories of his earlier lives, reaching back to the age before the Flood. His story links the first settlers of Ireland with the arrival of Christianity on the island.

The myth

According to the tradition, Tuan was originally a follower of Partholon, the first group of settlers to reach Ireland. When a plague, or in other versions the Flood, wiped out his people, he was the only survivor. To stay alive he passed through a series of animal transformations, and so he lived on into Christian times.

In that later period he spoke with Saint Finnian of Moville and told him a brief history of himself and of Ireland, from his original people up to the coming of Saint Patrick. For this reason his figure is often read as a way of linking the island's pagan memory with the new faith.
