deity of Etruscan mythology

Cilens

Cilens is a name recorded among the deities and legendary figures of Etruscan mythology. Its significance rests mainly on surviving Etruscan evidence, such as the bilingual inscriptions that accompany mythological scenes in art.

The myth

The available sources do not allow a story specific to Cilens to be reconstructed: no concrete tales survive explaining its origin, family or deeds. What can be said is that the name appears among those of Etruscan mythology, a field where many names are Etruscan adaptations of Greek figures, although the Etruscans also added their own themes to those stories.

This mixture makes it hard to tell which part of the figure is Etruscan and which comes from Greek or native Italic models, a question scholars still debate. Any detailed claim about Cilens would therefore be pure speculation.
