Ōkuninushi

Ōkuninushi is a kami of Japanese mythology, the lord of the land in the accounts of the Kojiki and the Nihon Shoki and one of the central figures of their myth cycle. His story explains how he handed over the country to the heavenly deities led by the sun goddess Amaterasu.

The myth

According to the Kojiki, Ōnamuji was the youngest of the yasokami, eighty brothers who all wanted to marry the princess Yagamihime. At the Cape of Keta he found a flayed hare, which told him to bathe in fresh water and roll in cattail pollen, and promised that he would win the princess. His brothers killed him twice, but his mother managed to bring him back to life. He then fled to the underworld of Susanoo, where he fell in love with the god's daughter Suseribime and passed the trials he set, with her help.

Later, in the event known as the kuni-yuzuri, the heavenly deities demanded that he give up the rule of the land. Ōkuninushi agreed in exchange for a realm of his own, which made way for the descent of Ninigi, Amaterasu's grandson and ancestor of the Japanese imperial house.

Worship and religion

His worship is strongly tied to Izumo, in western Japan. The Izumo-taisha shrine in Shimane Prefecture is the most famous and pre-eminent of the many shrines dedicated to him across the country. The Izumo-taishakyō sect regards him as its central deity and main focus of worship, and this devotion continues today. Under the shinbutsu-shūgō syncretic system he was identified with the Buddhist deity Daikokuten.

Legacy

Because the first two characters of his name can also be read as Daikoku, he was merged with the Buddhist deity Daikokuten and became popularly known as Daikoku-sama, a name that still appears in popular devotion and imagery.

Ancient sources: He appears in the Kojiki (712) and the Nihon Shoki (720), and the Izumo fudoki devotes many myths to him.

Trivia

A name with many forms. In the ancient texts he receives dozens of names and variants, such as Ōanamuchi or Ōkunitama. Each reflects a different aspect of his role.
The White Hare of Inaba. One of his best-known episodes is that of the flayed hare which, after following his advice, healed and predicted his success in courtship.
