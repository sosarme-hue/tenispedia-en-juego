Peloponnesian princess turned into a bird

Harpalyce

Harpalyce is a Peloponnesian princess, daughter of King Clymenus, whose story of abuse and revenge places her among the tragic figures of Greek mythology.

The myth

According to the tradition, Clymenus desired his daughter and ended up raping her. A son was born from this union, and Harpalyce killed him and served him to her father at a banquet as revenge. When Clymenus learned the truth, he killed her.

In another version, set in Argos, Harpalyce was betrothed to Alastor, but Clymenus forced her into a secret affair and, after the wedding, took her back with him. Harpalyce then killed her younger brother, Presbon, and served him at a feast. After praying to the gods to be taken away from mankind, she was transformed into a bird, a chalkis (a type of owl).

Legacy

One of Jupiter's irregular moons, Harpalyke (also known as Jupiter XXII), is named after her.

Trivia

A moon of Jupiter. Jupiter's irregular moon Harpalyke (Jupiter XXII) takes its name from this figure.
A parallel story. Her tale shares elements with that of Tereus and Procne.
