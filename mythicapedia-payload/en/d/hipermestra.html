Aetolian princess of Greek mythology, mother of Amphiaraus

Hypermestra

Hypermestra is an Aetolian princess in Greek mythology and the mother of the oracular hero Amphiaraus. Her story links the royal house of Aetolia with the city of Argos.

The myth

Hypermestra was the daughter of King Thestius and Eurythemis, and the sister of Althaea and Leda. Oicles chose her as his wife and took her to live in Argos, where she first gave birth to the oracular hero Amphiaraus, who later joined the expedition of the Seven against Thebes, and then to Iphianeira and Endeus.

Like her sisters, she also had relations with the gods, and some authors say Apollo enjoyed her.

The sources do not fully agree on her parentage. Diodorus Siculus names a Hypmnestra as the daughter of Thespius and the mother, by Oicles, of Amphiaraus, Polyboea and Iphianeira, which shows that genealogies varied among ancient writers.

Ancient sources: Diodorus Siculus mentions her as the mother of Amphiaraus, Polyboea and Iphianeira.
