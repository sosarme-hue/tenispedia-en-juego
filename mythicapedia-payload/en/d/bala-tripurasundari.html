Hindu child goddess, younger aspect of Tripura Sundari

Bala Tripurasundari

Bala Tripurasundari, also known as Balambika, is a Hindu goddess shown as a small child. She is usually regarded as the younger aspect and daughter of the goddess Tripura Sundari, and she is a tutelary deity of the Tantric Shri Vidya tradition.

The myth

According to the Lalita Mahatmya, included in the Brahmanda Purana, the young goddess appears as a nine-year-old girl yet possesses immense power. She wanted to confront the forces of the asura Bhandasura and asked her mother's permission to slay his thirty sons.

Tripura Sundari hesitated. She worried about her daughter's young age and her love for her, and reminded her that many Matrikas were ready to join the fight. When the girl insisted, the goddess gave in, handed over her own armour and several weapons, and Bala Tripurasundari slew the thirty sons of Bhandasura in battle.

Worship and religion

In the Tantric Shri Vidya tradition she is worshipped as a tutelary goddess, that is, a protector of her devotees.

Ancient sources: The Lalita Mahatmya, within the Brahmanda Purana, mentions her in chapter 26, where it narrates her battle against Bhandasura.
