minor Roman goddess

Adeona

Adeona is a deity of Roman religion. The reference texts do not describe her, but her name fits the group of minor divinities that Romans invoked for a specific action.

The myth

The available sources preserve no story about Adeona, so her origin, family and deeds cannot be told. They do describe the religious world in which Roman deities linked to childhood moved.

Among them the best known is Juno Lucina, the chief goddess of childbirth. Roman society valued family and tradition highly, and the births and deaths of children were marked by their own rites, although families grieved deeply regardless of ceremony.

Worship and religion

There is no information in the sources about temples, festivals or rites dedicated to Adeona. Full funeral rites, such as the Parentalia, were reserved for those who died after the age of ten, while infants under one year old received no formal ceremonies.
