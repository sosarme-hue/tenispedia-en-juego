Osa de Ándara

The Osa de Ándara is a being from Cantabrian folklore who lives in the Picos de Europa, in the Ándara area. Half woman and half bear, she is a feared figure in the mountain stories.

The myth

According to tradition, the Osa appears when the weather turns mild and vanishes again when the snows arrive, withdrawing into the mountains. She looks like a woman of middle age, though she is not quite one: her face is somewhat blurred, her hands are huge and dark, and her arms and legs are covered in bear-like fur. She wears an old, shabby dress and has long dark hair. When she gets angry, she squints.

She is fierce and very strong, though she rarely shows her aggression. Her mischief mostly involves getting hold of what she needs to eat: milk, chestnuts, roots, raw maize, berries from certain trees and, now and then, a young goat.
