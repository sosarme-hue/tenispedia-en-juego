Nereid of Greek mythology

Sao

Sao is one of the Nereids of Greek mythology, the fifty daughters of the sea god Nereus and the Oceanid Doris. Her Greek name, Σαώ (Saô), means «the rescuer», a trait that sums up her role at sea.

The myth

The Nereids were sea nymphs who lived in the depths with their father Nereus, an old and wise god of the Aegean, and who attended other sea deities. Sao belongs to this group, and her name links her to aiding those who sail and find themselves in danger. Like the other Nereids, she was imagined near ships and shores, and her name fits the protection people sought from the sea.

The surviving texts record hardly any specific episodes involving her, so her figure is known mainly through her parentage and the meaning of her name.
