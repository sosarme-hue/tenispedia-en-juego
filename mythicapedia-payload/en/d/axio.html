river god of Greek mythology

Axius

Axius is a river god of Greek mythology linked to a waterway of ancient Paeonia and Macedonia, today known as the Vardar. He matters mainly as the ancestor of heroic lineages.

The myth

According to the Greek tradition, Axius was a son of Oceanus and Tethys, and so belonged to the great family of waters that encircled the world. His domain was the river that bears his name, flowing through the ancient region of Macedonia, in Paeonian territory.

By Periboea, daughter of Acessamenus, he fathered Pelagon, and through him the river became an ancestor of Euphemus. From that line came Eurybarus, the hero who slew the drakaina Sybaris. In this way the river god appears in the myth as the root of a line of heroes rather than as the protagonist of deeds of his own.

Trivia

A river that changed its name. The river Axius is known today as the Vardar, the name by which it is identified in the ancient region of Macedonia.
A descendant of Axius. Eurybarus, a descendant of Axius through Euphemus, is the hero who slew the drakaina Sybaris.
