daughter of Scáthach in Irish and Scottish mythology

Uathach

Uathach is a figure from Irish and Scottish mythology, daughter of the warrior Scáthach and niece of Aífe, her mother's rival. Her story is tied to the training of the hero Cú Chulainn.

The myth

According to Irish and Scottish tradition, Uathach was the daughter of Scáthach, mistress of the fortress Dún Scáith, the 'Fortress of Shadows'. Through her mother she was also the niece of Aífe, Scáthach's sister and rival, so the young woman was caught in a web of family rivalry.

When Cú Chulainn arrived at Dún Scáith to train as Scáthach's pupil, he accidentally broke one of Uathach's fingers. Her suitor, Cochar Croibhe, then challenged him to single combat despite her protests. Cú Chulainn killed him and from then on became Uathach's lover.

The episode places the future hero at the stage of his training, before his deeds made him famous.
