Nereid of Greek mythology

Protomedea

Protomedea is one of the fifty Nereids of Greek mythology, the sea-nymph daughters of the sea god Nereus and the Oceanid Doris. Her name is usually rendered as "the first ruler" or "first counsellor", a hint of the authority attributed to her among her sisters.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Protomedea was one of the numerous Nereids, who lived in the sea with their father Nereus and their mother Doris. The available material presents her mainly through her lineage and the meaning of her name, without recounting feats of her own.

That name, meaning "the first to rule" or "first queen, first counsel", suggests that she held a place of prominence within the group of sisters. Her story is thus reduced to her position in the marine family, where she is remembered more as part of a chorus of fifty sisters than as the protagonist of individual tales.

Trivia

A name with meaning. The name Protomedea is interpreted as "the first to rule" or "first counsellor". It is one of the few individual traits attributed to her.
