fifteenth Jain tirthankara

Dharmanatha

Dharmanatha is the fifteenth Jain tirthankara of the present age, a teacher who, according to Jainism, attained liberation as a siddha. He is recognised by the thunderbolt emblem.

The myth

According to Jain accounts, he was born in Ratnapuri, son of King Bhanu and Queen Suvrata, in the Ikshvaku dynasty. After ruling his kingdom, he renounced power and worldly attachments to become an ascetic, and attained omniscience.

Tradition gives him a lifespan of 2,500,000 years and a height of 45 bows. He finally achieved liberation on the peaks of Mount Shikharji, in modern-day Jharkhand.

Worship and religion

He is depicted meditating, seated or standing, with a golden complexion and the thunderbolt emblem. On Mount Shikharji a shrine enshrines his footprints and draws pilgrims from across India. The Hutheesing Jain Temple in Ahmedabad, built in 1848 during a famine, honours him as its primary deity.

Trivia

A marble temple with 52 devakulikas. The Hutheesing Jain Temple in Ahmedabad is a two-storied white marble building known for its intricate stone carvings. It contains 52 devakulikas, small shrines dedicated to tirthankaras.
A temple in the deep south. The Jain temple in Mattancherry, Kochi (Kerala), was built in 1904 and serves as a centre of worship for the Gujarati Jain community of the region.
