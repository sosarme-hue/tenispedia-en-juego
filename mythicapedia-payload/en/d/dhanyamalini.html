wife of Ravana and mother of Atikaya in the Ramayana

Dhanyamalini

Dhanyamalini is one of the many junior wives of Ravana, the demon king who antagonizes the Hindu epic Ramayana. She is best remembered as the mother of Atikaya.

The myth

Dhanyamalini appears only rarely in the Ramayana. According to tradition, she is one of Ravana's thousand junior wives, the demon king who opposes the epic's hero. She is also called Dhanyamala or Dhanyamali.

Her best-known role is that of Atikaya's mother. Retellings present her as a secondary figure, without a personal story told in any detail.

In some versions of the Ramayana, she also had three sons with Ravana: Narantaka, Devantaka and Trishira. These variants do not match the most widely known text, so the number and names of her children change from one tradition to another.

Ancient sources: The Ramayana, in some of its versions.
