demon bear yōkai of the Kiso Valley

Onikuma

The onikuma (鬼熊, "demon bear") is a Japanese yōkai from the Kiso Valley in Nagano Prefecture. It is a bear that, after living for many years, gains the ability to walk upright and raid villages and livestock.

The myth

According to tradition, a bear that lives for many years becomes an onikuma. Its strength is immense: it can move boulders about two metres across and, it is said, can kill a small animal simply by pushing it. It comes down from the mountains to steal horses and cattle, which it carries back to its lair to eat.

Some large stones in the Kiso Valley are known as "onikuma stones" and are attributed to these creatures, which local tradition says hurled them at people from the heights above. To hunt one, hunters hollowed out a large tree to block the entrance of its nest of vines; when the disoriented animal emerged, it was attacked with spears and guns. In the Kyōhō era, the hides of several killed animals each covered six tatami mats or more.

Legacy

In the Monster High series, an onikuma named Kuma keeps its ability to throw rocks but can be subdued by tickling. It also features in the short film Onikuma (2016) by Alessia Cecchet, and gave its name to the extreme metal band OniKuma from West Virginia.

Ancient sources: Ehon Hyaku Monogatari (1841), an illustrated collection of Japanese supernatural tales that describes it.

Trivia

Onikuma stones. Large rocks in the Kiso Valley are called "onikuma stones" and are credited to these creatures because of their size and placement.
A ticklish weakness. In the Monster High series, the onikuma can be subdued by tickling, though it keeps its ability to throw rocks.
