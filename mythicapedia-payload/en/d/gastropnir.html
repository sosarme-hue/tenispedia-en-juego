giant's wall in Norse mythology

Gastropnir

Gastropnir is a mythical wall in Norse mythology, built from the limbs of the giant Leirbrimir and located in the realm of the giantess Menglöð. It appears in the poem Fjölsvinnsmál, part of the Eddic tradition, as the barrier that Svipdag must pass to reach her.

The myth

According to the poem Fjölsvinnsmál, included in the Poetic Edda within the tale of Svipdagsmál, Gastropnir is where the giantess Menglöð lives, in the realm of the giants, Jötunheim, which in these traditions was locked in constant war with the Norse gods.

In the poem, Svipdag reaches a gate in the wall and speaks with a watchman called Fjölsviður. When Svipdag asks what the wall is called, the guardian answers that it is Gastropnir and adds that he built it from the limbs of Leirbrimir, so that it would never give way as long as the world lasts.

Ancient sources: It appears in stanza 28 of the poem Fjölsvinnsmál, within the Poetic Edda.
