figure of Norse mythology, mother of the warriors

Erna

Erna is a woman in Norse mythology who appears in the Eddic poem Rígsþula. According to the account, she was the mother of twelve sons by Jarl, the ancestors of the warrior class of Norse society.

The myth

According to the Rígsþula, Erna was the daughter of Hersir, a tribal chief. She was married to Jarl and bore him twelve sons, whom tradition regards as the ancestors of Norse warriors. In this way the account places her as a link between a line of chieftains and a line of warriors.

The text gives no details of her deeds or her end, so her figure is known mainly through this genealogy. Her twelve sons appear as the origin of a social group rather than as characters with stories of their own in this poem.

Ancient sources: Rígsþula, a poem of the Poetic Edda.
