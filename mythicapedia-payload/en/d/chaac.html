Maya god of rain and thunder

Chaac

Chaac is the Maya god of rain, thunder, and lightning, and a patron of agriculture. His cult was especially important on the Yucatán Peninsula, where the lack of large rivers made rainfall vital, and caves and cenotes were seen as entrances to the underworld.

The myth

According to tradition, Chaac opened the mountain where maize was hidden by striking it with his lightning axe, one of the best-known myths involving the Chaacs or related rain and lightning deities. In some versions of the Q'eqchi' myth of the Sun and the Moon, the rain deity, brother of the Sun, commits adultery with his brother's wife and is punished; his tears of agony give rise to the rain.

In other versions he appears in full war-like fury, chasing the fleeing Sun and Moon and attacking them with his lightning bolts. He is shown with a human body covered in reptile or amphibian scales, a fanged head, a long pendulous nose, and a lightning axe, and he is linked to the frog or toad.

Worship and religion

Maya farmers prayed to him for rain and good harvests. In sixteenth-century Yucatán, the Ch'a Cháak ceremony centred on a ritual banquet for the rain deities, with four boys imitating frogs, and rituals at the cenotes sought rain and crops. Each year an ocellated turkey (yuum kuuts) was hunted and sacrificed so its blood could be offered to the fields. He is still venerated by farmers today, often under the name Saint Thomas, through the syncretism of Maya and Catholic religion.

Legacy

In 2024, a statue of Poseidon in Progreso, Yucatán, caused controversy among locals who saw it as offensive to Chaac; what began as a joke ended with attempts to destroy it. He appears in Marvel Comics among the Ahau, and in Black Panther: Wakanda Forever Namor and the people of Talokan worship him. He is also a playable warrior in SMITE.

Ancient sources: Much of the Dresden Codex, one of the few surviving Maya codices, is devoted to the Chaacs, and Bishop Diego de Landa described the Chaacs of the four cardinal directions in the sixteenth century.

Trivia

Four Chaacs. Each of the Chaacs of the cardinal directions had a colour: red in the east, white in the north, black in the west, and yellow in the south.
Talokan. In the film Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, the kingdom of Talokan, whose people have blue skin, worships Chaac.
