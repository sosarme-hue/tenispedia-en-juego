Welsh water deity and character in the Mabinogi

Manawydan fab Llŷr

Manawydan fab Llŷr is a figure of Welsh mythology, the son of Llŷr and brother of Brân the Blessed. His name shares a root with the Irish sea god Manannán mac Lir, though in Wales he has almost no connection to the sea.

The myth

In the Second Branch of the Mabinogi he advises his brother Brân, king of Britain, and survives the war with Ireland. After Brân's death he carries the severed head back to Britain, where it is buried as a talisman against invasion.

In the Third Branch, which bears his name, he lives with Pryderi and Rhiannon in Dyfed after a spell empties the land. After several failed attempts to earn a living as a craftsman, he discovers that a mouse stealing his wheat is the wife of Llwyd, the enchanter behind it all.

Legacy

He appears as one of Arthur's warriors in the poem Pa gur yv y porthaur and in the Trioedd Ynys Prydein. Welsh actor Daniel Evans portrays him in the 2003 film Otherworld.

Ancient sources: The Mabinogion (Second and Third Branches), the poem Pa gur yv y porthaur, the Historia Brittonum and the Trioedd Ynys Prydein.

Trivia

Protective head. Brân's head, which keeps speaking during the journey, was buried on the White Hill and was believed to protect against foreign invasion.
Golden shoemaker. The Trioedd Ynys Prydein name him as one of the three golden shoemakers of the Island of Britain.
