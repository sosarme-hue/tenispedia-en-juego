son of Dhritarashtra who defected to the Pandavas

Yuyutsu

Yuyutsu is a character in the Hindu epic Mahabharata. Born to King Dhritarashtra and a Vaishya maidservant, he broke with his Kaurava brothers on the eve of the Kurukshetra War to join the Pandavas, and he was the only son of Dhritarashtra to survive the conflict.

The myth

According to the Mahabharata, Yuyutsu was born after Gandhari, Dhritarashtra's queen, failed to bear a child despite a year of apparent pregnancy. Eager for an heir, Dhritarashtra turned to a maidservant who was neither of the warrior caste nor married to him, and Yuyutsu was the result. His name means "eager to fight," and the text also gives him the names Dhartarashtra, son of Dhritarashtra, and Kauravya, descendant of the Kurus.

Unlike his brothers, Yuyutsu was on good terms with the Pandavas and warned them of Duryodhana's plots, including a scheme to poison water that could have killed Bhima. Just before the battle began, Yudhishthira announced that anyone wishing to switch sides could do so before the conches sounded, and Yuyutsu did. He fought for the Pandavas; on the seventh day Kripacharya wounded him in a sword duel, and on the sixteenth he fought Ulooka, son of Shakuni, wounding him but failing to kill him as he fled. He was one of the eleven warriors who survived the war. When the Pandavas retired at the start of the Kali Yuga, Yudhishthira entrusted him with overseeing the kingdom while Parikshit was made king.

Ancient sources: The Mahabharata, the Hindu epic in which he appears as Dhritarashtra's son and a righteous figure within the Kaurava camp.
