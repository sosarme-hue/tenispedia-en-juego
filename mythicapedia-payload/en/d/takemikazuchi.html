Japanese god of thunder and swords

Takemikazuchi

Takemikazuchi is a deity of Japanese mythology, regarded as a god of thunder and of the sword. He is the chief deity of Kashima Shrine and a key figure in the conquest of the earthly world and in the mythical origin of sumo wrestling.

The myth

According to the Kojiki and the Nihon Shoki, Takemikazuchi was born from the blood that spilled from the sword Totsuka-no-Tsurugi when Izanagi beheaded the fire deity Kagutsuchi. In some passages the sword, called Ame-no-ohabari or Itsu-no-ohabari, is named as his father.

When the heavenly gods set out to conquer the Middle Country, Takemikazuchi was among the chief envoys. Along with Ame-no-torifune he reached Izumo and planted the upturned sword in a wave, demanding that Ōkuninushi surrender the province. His sons agreed, except Takeminakata, who challenged Takemikazuchi to a trial of strength. Takeminakata was defeated and fled to Suwa, where he became the chief deity of its great shrine. This bout is considered the mythical origin of sumo. The Nihon Shoki instead names Futsunushi as his partner, and the versions differ in names and episodes.

Later, during Emperor Jimmu's conquest of the east, Takemikazuchi sent his sword down to a man named Takakuraji, who presented it to the emperor, waking him and ending the evil deities of Kumano. That sword is the main sacred object of Isonokami Shrine.

Worship and religion

Takemikazuchi was originally a local deity revered by the Ō clan, linked to sea travel. Later the Nakatomi clan made him their ujigami, and his cult spread through the Kashima and Katori shrines, where people prayed for military success in the east. He was also venerated at Kasuga Grand Shrine in Nara. Kashima Shrine remains active today.

Legacy

In the Edo period, namazu-e prints showed him trying to subdue the giant catfish that caused earthquakes. He appears as a supporting character in the manga and anime Noragami, as the leader of a familia in DanMachi, as Kanji Tatsumi's initial Persona in Persona 4, and as the name of a Supreme Being in Overlord.

Ancient sources: He appears in the Kojiki and the Nihon Shoki.

Trivia

A founding contest. The bout with Takeminakata is considered the mythical origin of sumo.
A sacred sword. The sword he sent to Jimmu is kept as the main sacred object of Isonokami Shrine.
