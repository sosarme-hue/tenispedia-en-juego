demon duchess of the grimoires

Gremory

Gremory is a demon duchess listed in European demonological grimoires, such as the Ars Goetia. She is described as a beautiful woman wearing a ducal crown and riding a camel, and is credited with revealing hidden treasures and knowing the past, present and future.

The myth

According to the grimoires, Gremory commands twenty-six legions of demons, though some texts give her twenty-seven. The Munich Manual of Demonic Magic, the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum and the Lesser Key of Solomon also credit her with procuring the love of women, especially young ones, while the Liber Officiorum Spirituum links her to women's love more broadly.

Some texts place her crown at the waist, which scholars Stephen Skinner and David Rankine read as a mistranslation of the Latin cingitur, meaning 'encircling her head'.

Legacy

She appears in the anime and manga High School DxD, where her family name belongs to one of the 72 devil noble houses, as a magic class in the game Fire Emblem: Three Houses, in Bloodstained: Ritual of the Night, and in the South Korean series The Judge from Hell (2024).

Ancient sources: Medieval and Renaissance grimoires, such as the Liber Officiorum Spirituum, the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum and the Lesser Key of Solomon, are the written sources that describe her.

Trivia

A house of demons. In High School DxD Gremory is one of the 72 devil noble families of the Ars Goetia, and the series' female lead belongs to it.
A judge from hell. In the South Korean series The Judge from Hell (2024), Kim Ah-young plays Gremory, a demon who punishes other demons who fall in love with humans.
