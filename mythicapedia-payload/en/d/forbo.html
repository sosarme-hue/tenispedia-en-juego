figure of Greek mythology, father of Pronoe

Phorbus

Phorbus is a figure of Greek mythology best known as the father of Pronoe, wife of Aetolus, the founder of Aetolia. He matters chiefly for his place in the genealogy linking Aetolia with the royal house of Calydon.

The myth

In the Greek tradition, Phorbus appears as the father of Pronoe, who married Aetolus, founder of Aetolia. Through his daughter, Phorbus became grandfather of Pleuron and Calydon, and so the ancestor of the Calydonian royal family.

Notable members of that family include Oeneus, Althaea, Leda, Deianira and Meleager. The reference sources present Phorbus mainly as a genealogical link, with no stories of his own.
