bull spirit guardian of Basque mythology

Aatxe

Aatxe is a spirit of Basque folklore who dwells in caves and takes the form of a young red bull. He matters because he is linked to the goddess Mari and because his myth may reach back to the Paleolithic, as suggested by engravings of aurochs and bulls found in Basque caves.

The myth

In Basque tradition, Aatxe is a spirit who lives in a cave or hollow. His name means literally "young bull", and he is also known as Etsai or Aatxegorri, "young red bull". Although he usually appears as a red bull, he is a shapeshifter who can take the form of a man, a cow or a calf.

He emerges from his lair at night, especially in stormy weather. He punishes criminals and malevolent people, but he also protects the community by keeping people at home when danger is near. Some scholars see him as a representative of the goddess Mari or as an enforcer of her will, punishing those who cheat her.

Trivia

Palaeolithic traces. Engravings and paintings of aurochs, bulls and oxen have been found in caves such as Isturits and Sare. Some interpret these images as pointing to a very ancient origin of the myth.
