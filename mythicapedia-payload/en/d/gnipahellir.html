mythical cave in Norse mythology

Gnipahellir

Gnipahellir is a cave in Norse mythology, located at the summit of a mountain, where Garm, the hellhound who guards the gates of Hel, the realm of the dead, is kept chained. It matters because this place holds a creature central to the fate of the world.

The myth

According to Norse tradition, Gnipahellir is an imposing cave whose name is often rendered as "cave on the mountain peak." It is the home of Garmr, a hellhound who guards the gates of Hel.

The lore holds that Garmr is often kept chained here until the onset of Ragnarök, the final battle that heralds the end of the world. When that time comes, his bindings break and he runs free.

Ancient sources: The cave is mentioned in the Vǫluspá, the "Prophecy of the Völva," one of the poems of the Poetic Edda.
