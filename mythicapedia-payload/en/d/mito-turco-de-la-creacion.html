origin tale of the Gaoche (Tiele)

Turkic creation myth

An origin tale of the Turkic peoples, known mainly for the creation of the Gaoche (高車), identified with the Tiele. It matters because it is one of the few surviving records of the cosmogony of these peoples, preserved through Chinese sources.

The myth

Turkic tradition tells how the Gaoche, a people linked to the Tiele, came into being. The tale is known from two Chinese dynastic histories, the Book of Wei and the History of the Northern Dynasties, which recorded it in Chinese.

The reference text available does not describe the specific episodes of the story: it names no characters, places or events. For that reason its development cannot be reconstructed with certainty, only described as a myth of origin that explains the emergence of a people.

Because it survives through outside sources, the myth reaches us filtered through the eyes of Chinese chroniclers, who wrote it down in their own language and according to their own criteria.

Ancient sources: Book of Wei and History of the Northern Dynasties, Chinese chronicles that record the tale.
