divine sword of Japanese myth

Ame no Ohabari

Ame no Ohabari is a sword in Japanese legend and Shinto that the Kojiki presents first as a weapon and later as a deity. It matters because it links the story of the birth of the gods with its own son, Takemikazuchi.

The myth

According to the Kojiki, Ame no Ohabari first appears as the sword Izanagi wields to kill his son Kagutsuchi, born from the fire that burned Izanami. Several other deities are born from the blade and its blood, among them Takemikazuchi, who is listed as the sword's child.

Later, Omoikane nominates the sword to descend from Takamagahara to Ashihara no Nakatsukuni, the human world, but the story ends up sending his son Takemikazuchi instead.

Ancient sources: The Kojiki is the main ancient source that mentions him, in the account of Kagutsuchi's death and the descent to the human world.

Trivia

A sword that is a spirit. Motoori Norinaga, in his commentary on the Kojiki, read the sword as the spirit of the blade. Basil Hall Chamberlain, in his translation, noted that the text contains nothing that supports this reading.
