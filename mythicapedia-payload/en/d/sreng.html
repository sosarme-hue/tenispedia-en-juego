champion of the Fir Bolg in Irish mythology

Sreng

Sreng is a champion of the Fir Bolg, also called the Men of Bolg, in Irish mythology. He matters for his role in the first Battle of Magh Tuiredh and for the agreement that ended his people's fight.

The myth

According to Irish tradition, Sreng fought with an iron club or mace in the first Battle of Magh Tuiredh against Nuada, king of the Tuatha Dé Danann. With one mighty blow he split Nuada's shield in half and cut off his arm at the shoulder.

Though the Fir Bolg were close to defeat, Sreng and the three hundred survivors vowed to fight to the last man. The Tuatha Dé Danann, however, admired their courage so much that they offered them one fifth of Ireland. The Fir Bolg accepted, laid down their arms and chose Connacht.

The stories add that men in Connacht claimed descent from Sreng until the 17th century.

Trivia

A name with variants. The name Sreng is often misinterpreted as Streng, a reading that appears frequently in modern sources.
