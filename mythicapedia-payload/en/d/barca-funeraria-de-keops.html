solar barque of the pharaoh Khufu

Khufu ship

The Khufu ship is a full-size solar barque of about 43 metres in length, sealed into a pit beside the Great Pyramid of Giza around 2500 BC. It is the oldest intact ship known and one of the most important surviving vessels from antiquity.

The myth

In Egyptian belief, solar barques were ritual vessels that carried the resurrected king across the heavens alongside the sun god Ra, accompanying him on his daily journey and through the cycle of life and death.

The ship's exact function remains uncertain. Some researchers think it may have carried the embalmed body of the pharaoh from Memphis to Giza along the Nile, while others suggest Khufu used it during his lifetime as a pilgrimage ship to visit sacred sites. Its raised bow hints that it may also have sailed on open water.

Worship and religion

The ship was part of the funerary equipment meant to accompany the pharaoh into the afterlife. No human remains were found in it, unlike some northern European ship burials.
