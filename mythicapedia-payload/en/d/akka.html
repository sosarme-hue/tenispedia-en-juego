female spirit of Sami shamanism and Finnish mythology

Akka

Akka is a female spirit in Sami tradition and Finnish mythology. In Sami shamanism she appears as part of a group of goddesses called Áhkká, while in Finnish and Estonian mythology she is identified with Maaemä, the earth mother.

The myth

Stories about the Áhkká are fragmentary and were passed down mostly orally, so there is no complete, unified narrative for each of them. Sources record their names, which form a family of female figures, but they do not always describe their roles precisely.

In Finnish mythology, the closest figure is Maaemä, whose name means mother of the earth. In the tradition she is linked to the fertility of the land and the origin of life, a connection she shares with other mother goddesses of the region.

Worship and religion

The available sources say little about worship. In Sami shamanism these figures were part of the ritual life of the noaidi, the shamans, who communicated with the world of spirits.
