Heraclid hero of Greek mythology

Eatus

Eatus is a Heraclid hero of Greek mythology, son of Pheidippus and brother of Policlia. His story explains the origin of the name of Thessaly.

The myth

According to the tale, Eatus was a Heraclid, son of Pheidippus and brother of Policlia. Together they launched a campaign against the Boeotians, who, according to tradition, had once lived in Thessaly.

An oracle had declared that whoever first crossed the river Achelous and set foot on enemy land would rule the region. Policlia feigned an injured foot and asked her brother to carry her across. Near the opposite bank, she leapt onto the land and claimed the kingdom as the oracle required.

Eatus, rather than getting angry, married her, and they had a son, Thessalus, who gave his name to Thessaly. The story shows how the oracle was fulfilled through cunning rather than force.
