lake nymph of Greek mythology

Bolbe

Bolbe is a nymph of Greek mythology, linked to the lake that bears her name in Macedonia, today Lake Volvi. She is a little-known figure, but an intriguing one for her connection with freshwater and with the hero Heracles.

The myth

According to tradition, Bolbe was a daughter of Oceanus and Tethys, and she is described as a woman of great beauty who dwelt in the lake named after her. Like other lake deities, her offspring were the Limnades, nymphs who live in freshwater lakes.

The most cited account comes from Athenaeus, who says that Bolbe bore Olynthus to Heracles. Her figure appears mainly as a link between the lake and the hero, rather than as the protagonist of deeds of her own.

Ancient sources: Athenaeus, in The Deipnosophists, names her as the mother of Olynthus by Heracles.
