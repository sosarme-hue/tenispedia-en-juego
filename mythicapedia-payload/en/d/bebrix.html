mythical king of ancient Greece, father of Pyrene

Bebryx

Bebryx is a king of Greek mythology, the father of Pyrene, and his name is linked to the mythical tribe of the Bebryces.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Bebryx, also known as Bebrycius, was a king about whom few stories survive. Ancient sources remember him mainly as the father of Pyrene, and they say little about his deeds, his reign or the episodes of his life.

His name also appears in connection with a mythical people, the Bebryces. The most common explanation is that this tribe took its name from a hero called Bebrycus, so the king's lineage became tied to the memory of his people. This is a modern interpretation based on the similarity of names, not an explicit statement in the ancient texts.

Pyrene, his daughter, is the figure who best connects him to other stories in the tradition, although the information about both is brief and does not allow a complete genealogy to be reconstructed.

Trivia

A people named after a king. The mythical Bebryces would take their name from this figure, though this is a presumed explanation rather than a firmly established fact.
