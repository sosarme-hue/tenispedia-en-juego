president of Hell in demonology

Foras

Foras is a powerful president of Hell in early modern European demonology, a being who, according to grimoires, commands dozens of legions of demons. He belongs to the Western occult tradition, not to Inca culture.

The myth

Grimoires describing him portray a strong man obeyed by legions of demons. According to these texts, he teaches logic and ethics in all their branches, and knows the virtues of herbs and precious stones.

Whoever summoned him could gain wit and eloquence, become invisible or live a long life, and also uncover treasures and recover lost things. Some authors add that he makes one invincible. Authors disagree on the number of legions: some give twenty-five, others twenty-nine. His name seems to derive from Latin foras, meaning 'outside'.

Legacy

In modern occultism he has passed into tarot: Travis McHenry's Occult Tarot links him to the nine of pentacles and to the zodiacal decan covering roughly 2 to 12 March.

Trivia

Origin of the name. His name seems to derive from Latin foras, meaning 'outside', though its origin is not firmly established.
A tarot card. In Travis McHenry's Occult Tarot he is associated with the nine of pentacles.
