Euboean prince in Greek mythology

Nausimedon

Nausimedon is a prince of Euboea, son of King Nauplius and brother of Oeax and the famous Palamedes. He is a minor figure of Greek mythology, known mainly for his role in the conflict between Aegisthus and Orestes.

The myth

According to tradition, Nausimedon was the son of King Nauplius, ruler of Euboea. His mother is named differently across accounts: she may have been Clymene, daughter of King Catreus, Hesione, or Philyra. He was the brother of Oeax and of the famous Palamedes.

The story places him in the conflict between Aegisthus and Orestes. Nausimedon and his brother Oeax came to Aegisthus's aid in his fight against Orestes, but both were killed by Pylades, Orestes's friend. His story ends there, with no deeds of his own that the tradition especially remembers.
