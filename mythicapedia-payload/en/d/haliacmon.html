river god of the Haliacmon in Greek mythology

Haliacmon

Haliacmon is a river god of Greek mythology who gave his name to the Haliacmon river in Macedonia. His parentage varies depending on the tradition that records him.

The myth

According to one tradition, Haliacmon was a son of Oceanus and Tethys, the pair of Titans whom the ancient Greeks placed at the edges of the world as the source of fresh and salt waters.

Other accounts make him a son of Palaestinus and a grandson of Poseidon, linking him to the sea god through a different line of descent. As a minor river god, his role is mainly to embody the watercourse that bears his name.
