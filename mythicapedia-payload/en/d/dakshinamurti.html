aspect of Shiva as guru, teacher of yoga and wisdom

Dakshinamurti

Dakshinamurti is an aspect of the Hindu god Shiva in his role as guru or teacher. He is revered as the teacher of yoga, music, the Shastras and wisdom, and his icon appears in nearly every temple dedicated to Shiva.

The myth

Tradition presents Dakshinamurti as Shiva teaching in silence to a circle of sages, the rishis, who gather around him beneath a banyan tree. His name literally means "the one facing south", though an alternative derivation links it to dakshinya, the kindness with which the god imparts wisdom.

In his form as Jnana Dakshinamurti he sits on a deer skin throne with four arms. Beneath his right foot lies Apasmara, the personification of ignorance, while the left foot rests folded on his lap. His hand gestures, or mudras, carry their own meanings: the jnana mudra, with thumb and index finger joined, stands for the union of the Paramatman and the jivatman, and the three extended fingers refer either to three spiritual impurities or to three states of consciousness.

The iconography varies by form. In the yoga version he sits in padmasana, meditating, or with one leg supported by a yogic belt, and there are versions holding a veena (Veenadhara) or mounted on the bull Nandi (Rishabharudha).

Worship and religion

Although his icon appears in every Shiva temple, only a few sanctuaries make him the chief deity. The Mahakaleshwar in Ujjain, the only one of the twelve jyotirlingas dedicated to this form, is regarded by Shaivites as a place of learning. In southern India, especially in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, many temples honour him, and the one at Thiruvotriyur, which faces north, is called Vadagurusthalam. The cult remains alive today, with temples also in Mauritius, the United States, and at the Isha Yoga Center in Coimbatore, where a large Adiyogi statue is understood as his representation.

Legacy

His image is associated with the teaching of yoga and meditation in contemporary Hinduism and has spread to the West through yoga centres. Several mantras and stotras are dedicated to him, among them the Dakshinamurti Stotra and his gayatri.
