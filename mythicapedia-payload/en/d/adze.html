vampiric creature of Ewe folklore

Adze

The adze is a vampiric creature from Ewe folklore, told by the peoples of Togo and Ghana. It is believed to possess people and drink their blood while they sleep, so it is seen as a threat to families and neighbours.

The myth

In the wild, the adze takes the form of a firefly, but if someone manages to catch it, it turns into a human shape. In that form it can possess men and women, who are then regarded as witches. Suspicion of possession often falls on particular social situations: women whose brothers' children do better than their own, elderly people when the young start dying and they survive, and the poor who envy the rich.

The creature acts at night, slipping in as a firefly through keyholes, cracks in walls or under closed doors. Once inside the house, it sucks the blood of sleepers, who grow ill and die. Its effects fall on the victim's family or on those the victim envies. There is no known defence against an adze.

Trivia

A possible explanation. It has been suggested that these tales tried to describe the deadly effects of mosquitoes and malaria, whose transmission was not understood at the time.
