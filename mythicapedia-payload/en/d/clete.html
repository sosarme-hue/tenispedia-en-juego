Amazon of Greek myth, founder of the city of Clete

Clete

Clete, also known as Cleta, was an Amazon in Greek myth and the nurse of Queen Penthesilea. Her story explains the origin of an Italian city that bears her name.

The myth

When news reached her that Penthesilea had been killed by Achilles, Clete set sail for Troy, hoping to recover her queen's body and give it a proper burial. A storm, however, drove the ship onto the Italian coast. There, in fulfilment of a prophecy spoken by the princess Cassandra, she founded the city of Clete.

The city grew and prospered until it went to war with neighbouring Croton. Clete led its army, but she was killed by the Crotonians. Before dying, she asked that all the queens of the city should bear her name. She had a son, called Caulo or Caulon, who later founded the city of Caulonia.
