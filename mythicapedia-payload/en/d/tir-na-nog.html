Otherworld island of eternal youth in Irish mythology

Tír na nÓg

Tír na nÓg, the "Land of Youth", is a mythical island in Irish mythology and one of the names of the Celtic Otherworld. According to tradition, the Tuatha Dé Danann settled there after leaving Ireland, and time seems to stand still on its shores.

The myth

According to tradition, the island lies far to the west and can only be reached after a long voyage or by invitation from one of its inhabitants. There is no sickness or death there, and it is described as a paradise of music, beauty, strength and everlasting youth. Tales of heroes and monks who visited it were very popular in the Middle Ages.

The best-known episode is that of Oisín, a warrior of the Fianna, who falls in love with Niamh Chinn Óir, a woman of the Otherworld. She takes him to the island on a magical horse that crosses the sea. After three years there, Oisín grows homesick and returns to Ireland, though Niamh warns him never to touch the ground. On arrival he finds that three hundred years have passed. As he tries to help some men move a heavy stone, he falls from the horse and ages instantly. Another version has him, now an old man, telling Saint Patrick about the wonders of the Otherworld.

Another tale tells of a king of the island who, fearing he will lose his throne, turns his daughter's head into a pig's. She can only regain her own head by marrying a son of Fionn mac Cumhaill, who turns out to be Oisín. The couple return to the island, and Oisín wins the throne in a contest.

Legacy

The story of Oisín closely resembles the Japanese tale of Urashima Tarō. Other similar stories include that of King Herla, who returns two centuries after visiting the Otherworld, and that of the Seven Sleepers of Ephesus, Christian youths who woke about 180 years after falling asleep in a cave.

Ancient sources: Medieval Irish tales of adventures and voyages to the Otherworld, known as echtrae and immrama, record this place.

Trivia

The path of Mag Mell. The sea route to the island is called Mag Mell, the "Plain of Honey". It is the golden path the sun lays across the ocean.
A sea god. Manannán mac Lir almost always rules this region in the surviving tales, and his name is linked to the island in nearly every version.
