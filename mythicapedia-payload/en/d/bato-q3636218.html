charioteer of Amphiaraus in Greek myth

Baton

Baton is a figure of Greek mythology, the charioteer of the hero Amphiaraus and a member of the house of the seer Melampus. His story survives mainly in local traditions from Argos.

The myth

According to the tradition, Baton and Amphiaraus belonged to the same house of the legendary seer Melampus, and Baton drove the hero's chariot. After the war of the Seven against Thebes, both were swallowed up by the earth, a fate they share in the story.

A later episode also exists: according to Stephanus of Byzantium, a sixth-century grammarian, after Amphiaraus disappeared Baton emigrated to the town of Harpyia in Illyria. Stephanus seems to confuse the mythical Baton here with one of the historical people named Bato in Illyria.

Worship and religion

After his death he was worshipped as a hero and had a sanctuary at Argos. The Argives dedicated a statue of him at Delphi, standing beside one of Amphiaraus.

Legacy

Baton is shown on the chest of Cypselus, an ancient work of art.

Ancient sources: Stephanus of Byzantium, a sixth-century grammarian, mentions him in recounting his supposed emigration to Illyria.

Trivia

Statue at Delphi. The Argives dedicated a statue of Baton at Delphi, placed beside one of Amphiaraus.
A possible mix-up. Stephanus of Byzantium may have confused Baton with a historical Illyrian figure who bore the same name.
