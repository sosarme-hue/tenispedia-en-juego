Mahabharata warrior, born female and later male

Śikhaṇḍī

Śikhaṇḍī is a character in the Hindu epic Mahabharata. Son of Drupada, king of Panchala, he was born female (Śikhaṇḍinī) and became male after a sex exchange with a yaksha. His role in the Kurukshetra War was decisive in bringing about the death of Bhishma.

The myth

In most versions of the Mahabharata, Śikhaṇḍī was in a previous life the princess Amba, eldest daughter of the king of Kashi. Bhishma abducted her at her svayamvara, but she loved King Salva, who rejected her. Bhishma also refused to marry her because of his vow of celibacy, and Parashurama, his teacher, failed to make him change his mind.

Amba withdrew to practise austerities, and Shiva granted her wish for Bhishma's death. She would be reborn as a woman, later become a man, and kill him in battle. Amba immolated herself, and the blue lotus garland she had received from Kartikeya ended up hanging at the gate of King Drupada.

Drupada, who had no sons, raised Śikhaṇḍinī as a boy. When the king of Dasharna discovered the deception, the young man fled into a forest to fast to death. There the yaksha Stunakarna agreed to exchange sexes with him. Kubera made the change permanent, on the condition that it would be reversed after Śikhaṇḍī's death.

At Kurukshetra, Bhishma declared that he would not fight someone who had once been a woman. Arjuna rode behind Śikhaṇḍī and brought Bhishma down with a volley of arrows. Śikhaṇḍī later fought Ashwatthama, Kripa and Kritavarma, and died of wounds after a night attack on the Pandava camp.

Legacy

In the Javanese wayang tradition, Śikhaṇḍī appears as Srikandi, who remains a woman and becomes the second wife of Arjuna, after Sembadra.

Ancient sources: Appears in the Mahabharata, the Sanskrit Hindu epic.

Trivia

A temporary sex exchange. According to the story, the yaksha Stunakarna agreed to swap sexes with Śikhaṇḍī for a period, and Kubera's curse made the change permanent.
Srikandi in Java. In Javanese wayang the character is called Srikandi and stays a woman, playing a role as Arjuna's wife.
