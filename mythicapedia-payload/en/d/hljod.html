jötunn of Norse mythology, wife of Völsung

Hljód

Hljóð (also spelled Hljod or Ljod) is a jötunn, or giantess, in Norse mythology. She is the wife of Völsung and the mother of Sigmund and Signy, central figures in the Völsung saga. Her Old Norse name has been translated as 'howl'.

The myth

According to the Völsunga saga, Hljóð is the daughter of the jötunn Hrímnir and appears as a 'wish-maiden' of the god Odin, a title that could be understood as a Valkyrie in Odin's service.

When Rerir's marriage remains childless, Hljóð takes the form of a crow and gives him an apple of fertility. Thanks to it, Rerir fathers Völsung, who in time becomes Hljóð's own husband and the ancestor of the Völsung line.

Ancient sources: The Völsunga saga, in its first chapter, presents her as the daughter of Hrímnir and a wish-maiden of Odin.

Trivia

A name that sounds like a howl. The name Hljóð has been translated as 'howl'. It appears in Old Norse and is also transcribed as Hljod or Ljod.
