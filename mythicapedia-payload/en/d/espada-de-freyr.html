sword of the Norse god Freyr

Sword of Freyr

The Sword of Freyr is the weapon of the Norse god associated with fertility, harvest and peace. Although the sources mention it only briefly, its story is tied to two of the best-known myths about the god.

The myth

In the Skírnismál, part of the Poetic Edda, Freyr looks down from Odin's throne and falls in love with the giantess Gerðr. To win her favour, he sends his servant Skírnir, and gives him his sword for the journey. By handing over the blade, Freyr is left without it.

According to the tradition, during Ragnarök, the final battle of the world, Freyr faces the giant Surtr without his sword and is killed. Norse sources present this as one of the causes of his fall.

Ancient sources: The Skírnismál in the Poetic Edda, and Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda for Ragnarök.
