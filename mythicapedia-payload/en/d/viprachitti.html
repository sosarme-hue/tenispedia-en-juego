asura and danava in Hindu mythology

Viprachitti

Viprachitti is an asura, or danava, from Hindu mythology, a son of Kashyapa and Danu. He matters as the eldest of the thirty-three powerful asuras born to Danu and as the king of the danavas.

The myth

According to the Vishnu Purana, Danu gave birth to thirty-three powerful asuras, and Viprachitti was the eldest, with Shambara and others as his younger brothers. The Mahabharata tells that after his brother Puloman was killed by Indra, Viprachitti became king of the danavas.

He married Holika, sister of Hiranyakashipu and daughter of Diti. When Vishnu took the form of Vamana to measure the three worlds, Viprachitti and other asuras surrounded him. The Adi Parva of the Mahabharata says that in his next life he was reborn as the demon Jarasandha.

Ancient sources: The Vishnu Purana and the Mahabharata.

Trivia

Brother-in-law of Hiranyakashipu. By marrying Holika, sister of Hiranyakashipu, Viprachitti became the brother-in-law of this powerful demon.
A later life. The Adi Parva of the Mahabharata states that Viprachitti was later reborn as the demon Jarasandha.
