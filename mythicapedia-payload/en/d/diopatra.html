naiad in Greek mythology

Diopatra

Diopatra is a naiad of Mount Othrys, daughter of the river-god Spercheus and the naiad Deino, and one of the Spercheides. Her story, recounted by Cerambus, is one of the Greek tales of Poseidon's pursuit of a nymph.

The myth

According to the tradition recorded by Cerambus, Poseidon fell in love with Diopatra, one of the Spercheides, nymphs of Mount Othrys who were daughters of Spercheus. To get her alone, the god transformed her sisters into poplars and so tried to force himself on the girl.

Other nymphs, outraged by what had happened and by the rumours Poseidon had spread about themselves, punished Cerambus by turning him into a beetle. The story ends with the punishment falling on the narrator rather than on the god.

The name Diopatra means "divine family" and comes from dion and patra.

Ancient sources: Cerambus, an ancient author who recounts the story of Poseidon's love for Diopatra and its consequences.

Trivia

A name with meaning. Her name means "divine family" and is formed from dion and patra.
Sisters turned into poplars. Poseidon transformed Diopatra's sisters into poplars so he could get close to her.
