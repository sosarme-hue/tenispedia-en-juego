Mecon

Mecon was a beautiful young Athenian man, loved by the goddess Demeter, who according to the story was transformed into a poppy. His tale belongs to Greek mythology and explains the goddess's bond with this flower.

The myth

According to tradition, the handsome Mecon became the lover of Demeter, goddess of agriculture. At some point he was transformed into a poppy, a way of preserving him according to the story, though the sources do not detail exactly how the change came about.

The poppy was one of the flowers associated with Demeter, who regarded it as one of her symbols. Mecon's story is thus tied to the world of cultivated fields and the plants the goddess watched over.

Worship and religion

The sources do not describe temples or rites dedicated to Mecon. His role is reflected mainly in the symbolism of the poppy, a flower associated with Demeter in Greek religion.

Trivia

A name that means poppy. Mecon comes from a Greek word meaning "poppy", which explains the direct link between the character and the flower.
Another spelling. The name also appears spelled as Mekon, a spelling variant of the same character.
