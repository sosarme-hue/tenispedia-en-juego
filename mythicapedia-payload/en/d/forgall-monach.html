Emer's father in the Irish Ulster Cycle

Forgall Monach

Forgall Monach is the father of Emer, the woman Cú Chulainn wants to marry in the Irish tale Tochmarc Emire ("The Wooing of Emer"). He belongs to the Ulster Cycle and is the main obstacle in the hero's love story.

The myth

According to the tale, Forgall opposes his daughter's marriage to Cú Chulainn. To get rid of him, he sends the young hero to train with the warrior-woman Scáthach in Alba (Scotland), hoping the ordeal will kill him. Meanwhile, he offers Emer's hand to Lugaid mac Nóis, king of Munster, who backs down once he learns she loves the hero.

On his return, Cú Chulainn storms Forgall's fortress, kills twenty-four of his men and carries Emer away. Forgall dies after falling from the ramparts. Tradition also tells that King Conchobar claimed the right of the first night over the bride, and that the druid Cathbad proposed a solution: he would sleep between the two of them.

Ancient sources: Tochmarc Emire, a tale of the Ulster Cycle preserved in medieval Irish manuscripts such as the Leabhar na hUidre.
