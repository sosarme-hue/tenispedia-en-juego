Nereid of Greek mythology, of truth and sound counsel

Nemertes

Nemertes is a Nereid of Greek mythology, one of the fifty sea-nymph daughters of Nereus and Doris. Her name, often read as "the unerring" or "the truthful", links her to honesty and wise advice.

The myth

According to the tradition, Nemertes was a daughter of Nereus, the "Old Man of the Sea", and of the Oceanid Doris, and she belonged to the company of Nereids who dwelt in the deep waters. Like her father, she was regarded as able to know and speak the truth, a trait she shared with her sister Apseudes.

In the story, Nemertes and her sisters gather around Thetis when she mourns Achilles' grief over the death of his friend Patroclus. It is one of the few moments in which she takes part in the story, joining the collective sorrow of the sea.
