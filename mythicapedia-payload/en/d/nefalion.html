son of Minos in Greek mythology

Nephalion

Nephalion is a figure from Greek mythology, one of the four sons of Minos, associated with the island of Paros. He is remembered for his clash with Heracles, in which he took part in the killing of two sailors from the hero's ship.

The myth

According to the tradition, Nephalion was one of the four sons that Minos had with Paria and lived on the Greek island of Paros. Together with his brothers Eurymedon, Khryses and Philolaus, he rebelled against Heracles.

The key episode begins when one of Heracles' ships was wrecked and washed up on the rocks of Paros. The four brothers killed two of the crew, an act that brought them into conflict with the hero.
