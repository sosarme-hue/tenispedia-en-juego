Japanese god linked to Izumo Province

Omizunu

Omizunu is a Japanese god who appears in the Izumo Fudoki, where he is credited with expanding Izumo Province. He belongs to the lineage of Susanoo, and his figure shows how ancient texts tied the origins of a territory to divine genealogy.

The myth

According to the tradition recorded in the Izumo Fudoki, Omizunu is the son of Fukabuchi-no-Mizuyarehana and Ame-no-Tsudohechine, and a descendant of Susanoo. In the story he is tied to the land of Izumo, since the text credits him with expanding the province, presenting him as a figure linked to the shaping of the territory.

He married Futemimi, and the couple had a son, Ame-no-Fuyukinu. The available sources do not describe further deeds or a specific ending for him, so his story is known mainly through this genealogy and the territorial role attributed to him.

Ancient sources: He appears in the Izumo Fudoki, an ancient chronicle of Izumo Province that records its local traditions.
