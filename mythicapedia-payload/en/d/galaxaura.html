Oceanid of Greek mythology

Galaxaura

Galaxaura is one of the Oceanids of Greek mythology, the water nymphs who were daughters of the Titan Oceanus and Tethys. Her name is linked to calm and to the cool freshness of a shaded stream, and she is counted among the companions of Persephone when Hades abducted her.

The myth

According to Greek tradition, Galaxaura was one of the three thousand Oceanids, daughters of the Titans Oceanus and his sister and wife Tethys. The Oceanids were nymphs associated with the waters, and their great number means they appear more as a group than as characters with stories of their own.

Galaxaura appears in the abduction of Persephone, daughter of Demeter, by Hades. Together with her sisters, she accompanied the young goddess at that moment, so her role in the myth is that of a member of Persephone's retinue. The surviving accounts give her no deeds or adventures of her own.

Trivia

A name with meaning. Her name is rendered as "the calm one" or "the charmer", and has even been compared to the refreshing coolness of a shady stream.
Persephone's companion. Galaxaura was among the companions who accompanied Persephone when Hades carried her off to the underworld.
