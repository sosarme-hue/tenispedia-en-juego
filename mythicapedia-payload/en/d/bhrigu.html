Vedic sage, one of the seven Saptarshis

Bhrigu

Bhrigu is a Hindu rishi, one of the seven Saptarshis and among the Prajapatis created by Brahma. He is regarded as the first writer on predictive astrology in India, through the Bhrigu Samhita, and his lineage gave its name to the Bhargava tradition.

The myth

According to the myth, Bhrigu was born from the mind of Brahma and married Khyati, daughter of Daksha. Their children were Dhata, Vidhata and the goddess Lakshmi. His son Shukra became teacher of the asuras. The most famous story concerns his wife Puloma, who had earlier been promised to the asura Puloman. While Bhrigu was away, the asura carried her off in the form of a boar, and her son Chyavana was born as she fell to the ground. Agni had revealed their whereabouts, so Bhrigu cursed him to devour everything he came across. Brahma turned the curse into a blessing, since offerings would now reach the gods through the fire.

In another tale, sages ask Bhrigu to decide which member of the Trimurti is supreme. He tests them: Brahma ignores him, Shiva nearly strikes him down, and Vishnu receives a kick on the chest, yet remains calm and massages the sage's foot. Bhrigu then declares Vishnu the greatest.

Worship and religion

His hermitage stood by the Vadhusar River near Dhosi Hill, and he is worshipped at places such as Bharuch, Tirumala and Swamimalai. Khedbrahma in Gujarat recalls the test of the Trimurti.

Legacy

His name lives on in the Bhargava tradition and in Indian astrology. In traditional astronomy his son Shukra is identified with the planet Venus. The root of his name, meaning to shine, is related to words such as Latin fulgere and Greek phlegō.

Ancient sources: The Rig Veda, the Mahabharata, the Bhagavata Purana, the Vayu Purana and the Taittiriya Upanishad mention him.

Trivia

Astrologer of whole lives. He is credited with the Bhrigu Samhita, considered one of the first books of astrology, with predictions covering a whole life.
Lakshmi, daughter of Bhrigu. In some traditions Lakshmi is raised by Bhrigu and Khyati, which is why she is called Bhargavi, daughter of Bhrigu.
