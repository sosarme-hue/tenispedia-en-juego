evil fairy of Slavic mythology

Blud

Blud is one of the Slavic fairies of Slavic mythology, an evil deity that confuses those under its influence and leads them aimlessly around and round. The name also came to mean illicit fornication, which shows that the word carried a moral sense as well as a magical one.

The myth

In Slavic lore, the blud is counted among the fairies, although it is described as an evil deity. Its defining trait is disorientation: whoever falls under its influence loses their sense of direction and ends up wandering aimlessly, going round and round.

The word also carries a moral meaning. It was used for illicit fornication, and some Slavic clerics claimed that the desire for such unions came from the Devil. In Russian, blud still means debauchery, adultery and deviation from the straight path, both literally and figuratively.
