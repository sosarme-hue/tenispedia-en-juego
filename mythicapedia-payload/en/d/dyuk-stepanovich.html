Russian epic hero of the byliny

Duke Stepanovich

Duke Stepanovich is a character in the Russian byliny, the popular epic poems of Rus'. He is a young bogatyr, or epic knight, whose wealth and boldness bring him into conflict with Prince Vladimir of Kiev.

The myth

The story follows the young bogatyr as he crosses a treacherous mountain pass on his way to Kiev, capital of ancient Rus'. There hungry birds swoop down to peck at him, and a dragon breathes fire at his horse's hooves, yet he rides on.

When he reaches the city and meets Prince Vladimir, he finds it poor and tells the prince how rich his homeland is. The versions disagree about where that homeland lies, naming India, Volyn, Korela or Galich.

At the urging of Churilo Plyonkovich, a rash young knight, Vladimir imprisons Stepanovich and sends scouts to check his claims. They return confirming the wealth and report that even selling all of Kiev's treasures would not buy enough paper to inventory it. Churila then sets Stepanovich a series of tests, but the Duke prevails and Churila is left chastened.

Trivia

A disputed homeland. Different versions of the bylina place the duke's homeland in different places, such as India, Volyn, Korela or Galich.
Uncountable wealth. According to the story, selling all of Kiev's treasures would not even buy enough paper to list the wealth of his homeland.
