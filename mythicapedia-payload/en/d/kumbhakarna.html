giant rakshasa, brother of Ravana in the Ramayana

Kumbhakarna

Kumbhakarna is a gigantic rakshasa from Hindu mythology, the younger brother of the demon king Ravana and famous for his enormous appetite and his near-perpetual sleep. He appears in the Ramayana as a powerful warrior who, despite his demonic nature, is described in some texts as loyal and virtuous.

The myth

According to the story, Kumbhakarna was a being of enormous size who, after severe austerities, asked Brahma for Indra's throne. The goddess Saraswati, at Indra's request, tied his tongue, so that instead of Indrasana (the throne of Indra) he asked for Nidrasana (a bed for sleeping). Brahma granted him endless sleep. Ravana persuaded Brahma to soften the boon into six months of sleep at a time, after which he would wake to eat without limit, including humans.

In the war for Lanka, Kumbhakarna woke after being trampled by elephants, devoured monkeys, wounded the mighty Hanuman and captured the monkey king Sugriva. He tried to persuade Ravana that Rama was an avatar of Vishnu, but out of loyalty to his brother he fought on until Rama felled him with the brahmastra. The Bhagavata Purana says that he and Ravana were reincarnations of two gatekeepers of Vaikuntha who had been cursed by the Kumaras.

Legacy

He appears in the popular Indian television series Ramayan, directed by Ramanand Sagar and broadcast between 1987 and 1988, where he was played by the actor Nalin Dave. He also appears in the MOBA video game Smite.

Ancient sources: The Ramayana and the Padma Purana, which recounts his death in the chapter «Kumbhakarna vadha»; the Bhagavata Purana, which links him to the curse of the gatekeepers of Vaikuntha.

Trivia

His name. Kumbhakarna means «pot-eared», from kumbha (pot) and karna (ear).
A hit series. In its original broadcast, the television series Ramayan was reportedly watched by around 100 million viewers.
