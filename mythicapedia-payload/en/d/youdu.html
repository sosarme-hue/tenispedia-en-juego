capital of the underworld in Chinese mythology

Youdu

Youdu, whose name means “Dark Capital”, is the capital of Hell, or Diyu, in Chinese mythology. It is imagined as a typical Chinese capital city, similar to Chang'an, but shrouded in darkness.

The myth

According to tradition, Youdu lies beneath the earth, in the Region of Darkness, also known as Diyu or the Yellow Springs. Its general ruler is Houtu, but the more important officials live in the capital, which serves as the seat of government.

The sovereign deity is usually Yanluo Wang, assisted by ten judge-kings. The city has walls, palaces, a hall of justice and residences for ghosts. Its official records are kept there, and they help the judges decide punishments or, occasionally, return to life someone who died before their time. Creatures include Tu Bo, the warden of the city, and Diting, the steed of Di Zang, which can tell truth from falsehood.

Legacy

Youdu appears in many artworks and in literary works derived from these myths. In the novel Journey to the West, Sun Wukong visits it repeatedly.

Trivia

Well lit. Despite being underground, Youdu may be the best-lit area of Diyu, with torches and candles.
A famous visitor. Sun Wukong, the protagonist of Journey to the West, visits Youdu several times in the novel.
