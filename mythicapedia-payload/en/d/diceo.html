Greek hero, son of Poseidon, model of goodness

Dicaeus

Dicaeus is a hero of Greek mythology, a son of Poseidon remembered as the model of the just and hospitable man. His name, Dikaios, means "just", and the tradition presents him as the very embodiment of goodness.

The myth

According to the tradition, Dicaeus was one of the sons of Poseidon and the brother of Syleus. The two lived near Mount Pelion in Thessaly, on the edge of the region, where their quiet life stood apart from the great heroic deeds.

The stories draw a sharp contrast between the brothers. Syleus was killed by Heracles, while Dicaeus was regarded as a truly just man, a quality his very name suggests. On one occasion Heracles was his guest, and the episode highlights the hospitality and uprightness of his host.

Legacy

A city in Thrace called Dicaea was named after him, according to the tradition.

Trivia

A name that describes his character. His Greek name, Δίκαιος, means "just", which fits the image the texts give of him.
A city bearing his name. The Thracian city of Dicaea was named after him, according to the tradition that records it.
