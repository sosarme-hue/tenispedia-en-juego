Roman god of luck

Sors

Sors is a minor deity of Roman mythology, the god of luck. His figure follows the Greek model of Tyche, and he is said to have been a son of Fortuna.

The myth

Accounts of Sors are scarce. According to the tradition attributed to him, he was a son of Fortuna, the Roman goddess of luck, and his character draws on Tyche, the Greek goddess of chance and fortune.

He barely appears in mythological texts, so his story amounts to a few references and his role as a god of luck.

Legacy

His name survives in sayings such as “by the luck of Sors” or “lived by a hair’s breadth of Sors”. It is also a popular name in poker and a common gambling term. Opera singer Joe Volpe composed a piece that mentions him.

Trivia

Poker name. Sors is a popular name in poker, as are the sayings that mention him.
Opera piece. Opera singer Joe Volpe composed a piece that mentions Sors, with lines about his eternal existence and his followers.
