Japanese god of victory and speed

Amenooshihomimi

Amenooshihomimi is a deity in Japanese mythology, the firstborn son of Amaterasu and regarded as an ancestor of the Japanese imperial family. His name can be rendered as 'ruler of the heavenly rice ears'.

The myth

According to the tradition, he was born from a contest of kami creation between Amaterasu and Susanoo. In many versions, Susanoo chewed Amaterasu's beads and from them came five male deities, the first of whom to be born was Amenooshihomimi.

When the question of who should rule the earth arose, he was the first to be proposed, but he declined. He later fell in love with Takuhadachijihime, and fathered Ninigi-no-Mikoto. In some versions, Amaterasu gave him a bronze mirror called Yata no Kagami.

Worship and religion

He is worshipped at the Nogami-jinja shrine, dedicated to him and his wife, and also at the Hikosan Jingū shrine.

Trivia

A long name. Other sources give him the full name Masakatsu-akatsukachi-hayahi-ame-no-oshihomimi, roughly 'truly I have won with rushing might, ruling the grand rice ears of heaven'.
Bronze mirror. In some versions, Amaterasu gave him the Yata no Kagami mirror, an object associated with the divine line of the imperial family.
