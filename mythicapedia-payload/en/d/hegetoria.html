nymph of Rhodes, wife of Ochimus

Hegetoria

Hegetoria is a local nymph of Rhodes, wife of Ochimus and mother of Cydippe. Her story is tied to the succession to the island's throne and to one of the abduction tales in Rhodian tradition.

The myth

According to the tradition, Hegetoria married Ochimus, and the couple had a daughter, Cydippe, who was later also called Cyrbia. Cydippe married Cercaphus, brother of Ochimus, who succeeded his brother on the throne.

Another version holds that Ochimus had promised his daughter to Ocridion, but Cercaphus fell in love with her and abducted her. He did not return until Ochimus was already an old man.

The ancient accounts do not agree on every detail, and the abduction version survives as an alternative to the arranged marriage.

Trivia

Two versions. The better-known account of Cydippe's marriage to Cercaphus is also told in an alternate version in which she is abducted.
Another name. Cydippe also appears as Cyrbia in some versions of the story.
