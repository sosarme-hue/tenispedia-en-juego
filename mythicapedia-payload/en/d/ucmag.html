heaven or paradise in Turkic mythology and Tengrism

Uçmag

Uçmag is the heaven of Turkic mythology, where the souls of the righteous go after death. It is the opposite of Tamag, and its name is usually linked to the idea of flight.

The myth

According to Turkic tradition, the souls of righteous people dwell in Uçmag after death. They often reach it by flying, either taking the form of a bird or carried by a spirit sent by Ülgen.

In Tengrism, Uçmag resembles the earth but is untouched by humans, with a pristine nature and far more light. Its inhabitants have never strayed from the traditions of their ancestors. Shamans can visit this place during their rituals.
